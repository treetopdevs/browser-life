This is the same single setting as the first rung (`LARGE`, two genes, 16 sites, `mutPar 1e-4`, 64 x 64,
200,000 steps) on eight more seeds. It says the first rung's gate result at that setting was not a four-seed
accident. It says nothing new about other gene counts, parasite rates, the parts of the `LARGE` bundle, or
arena size; those are Part B of the plan and have not been run.

## What ran

`LARGE`, two genes, four arms, seeds 9 to 16, 200,000 steps, 64 x 64: 32 runs, as planned.

```bash
deno run -A --no-lock experiments/dropgenes/sweep.ts --dir runs/dropgenes/firmup/a-gate --genes 2 --caps 16 \
  --seeds 9,10,11,12,13,14,15,16 --steps 200000 --mutPar 1e-4 --jobs 4 --tag large \
  --ov '{"diffT":0.1,"diffW":0.03,"diffF":0.6,"diffA":0.44}'
deno run -A --no-lock experiments/dropgenes/summarize.ts runs/dropgenes/firmup/a-gate --per-seed   # FIRMUP-TABLES.md
deno run -A --no-lock experiments/dropgenes/summarize.ts runs/dropgenes/firmup/a-gate --json       # last line: firmup-summary.json
```

The sweep was invoked twice into the same directory: first with `--arms GATED_PAR --seeds 9,10,11,12` (4
runs), then the full command above, which skipped those four and ran the other 28. Neither invocation was
interrupted.

The seeds 5 to 8 figures quoted below are the first rung's files, summarised with the same script:

```bash
deno run -A --no-lock experiments/dropgenes/summarize.ts <first-rung worktree>/runs/dropgenes/confirm-v3 --per-seed --json
```

(`<first-rung worktree>` is PLAN.md's `../browser-life-dropgenes`, beside the main checkout; rows
`large | 2 | 16`; the tables this prints that TABLES.md already has are byte-identical to TABLES.md).

A few figures below are not printed by the summariser: the same-seed ratios and differences, the share of
daughters read at 500 steps, and the speeds. They come from the run files' summaries by a short script kept
with the raw runs (not in git), run on both directories:

```bash
python3 -I runs/dropgenes/firmup/derived.py runs/dropgenes/firmup/a-gate
python3 -I runs/dropgenes/firmup/derived.py <first-rung worktree>/runs/dropgenes/confirm-v3
```

It computes: late compartments of `GATED_PAR` over `GATED_CLEAN` on the same seed; the gene-0 parent
correlation of `GATED_CLEAN` minus `UNGATED_CLEAN` on the same seed; read / (read + gone + fused) per seed from
`heredityGene0Delayed`; and the range and median of `stepsPerSecond` and `seconds`.

## The command was proved first

Both checks were made before any seed 9 to 16 run.

1. **Configuration.** The 16 recorded `LARGE` two-gene files of seeds 5 to 8 were copied to a scratch
   directory and the Part A command was pointed at it with `--seeds 5,6,7,8`. `sweep.ts` compares the whole
   effective configuration, the protocol and the step count of an existing file with the request and refuses
   a file that differs. It reported "0 runs to do": all 16 match.
2. **Bit for bit.** `GATED_PAR` seed 5 was run again:

   ```bash
   deno run -A --no-lock experiments/dropgenes/sweep.ts --dir runs/dropgenes/firmup/repro --genes 2 --caps 16 \
     --seeds 5 --steps 200000 --mutPar 1e-4 --jobs 1 --arms GATED_PAR --tag large \
     --ov '{"diffT":0.1,"diffW":0.03,"diffF":0.6,"diffA":0.44}'
   ```

   Against `confirm-v3/large-K2-cap16-GATED_PAR-s5.json`: all 32 summary fields other than the two wall-clock
   fields (`seconds`, `stepsPerSecond`) are equal, including the configuration, the protocol, every tracker
   count and all four heredity records; all 100 time-series rows are equal, the first and the last among
   them (`python3 -I runs/dropgenes/firmup/compare_repro.py <recorded file> <rerun file>`, also kept with the
   raw runs). The rerun was started twice: the first start was stopped after a few minutes, before it wrote
   anything, because it had been launched with a 30-minute limit; the file compared is from the second start.

Readings:

- **The heredity figures repeat as well.** In the three arms that last, no parent correlation, control or
  margin on seeds 9 to 16 lies more than 0.02 (to two decimals) outside its seeds 5 to 8 range, and the margin
  is at least 0.2 on every seed (smallest: 0.41 gated with the parasite, 0.44 gated without, 0.32 ungated
  without).
- **The ungated world without a parasite passes the same margin on 8 of 8**, as on seeds 5 to 8. So here too
  the gate is not what makes compartments resemble their parents. On the same seed the gated clean parent
  correlation is 0.09 to 0.13 above the ungated clean one (0.10 to 0.13 on seeds 5 to 8).
- **The ungated parasite arm's heredity figures are estimates from short runs.** Those runs ended by step
  4,450 with 39 to 92 daughters each, all of them daughters that lasted 500 steps in a world about to end;
  the correlations range from 0.13 to 0.47 for the parent and -0.13 to 0.19 for the neighbour. The "6 of 8"
  there is given for completeness and is not read as a result.
- **Conditional on surviving, as before.** In the gated parasite arm 51 to 52% of daughters were read at 500
  steps (the rest were gone or merged); 65 to 66% gated without the parasite and 65 to 67% ungated without.
  Totals over the eight seeds, read / gone / fused / pending: 79,000 / 71,990 / 3,289 / 392 (gated,
  parasite), 82,410 / 41,994 / 2,394 / 272 (gated, clean), 78,545 / 38,711 / 2,157 / 276 (ungated, clean).
- **Parasite load at 500 steps** in the gated parasite arm: parent 0.06 to 0.10, nearest neighbour 0.01 to
  0.03 (seeds 5 to 8: 0.09 to 0.11 and -0.01 to 0.03). RESULTS.md's reading of that low figure (loaded
  daughters do not last 500 steps) came from an exploratory check and was not re-tested here.
- The trait is the same neutral mix as in the first rung. Nothing here shows a difference selection could act
  on. Evolution was not tested: the number of genes is a setting of the run, the two genes are interchangeable
  and the parasite is one fixed type.

## Speed and time

- 24 runs reached step 200,000, four running at a time on a machine shared with other lines: 322 to 461 steps
  a second each (median 350), 434 to 621 s per run. The 8 ungated parasite runs took 5 to 10 s each.
- The 32 runs took 56.7 minutes of wall time in two invocations (20:54:55 to 21:04:53 and 21:05:12 to
  21:51:55 on 2026-11-21), 13,511 s summed over runs.
- The seed 5 rerun, alone and earlier under heavier load, ran at 112 steps a second (1,789 s); the first
  rung recorded 285 steps a second for the same run. Speed on this machine moved by a factor of three with
  load during this work.

## Limits

- One setting. Everything in RESULTS.md's limits still applies: one arena size, one horizon, a parasite rate
  that is the top of its calibration grid, and `LARGE` as an unseparated bundle run at one site count.
- Twelve seeds at this setting now agree, eight of them unseen when the rule was written. The spread between
  seeds is small (gated parasite compartments 35.5 to 37.6). The next runs go to Part B's open questions
  and not to more seeds at this setting; that is a choice of where to look, not a finding.
- Being held at 200,000 steps is not a proof of stability beyond it.
- Heredity is conditional on the daughter surviving 500 steps, the trait is neutral, and the nearest neighbour
  is often a relative (both controls are shown).
- Fission, fusion, death, age and turnover figures depend on the 50-step census.
- CPU reference only.

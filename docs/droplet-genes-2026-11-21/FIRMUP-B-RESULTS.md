Readings:

- **Three genes are held with the parasite on three of four seeds, and with much less room than two.** The
  three runs that held have late means of 16.4 to 22.1 compartments (26, 20 and 25 at step 200,000 itself),
  against late means of 35.5 to 37.6 for two genes on the same seeds and against a bar of 10. On the same
  seed the gated parasite world has 0.50 to 0.68 as many late
  compartments as the gated clean one (two genes: 0.72 to 0.77). The compartment count is not steady: a
  report row is one census every 2,000 steps, and from step 10,000 on the lowest row is 6, 5 and 10
  compartments in the three runs that held (two genes: 21 to 26). The fourth run, seed 11, had 12 to 24
  compartments on every report row to step 44,000, 7 at step 46,000, no compartment at the census of step
  47,700, and the run ended with no wall left at step 47,800.
- **The label rests on the smallest count the rule allows.** 3 of 4 is "held" by the plan's rule and is
  reported as such. With one of four runs lost at about a quarter of the horizon and the other three dipping
  to 5 to 10 compartments, these runs do not show that a three-gene world under this parasite lasts
  indefinitely; they show three of four still there at step 200,000.
- **Without the parasite three genes are held with room to spare**, gated or not: 30.9 to 32.6 late
  compartments on 8 of 8 runs, lowest report row 21 or more. So the lost run is not three genes failing on
  their own.
- **The ungated twin is lost on every seed**, at step 4,400 to 7,750 (two genes: 2,200 to 4,450), and the
  ungated world without the parasite is held on 4 of 4, so the comparison is not confounded with dilution.
- **Beside the recorded outcomes at other gene counts** (each on its own seeds, `GATED_PAR` at `LARGE`, 16
  sites, `mutPar 1e-4`): two genes held on 12 of 12 (seeds 5 to 16); three genes on 3 of 4 (seeds 9 to 12,
  here); four genes on 0 of 4 (seeds 5 to 8, ended at step 3,750 to 7,850). Without the parasite, gated: two
  genes 12 of 12 with 48.3 to 50.7 late compartments, three genes 4 of 4 with 32.0 to 32.6, four genes 4 of 4
  with 11.9 to 13.6. As the plan says, this is not turned into a single "capacity": the three counts were run
  on different seeds, and a count that fails does not show that every larger count fails.

Limits of B1:

- Four seeds, one setting, one horizon. "Held" on 3 of 4 is the weakest count that earns the label.

  recovers each time or is eventually lost, as seed 11 was, is not something 200,000 steps on three runs can

Readings:

- **At a tenth of the dose the gate is still needed.** With `mutPar 1e-5` the ungated world ended on 4 of 4
  seeds, later than at `1e-4` (step 14,850 to 39,350 against 2,200 to 4,450), and the gated world held on 4

Limits of B2:

- Three doses a factor of ten apart, four seeds each, one physics (`LARGE`), two genes, 64 x 64. The change
  from "needed" to "not needed" lies somewhere between `1e-5` and `1e-6` and was not located.
- "Not needed" means the ungated world was held at step 200,000 on 4 of 4 by the plan's rule. It does not

  dose is that trial probability, not a count of events and not a parasite load; the loads above are
  outcomes.
- No clean arm was run in B2. The no-parasite figures are Part A's on the same seeds and physics.

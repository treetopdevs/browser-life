# Droplet genes: results of the first rung

**Date:** 2026-11-21. **Plan and pass rules:** [PLAN.md](./PLAN.md) (rules and Amendment 1 were fixed before the
confirmation runs). **Full tables:** [TABLES.md](./TABLES.md), generated by `experiments/dropgenes/summarize.ts`;
per-seed figures in [confirm-summary.json](./confirm-summary.json).

## Outcome against the decision rules

- **Stop rule: not fired, but only through the `LARGE` physics.** At the three site counts on the plan's size
  axis (default physics), two genes with the parasite were held on 0, 1 and 0 of 4 seeds. With all three
  diffusion rates doubled (`LARGE`) they were held on 4 of 4.
- **Next rung reached** at `LARGE`, 16 sites per tile. Largest K held there, on the ladder 2, 4, 8, 16: gated
  with parasite **2**, gated without **4**, ungated without **4**, ungated with parasite **none**. Two is the
  bottom of the ladder, so for the parasite arm it is a floor that was met, not a measured capacity.
- **The twin counts** at that size: without the parasite the ungated world is held, with it the ungated world
  is not and the gated one is.
- **Heredity counts** in the gated arms at that size with two genes, by the rule as amended. The ungated world
  without a parasite passes the same rule, so the gate is not what makes compartments resemble their parents.

All of this is one arena size (64 x 64), four seeds per cell, 200,000 steps, and one parasite rate that was
the top of its calibration grid. It is a first rung, not a general result. Nothing evolved in these runs.

## What ran

Seeds 5 to 8 (never used in calibration), 200,000 steps, 64 x 64, census every 50 steps, frozen settings
`diffT 0.1`, `mutPar 1e-4` in the parasite arms, the rest `defaultDropGenesConfig()`. `LARGE` adds `diffW 0.03`,
`diffF 0.6`, `diffA 0.44`. 112 runs; conservation residual 0 in every one.

```bash
deno run -A --no-lock experiments/dropgenes/sweep.ts --dir runs/dropgenes/confirm-v3 --genes 2,4,8,16 --caps 16 \
  --seeds 5,6,7,8 --steps 200000 --mutPar 1e-4 --jobs 8 --tag large --ov '{"diffT":0.1,"diffW":0.03,"diffF":0.6,"diffA":0.44}'
deno run -A --no-lock experiments/dropgenes/sweep.ts --dir runs/dropgenes/confirm-v3 --genes 2 --caps 8,16,32 \
  --seeds 5,6,7,8 --steps 200000 --mutPar 1e-4 --jobs 8 --ov '{"diffT":0.1}'
deno run -A --no-lock experiments/dropgenes/summarize.ts runs/dropgenes/confirm-v3
```

The confirmation cells were run three times as the observer was corrected after two code reviews (see "What
changed on the way"): the K = 2 cells in `runs/dropgenes/confirm`, all cells in `confirm-v2`, all cells again
in `confirm-v3`. The kernel's dynamics did not change between them. All 64 runs of the first sweep and all 112 of the second agree exactly with
the third on held, late compartments, complete share, steps run, every tracker count and the time series. The tables here are from
`confirm-v3`; only the heredity figures differ between the sweeps.

Readings:

- **At this parasite rate, the gate decides.** At `LARGE` with two genes the gated world is still there at
  200,000 steps on 4 of 4 seeds, with a late mean of about 36 compartments and the parasite at 11 to 16% of
  all copies. The ungated world ended at step 2,500 to 5,100. Without the parasite both are held, so at this
  size the ungated world is not dying of dilution.
- **The gated world holds by turnover, not by keeping the parasite out.** About 9,600 fissions and 9,200
  deaths per run (tracker counts), 36 compartments against 50 without the parasite. The parasite is always
  there.
- **Small compartments lose two genes to this parasite.** At the default physics the gated parasite arm held
  on 0, 1 and 0 of 4 seeds at 8, 16 and 32 sites; runs ended between step 6,850 and 106,200. Without the
  parasite the gated compartments held two genes on 4 of 4 at every site count. Ungated without the parasite
  held at 16 and 32 sites and failed at 8 (0 of 4, ended by step 34,400), so at 8 sites ungating alone is
  enough to lose the world.
- **More sites did not help the small compartments** (32 sites was no better than 16). The one setting that
  helped was `LARGE`, which was run at one site count and changes several things at once: all three diffusion
  rates, compartment area, lifetime, and how many compartments fit. Which of these matters was not separated.
- **Four genes are held without the parasite and not with it** (the gated parasite runs ended at step 3,750
  to 7,850). Without the
  parasite four genes clear the bar of 10 compartments only narrowly (10.5 to 13.6), in an arena that carries
  about 12 compartments at that setting.
- **Eight and sixteen genes die at the founders**, in every arm: every run has ended by step 400 (eight genes
  at 250 to 400, sixteen at 200 to 250), with no fission at eight genes and one fission in two of the
  sixteen-gene runs. Sixteen sites shared among eight genes leave about two sites per gene on a full tile, and
  wall is made at full rate only with two copies of every gene in the 3 x 3 patch, so a shortage of sites is
  the likely cause under this seeding and these rates; it was not isolated. Those rungs say nothing about the
  arms.
- **The ladder is coarse.** K = 3, 5, 6 and 7 were not run, so "largest K held" means largest on the ladder.

## Heredity

Trait: the share of gene 0 among a compartment's essential copies. Daughter read 500 steps after the split
(about ten template lifetimes), against the parent before the split, against the parent's nearest neighbour,
and against the nearest compartment that is not a close relative. Correlations, range over four seeds:

| setting, K | arm | parent | nearest neighbour | unrelated neighbour | margin at least 0.2 | copies per compartment |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `LARGE` 16 sites, 2 | gated, parasite | 0.48 to 0.49 | 0.05 to 0.06 | 0.00 to 0.01 | 4 of 4 | 93 to 97 |
| `LARGE` 16 sites, 2 | gated, no parasite | 0.47 | 0.01 to 0.02 | -0.01 to 0.02 | 4 of 4 | 78 to 83 |
| `LARGE` 16 sites, 2 | ungated, no parasite | 0.35 to 0.38 | 0.00 to 0.01 | -0.01 to 0.02 | 4 of 4 | 71 to 74 |
| `LARGE` 16 sites, 4 | gated, no parasite | 0.16 to 0.18 | 0.00 to 0.02 | -0.01 to 0.01 | 0 of 4 | 120 to 138 |
| `LARGE` 16 sites, 4 | ungated, no parasite | 0.08 to 0.11 | -0.01 to 0.03 | -0.02 to 0.02 | 0 of 4 | 114 to 126 |
| default 8 sites, 2 | gated, no parasite | 0.01 to 0.03 | 0.00 to 0.02 | 0.00 to 0.03 | 0 of 4 | 29 to 30 |
| default 16 sites, 2 | gated, no parasite | 0.28 to 0.29 | 0.00 to 0.01 | -0.01 to 0.02 | 4 of 4 | 54 to 56 |
| default 16 sites, 2 | ungated, no parasite | 0.16 to 0.18 | 0.01 to 0.03 | 0.01 to 0.02 | 0 of 4 | 44 to 46 |
| default 32 sites, 2 | gated, no parasite | 0.69 to 0.70 | 0.11 to 0.13 | 0.00 to 0.03 | 4 of 4 | 109 to 112 |
| default 32 sites, 2 | ungated, no parasite | 0.62 to 0.64 | 0.06 to 0.08 | 0.03 to 0.05 | 4 of 4 | 72 to 76 |

The parent figure is nearly unchanged (within 0.01) whether it is taken over the daughters with a
nearest-neighbour control or over those with an unrelated control; TABLES.md shows both with their counts.

Readings:

- **Among daughters that still exist 500 steps after the split, the gene mix tracks the parent's and not a
  neighbour's.** In the cells tabled above that is 34 to 66% of daughters, depending on the setting; the rest are gone or merged by then, so the figure
  is conditional on surviving.
- **The trait is a neutral mix.** The two genes do the same job, so this shows a drifting quantity persisting
  along a lineage for about ten template lifetimes. It does not show a difference that selection could act on.
- **Among gated two-gene runs, resemblance rises with copies per compartment**: near zero at about 30 copies
  (8 sites), about 0.3 at 55, 0.5 at 80 to 95, 0.7 at 110. Across other settings it does not line up on copy
  number alone (four genes hold 120 to 138 copies at 0.15 to 0.18, and the share of one gene among four is a
  different trait). A plausible reading, not tested, is that small numbers let the mix drift away faster.
- **The gate adds about 0.06 to 0.11** to the parent correlation, comparing the seed ranges of the three
  no-parasite twin pairs where both are held (single seeds differ by 0.05 to 0.13). Ungated compartments resemble
  their parents almost as well. That would fit copies that leave an ungated compartment mostly decaying before
  they reach another one, but where copies came from was not measured.
- **Parasite load: the delayed figure is low because loaded daughters are gone.** In the gated parasite arm at
  `LARGE` the 500-step correlation is 0.09 to 0.11 for the parent and about 0 for a neighbour. A reviewer's
  exploratory check (seeds 1 to 4, 20,000 steps) found parasite share passed on at about 0.85 at the 50-step
  read, about half of daughters gone by 500 steps, and the ones that died were the heavily loaded ones. So the
  low figure says loaded lineages do not last 500 steps, not that load is not inherited.

Limits of the heredity readout: it follows a daughter by tracker id, which stays with the fragment that
overlaps it most when a compartment splits again; daughters that merged with another compartment at or after
the split are left out (about 2% of daughters at `LARGE` with two genes, 8 to 10% with four, 9 to 13% at the default physics); between a third and just over a half of daughters no longer exist at 500 steps and are
not in the correlation. The nearest neighbour is often a relative, which is why the unrelated control was added
after review; both are reported and the pre-stated rule uses the nearest neighbour.

## What this rung does and does not show

With two interchangeable genes in `LARGE` compartments, the population of compartments keeps both genes for
200,000 steps while a fixed-type parasite sits at 11 to 16% of copies. The reading that fits is that loaded
compartments die and clean ones divide (heavy turnover in the tracker counts, and the exploratory check that
the daughters lost within 500 steps are the loaded ones); that mechanism was not measured directly. The gate
is what lets it work at this parasite rate. With four genes the parasite arm was lost on 4
of 4 seeds.

It does not show evolution. The number of genes is a setting of the run, the genes are interchangeable, the
parasite is one fixed type, and the trait whose inheritance was measured is neutral. Whether compartments like
these could carry a genome that grows is the question for the next rung, not a result of this one.

## Exploratory, not confirmation (calibration seeds 1 to 4, short runs)

- **Lower parasite rates** (a reviewer's check; `LARGE`, two genes, 20,000 steps): ungated at `mutPar 1e-5`
  ended on 2 of 4 seeds with the parasite still rising on the others; ungated at `1e-6` all four alive with
  the parasite share up to 0.19; gated at both rates kept 46 to 52 compartments with the parasite at 0 to 0.04.
  So the gap between gated and ungated looks graded in the rate.
- **Eight genes with more sites** (`LARGE`, seeds 1 and 2, files in `runs/dropgenes/explore`): at 32 sites the runs ended at steps
  1,400 and 2,100 after 10 and 22 fissions; at 64 sites at steps 23,900 and 24,200 after 269 and 231 fissions.
  More sites carry eight genes for longer, and not for 30,000 steps.
- **Founder seeding** (a reviewer's check): eight genes at 16 sites died by step 300 whether founders carried
  2 or 6 copies per gene.

## What changed on the way

1. **The Phase B2 defaults do not make compartments in a one-wall world** (travelling rings; see PLAN.md). The
   sandbox uses a spot regime of the same chemistry. With templates off the sandbox kernel equals the B2
   reference bit for bit (tested on three settings; an auditor checked 112 random ones).
2. **Audit** (three reviewers, one worker): no blocker. Fixed: the parasite's copy rate is tied to twice the
   gene rate; parasite origin picks among genes present on the tile; the stranded readout is split into bare
   tiles and the soft rim; tests were added for each rule.
3. **Heredity was re-defined before the confirmation runs** (Amendment 1): at a 50-step read the correlation
   was 0.7 or more even in an ungated world.
4. **Codex review (Sol 6.1, High), first pass, no P1.** Applied after the first K = 2 sweep: both heredity
   correlations use the same daughters; daughters that merged during the delay are excluded; the
   unrelated-neighbour control; the summariser takes the largest K held, reads "late" from the planned window
   and refuses mixed settings; the runner refuses overrides of seed, size or arm and never leaves a failed run
   under a finished name; kernel bounds tightened where a signed product could wrap.
5. **Codex second pass, one P1 on the heredity readout:** a daughter that merged in the same census as its
   split was still counted. Fixed, with a wall bound enforced in `step()`, a full-configuration check when a
   sweep resumes, and the set-up protocol recorded in each run. All 112 runs were made again. A closing
   pass found no P1; its one P2 (the wall bound was checked before synthesis, not after) was fixed without
   changing what a run computes (checked against a confirmation run's first 4,000 steps).
6. **Independent check of this write-up** (two agents): one rebuilt every table from the raw files with its
   own script and agreed on all 112 held verdicts and the four rule outcomes; the other attacked the readings,
   and the narrower wording above is the result.

## Calibration record (seeds 1 to 4; none of this is evidence)

- Six rounds on wall physics: defaults kept. `LARGE` chosen as all diffusion doubled. With templates off its
  compartments are 2.7 times the default area (77 on 64 x 64, 4 of 4 seeds); in the confirmation runs with
  templates on the difference is smaller (late mean area about 19 to 22 tiles against about 12).
- Six rounds on template rates: only `diffT` changed, 0.2 to 0.1 (fewer copies on the rim, fewer merges at 32
  sites). The scout noted the defaults also met every target.
- One round for `mutPar`, by the stated rule: held 4 of 4 at every grid value with K = 1, so the top value was
  taken.
- An independent re-run of the proposal: held 4 of 4 in all six K = 1 cells, residual 0.
- PLAN.md's "about a third of all copies sit on the soft rim" was a calibration reading of the rim plus bare
  tiles; in the confirmation runs the rim alone holds 14 to 25%.

## Correction to PLAN.md Amendment 1 (2026-11-21, written after the confirmation runs)

Amendment 1 says that before the confirmation runs "no `GATED_PAR` run with K = 2 and no ungated run with a
parasite was made". That is wrong. The workflow transcripts show these, all on seeds 1 or 3, default physics,
none longer than 10,000 steps:

- the fix worker: gated, K = 2, `mutPar 1e-4` and `1e-3`, 3,000 steps (smoke runs of the changed parasite code);
- the observer auditor: gated, K = 2, `mutPar 1e-6`, seed 1, 10,000 steps, at four census intervals;
- the science auditor: gated **and ungated**, K = 2, `mutPar 1e-5`, seed 3, 9,000 steps, and instrumented gated
  runs with K = 2 to 8 at `mutPar 1e-3` for 1,500 steps;
- the arithmetic auditor: conservation stress tests on tiny grids and extreme settings with both gates and
  `mutPar` up to 1, a few hundred steps each.

None used seeds 5 to 8, none was passed to the scouts, and no setting was chosen from them: `mutPar` came from
the stated rule on K = 1, and `diffT` from gated runs without a parasite. The rule that scouts would not run
those arms held; the auditors and the worker were not under it and the amendment should have listed what they
ran. The outcome is unchanged; the record is corrected here and the amendment is left as written.

## Limits

- One arena size, four seeds, one horizon. The gated parasite arm at default 16 sites held on one seed and
  ended at steps 13,050, 64,250 and 106,200 on the others, so being alive at 200,000 steps does not make a
  cell stable.
- The parasite rate is a grid edge, not a measured threshold, and the gate comparison was confirmed at that
  one rate.
- `LARGE` escaped the stop rule and is a bundle of changes run at one site count.
- Parasite origin is per tile, so pressure per copy differs between site counts.
- Fission, fusion and death counts depend on the 50-step census; they are bookkeeping. Between 14 and 25% of
  the copies sit on the soft rim outside what the observer calls a compartment.
- "Complete" needs one copy of each gene somewhere in the compartment; the kernel needs copies in every 3 x 3
  patch, which is stricter.
- CPU only, about 290 to 540 steps a second for the 200,000-step runs. No WGSL port and no lab page; nothing
  is on screen yet beyond the runner's `--snapshot` text picture.

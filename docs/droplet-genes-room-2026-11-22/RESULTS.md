# Droplet genes, ROOM: results of the screen

**Date:** 2026-11-22. **Plan, labels and outcome names:** [PLAN.md](./PLAN.md), fixed before the first run of
the protocol (dated copy and hashes in `runs/dropgenes/room/`). **Full tables:** [TABLES.md](./TABLES.md),
generated by `experiments/dropgenes/room.py table`; every run's figures in [room-summary.json](./room-summary.json).

**This is a screen on seen seeds (1 to 4). It is not evidence.** It says where to point a confirmation; the
unseen seeds 41 to 48 were not run.

## Outcome

**Room found.** On the brief's grid the largest gene count held at the frontier stage with a parasite source
present in effect is **six**: a free parasite at `mutPar 1e-5`, 64 sites per tile, in both physics, on 4 of 4
seeds each, with every seed above the parasite floor (0.005; all eight seeds are also above the brief's
example floor of 0.01, the thinnest at 0.0104). The guard passes on the parasite's share alone: the ungated
twin was lost on 4 of 4, but no ungated world without a source was run at six genes, so that loss is not
shown to be the parasite's. With the added ladder rate included the outcome is the same (it holds three
genes at most).

Two things bound that reading:

- **Six is where the grid's site axis ends, not a parasite-set limit and not a measured limit of the
  kernel.** Eight genes fail without any parasite source at every setting run (20 of 20 clean runs lost, 16 of them in stage 1), the
  best of them at 64 sites, the largest site count on the grid. Going from 16 to 64 sites raised what the
  compartments carry without a source from four genes to six; more sites were not tried.
- **Room exists only at the lower dose.** At the first rung's dose (`1e-4`) the most that held is three genes
  (`LARGE`, 16 sites, 3 of 4 seeds), and at 64 sites that dose loses even two. In the six-gene cells at `1e-5`
  the parasite stands at 1 to 4% of copies (0.3 to 3.8% over every held `1e-5` seed); at `1e-4`, where a
  world holds at all, 10 to 13%.

**Per tile, copies per gene fall as 1/K, as the first rung read it; per compartment they do not.** A tile
holds about the same number of copies whatever K is (4 to 5 at `LARGE` with 16 sites, 18 to 20 with 64, 25 at
fuel only with 64), so each gene's share of a tile is close to 1/K of it (within 0.07 of 1/K in every cell
above). What changes is the compartment: its area grows with K (by a half from two genes to six at `LARGE`,
nearly double at fuel only), so copies per gene per compartment fall by less than half where 1/K would be a
third, and at 16 sites they stay near 30 to 38. The cost is paid in the number of compartments and in the
ground they cover: at 64 sites the count falls to a third or a quarter and the covered tiles to half between
two genes and six. A setting is lost as K rises when the count falls to the bar and below (in most lost runs
to zero; a few reached the end with fewer than 10). Why compartments are larger at higher K was not measured;
one reading that fits is that a compartment needs enough copies of every gene and gets them by area when the
sites per tile are shared more ways. With the parasite at `1e-5` the six-gene figures are the same as without
it (3.24 against 3.29 copies per gene per tile at `LARGE`, 4.19 against 4.27 at fuel only; area 23.8 against
22.9 and 16.7 against 16.3 tiles).

**Dose moved the frontier most**, and all of it between `1e-4` and `1e-5`. By the plan's rule the figure is
the largest K held with the source on; for the step `1e-4` to `1e-5` the "parasite in effect" figure is the
same 2.5 rungs. Two things the single-factor table hides:

- **Sites cut both ways.** At `1e-4`, 64 sites lose worlds that 16 sites hold (two and three genes). At the
  lower doses 64 sites are what lets six genes in at all. So more sites raise capacity and, at the top dose,
  the parasite's reach; which of the two wins depends on the dose. The mechanism was not measured.
- **Fuel only was never better than `LARGE` for the frontier.** It gives more compartments at a given K (32
  against 22 at K = 6) but its 16-site worlds lose four genes without any parasite.

## The regime frozen for confirmation

By the plan's rule (highest K held with the parasite in effect; ties by seeds holding against, then late
compartments): **free parasite at `mutPar 1e-5`, fuel only, 64 sites, K = 6** (4 of 4 seeds; 28.8 late
compartments against 19.5 for the same cell at `LARGE`, which is the other candidate). The pass rule, the
commands and the run times are in [PLAN.md](./PLAN.md), "Frozen regime". **Seeds 41 to 48 were not run.**

## What was cut, and what was not run

The step budget (31.5 million steps) was reached with 31,382,000 steps run. By the plan's order, 66 runs were
cut; none of them can change a label or the outcome name:

- **Tier 12 (12 runs):** the ungated arm of the added rate's three held cells (1/4096, K = 3).
- **Tier 13 (54 runs):** seeds 3 and 4 with the source, and the no-source arm, of cells that could never be
  held because seeds 1 and 2 were both lost in stage 2: two genes at `1e-4`, 64 sites (both physics); four
  genes at `1e-4`, `LARGE`, 16 sites; two genes under the ladder at 1/64 and 1/256 (all eight groups) and at
  1/4096, fuel only, 64 sites; six genes at 1/4096, `LARGE`, 64 sites. Their labels ("not held") rest on
  seeds 1 and 2 alone.
- **What the added rate cost.** The ladder rate 1/4096 was this line's addition to the brief. It took about
  9.0 million steps (868,350 in its screen and all 8,158,900 of the ladder's frontier directory, its
  no-source arms included): about 7 of the 25 core-hours. By the plan's order it came after tiers 1 to 6 of
  the brief's grid and before tier 13, so the 54 tier 13 runs that were cut are the brief's own "K just
  above" detail (seeds 3 and 4, and no-source arms at 200,000 steps) given up for it. Without the added rate
  most of tier 13 would have fitted.
- **Carried over, not run:** 37 seed results at the frontier stage are 50,000-step runs that ended early,
  counted as lost at 200,000 steps (the rule in PLAN.md, checked on two seeds); they are marked "[carried]"
  in TABLES.md.
- **Before the plan was frozen:** five timing smoke runs and a plumbing check of the driver (541 runs at
  2,000- and 4,000-step horizons over the whole grid, seeds 1 to 4, in a scratch directory). Two outcomes
  seen in it prompted the added rate; PLAN.md lists them.
- **Not run by design:** no step down below a held cell that does not count (the four `1e-6` groups read "not
  established"); K = 5 and 7 are not on the grid; nothing on any seed above 4.

## Limits

- **Seen seeds, a screen.** Seeds 1 to 4 have been run on this world many times, and two seeds decided stages
  1 and 2. The six-gene cells are 4 of 4 here; that is where to aim a confirmation, not a result.
- **The floor is a judgement.** 0.005 was fixed before the runs from two recorded doses at two genes and 16
  sites. With the brief's example of 0.01 the six-gene cells still count (shares 0.0104 to 0.038; the frozen
  regime's lowest is 0.0147) and one label changes: `LARGE`, 16 sites, K = 4 at `1e-5` would read "absent in
  effect" (two of four seeds under 0.01). The six-gene cells clear the 0.005 floor by a factor of 2 to 8 (and 0.01 by a factor of 1.04 to 3.8); the `1e-6` cells fall under the 0.005 floor while the
  ungated twins of three of them are not held (the six-gene ones ended on 4 of 4). A reader who prefers "the
  ungated twin is not held" as the test of a parasite in effect would count those as well; the outcome name
  is the same either way.
- **One dose step carries the result.** Nothing between `1e-4` and `1e-5` was run, so where in that decade
  room for four and six genes opens is not known.
- **The dose is per tile per step.** A tile at 64 sites holds about four times the copies of one at 16, so the
  same `mutPar` is a lower pressure per copy there. The comparison across sites mixes capacity with that.
- **Six is the top of what the compartments carry on their own on this grid**, whose site axis stops at 64,
  and five and seven were not run. "Room found" by the plan's name means six here; whether more sites carry
  eight was not tried (the first rung's exploratory runs saw eight genes last longer at 64 sites than at 32).
- **The ladder as a parasite source is far harsher than the free parasite.** With two levels (the only ladder
  the species bound allows at six genes) the brief's two rates lose two genes on seeds 1 and 2 (by step 3,150 at 1/64 and by step 23,050 at 1/256),
  and the added rate, chosen to sit inside source A's dose range by a rough conversion, holds three genes and
  not four. So a genome whose genes decay into parasites by mutation has, on this screen, room for three.
  That is the finding that bears most on the next rung, and it rests on seeds 1 to 4 and one added rate.
- 200,000 steps, 64 x 64, CPU reference only. Tracker counts depend on the 50-step census. "Held" is a rule
  on the last tenth of the run (a mean of at least 10 compartments, half of them complete), not stability and
  not even being alive at the last step (one ungated run above); the four-gene `LARGE` 16-site cell sits on
  the bar.

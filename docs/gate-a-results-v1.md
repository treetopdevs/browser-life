Every physical step follows the original focal support through one-to-one spatial overlap. Splits, mergers and lost overlap irreversibly make its identity unresolved. ## Interpretation and stopping point

The physical question is now more precise: the threshold 48 support and its more diffuse threshold 1 surroundings do not give the same maintenance/recovery answer. The former remains active after lesion but does not meet the frozen mass-recovery criterion; the latter recovers mass while the sham's identity becomes ambiguous at a split. This may reflect meaningful organization at different spatial scales, segmentation artifacts, intervention effects, or a combination. This experiment does not resolve those alternatives.

The next foundational design should test a causal boundary between a dense support and its surroundings, with prespecified matched perturbations and viable controls. It should not select a support threshold retrospectively because it passes these outcomes. These cases are development evidence and cannot serve as fresh validation of a revised definition. No further protocol or experiment was launched here.

A function-only calibration pass would not establish reproduction. Here even that calibration is unresolved across supports. Causal origin, independently functioning descendants and a validated nonmass organizational endpoint remain necessary for the stronger claim. There is no actual-rule positive production control in this experiment. No natural-history replay or independent confirmatory biological evaluation was performed.

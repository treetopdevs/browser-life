## Implementation-stage deviations from PLAN v2 (recorded before the freeze commit; dev and replicate-noise data only)

1. **Sensitivity bound `maxFloorCapPpm` = 500,000 (added; PLAN v2 only had "sense tracks on dev").** Calibration measured the replicate-noise floors: delta_cap = 1,429,742 ppm (fixed), 398,139 (paused), 2,592,988 (wandering), 2,222,461 (reversed, after the exact-turn window fix) of the audited inoculum input energy. The fixed floor is 143 percent of the audited input energy, which makes criterion (ii) practically unclearable (capture per input energy is not bounded by one, so this is an operational sensitivity choice, not a mathematical impossibility). The added rule says: if the fixed-schedule capture floor exceeds half the audited input energy (the dev grouped `sense` capture medians were about 400,000 to 640,000 ppm, so the bound encodes "the floor may not exceed the typical scale of the readout it bounds"), the assay is insensitive and the label is `unsupported-measurement`, not "no benefit". The bound was chosen after seeing dev floors (honest: it is a post-hoc sensitivity rule, but it uses dev and noise seeds only, is frozen in `protocol.lock.json` before any fresh seed is read, and the full contrast family, controls and decision are still run and reported). Consequence: the screen is `unsupported-measurement` before any fresh block is read; the fresh seeds 8700501-8700508 are consumed by this run, and a sharper geometry (more bodies, a larger tile, longer horizon, or a lower-variance capture readout) needs new fresh seeds.
2. **`sense-nosig` is dynamically identical to `still`** (identical capture, survival, tracking and coordination numbers in all 40 dev and noise runs compared; the state hashes differ only because the genome words differ): without signal the darkFlee movement offsets cancel. N equals the `still` contrast, `crowding-physical` is zero by identity, and the plan's "movement-capable information-free comparator" does not separate "could move" from "does not move". Recorded in the sealed LIMITS.
3. **Cross-talk** (dev seed 1, fixed): dispersed 958,904 ppm, grouped 963,768 ppm of the neighbourhood signal is foreign; the dispersed geometry is flagged and the claim stays "dispersed". The 24 x 16 tile is effectively one signal field.
4. **Patches vary per block** (patch seed = block seed, so the 8 fresh blocks differ in biomass noise) and replicate-noise runs clone the dev seed 1 patches; floors are the largest spread of each contrast and residual over the four noise seeds, per schedule.
5. **Geometry and schedule values fixed on dev seeds** (PLAN 4.1/4.2 left them to calibration): tile 24 x 16, four radius-2 bodies of mean biomass 96 per cell, nutrient 64, grouped as a 2 x 2 block six cells apart, dispersed at 12 x 8 spacing, both centred four cells west of the t=0 meridian (equal mean offset); T = 1,024; fixed P = 1,024 (about 1.5 times the blind drifter's speed), wander WP = 1,024 and A = 12 cells; census 32; warm-up 128. Cohesion is computed on the four lineage centroids (the biomass field fills the tile, so a mass-based cohesion is almost always unavailable).
6. **Cross-lineage displacement jumps** above six cells per census interval are excluded from the coordination statistic as label hand-overs (a frozen constant, used by the `label-takeover` fixture).
7. Page strips store the first fresh block only; `replay-check` regenerates the first two fresh blocks and the cheap synthetic fixtures (physical fixtures are checked by the saved-artifact path only).

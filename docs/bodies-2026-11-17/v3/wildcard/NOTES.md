# wildcard arm notes (2026-11-18, exploratory, 3 seeds is not a test)
Runs: ./run.sh NAME <harness flags> (each dir has cmd.txt, readout.json, frames). Summaries: node sum.mjs DIR..., node ts.mjs DIR STEP. Late means use t >= T/2.
Config "F" = --scenario crowd --founders 5 --gates grow --tag-mut-rate 65536 --tag-mut-bud --tag-step 40 (default tol 16, genome 16,60,4,0): a bud-born body always gets a mark 40 away from its parent, so the grow gate (contact inhibition on the newborn's FINAL mark) keeps every body apart.

Rung 1 pair (r1-*, 10000 steps, seeds 1-3): two bodies in all 9 runs. plain: 1 component, seam 5-9 permille, patches 2. gated: 2 components, seam 0, bodies keep growing (330-790 cells, above lone ~170).
Rung 2 crowd, no mutation, distinct tags (r2-*): plain = one mat (comp 1.0-1.04, seam 17-25 permille, ~40 basins median 166-183). gates grow / share+grow+signal give identical results (3 clades; one clade is 5499-6574 cells, 2 others ~145-160 frozen = sweep-to-mat inside the dominant clade). Marks are clonal within a clade, so gates separate clades, not bodies.
Rung 3 (the findings): config F, seeds 1-3, 20000 steps (L-F, L-F-s2, L-F-s3): comps 34.9/34.5/39.1 late mean, largest component share 123/95/78 permille, seam exactly 0, ~6890 cells, 13-16 distinct marks, patch births/deaths after t~4000 = 0 (jammed-mosaic). Tag-blind control (same marks, gates inert): 1.47/1.16/1.22 components, 1000 permille largest, seam 129-143 permille: it is the plain fused mat. Founder tags all 'same' (L-F-same): 36/32/38 components, because bud births mutate marks, so same founder tags does not remove the effect.
 Frames: L-F/frame-t20000.png shows ~35 compact, cell-like bodies separated by 1-2 tile dark gaps, each its own hue, holes inside; control L-Fblind/frame-t20000.png shows one speckled mat with colour drift patches and white seam cells.
 Gene mutation ON (M-F-mut, rate 12/256): comps 21/8/17 (gene-blind control s1: 34.9). Genes move in the first 2000 steps (bud 4.0 -> 2.6, rim 0 -> 11.6, s2) then everything is frozen (0 deaths). So mutation changes who fills the map first, no replacement afterwards.
 Wound radius 4 (W-F-s1..3, wound at 12000): the 33-36 killed cells in the disk are 82-93% live again within 200-400 steps in the target basin (observer basin ~90 cells, not the whole body); disk foreign marks 0/0/7 of ~27-30 (the neighbouring body took a few tiles). Radius 8 (WB-r8-s1,s2; s3 and r11 have no valid wound centre): disk 112-128 tiles live again by +5000; s1 all 114 carry the original mark, s2 36 of 128 are foreign (a neighbour body took part of the hole). rootAlive turned false in W-F-s1 (t+1000) and WB-r8-s1: the observer root moved, the body did not die. Tag-blind wound r11 (WB-r11-blind-s1): 85-128 of 150 foreign marks in the disk (drift mat).
Failure modes seen: sweep-to-mat (plain, tag-blind, r2 dominant clade); jammed-mosaic (F at SUPPLY 24: lifelike geometry, zero turnover); dust-or-film (F at SUPPLY 15 and 17, UPK 5, and partly 18-19: lacy mazy bodies, ~120-390 components, F15 has ~13 patch births and deaths per 200 steps but ages <= 13000 and tiny basins median 14); seam-cost/raid without grow gate (L-C/D/I): one connected mat with ~40 patches, many small basins (median 32-70), seam 25-160 permille, patch turnover 0.02-0.35 per sample; the tag-blind controls show 0.25 births per sample too (drift), so that turnover is not distinguishable.
Turnover vs compactness trade-off: SUPPLY 18-19 gives a few deaths (3-10 of ~45 bodies, 15000 steps) but bodies are lacy. No regime found with compact bodies AND births+deaths.
Bug-ish: none certain. Observations: frames 'lineage' panel is almost one colour (all lineage tags equal in the gated world). --tags same does not stop bud-born marks. woundTrack 'basin' is an observer basin (~90 cells) not the tag body (~200+).

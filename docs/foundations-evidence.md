# Foundation claims: evidence and limits

This audit reads only the compact committed summaries `experiments/m4/gradient-m3.json` and `experiments/m4/spots-m3.json`; it does not reopen run bundles. Reproduce the tables with `deno run -A tools/foundation-audit.ts`. Means and ranges below are over the listed per-run summary values, with unavailable values excluded and their sample counts retained.

## What these M4 reports show

The gradient report contains 120 run rows (20 per condition); all 20 treatment rows are listed against `m4Descriptive.runs=20` (`experiments/m4/gradient-m3.json:17`). The spots report contains 50 rows (10 per condition), with 10/10 treatment rows listed against its descriptive denominator (`experiments/m4/spots-m3.json:17`). This is coverage of the compact report rows, not a census-by-census completeness audit.

Across those run summaries, mean role count ranges from 1.128 (gradient no-mutation) to 3.636 (gradient uniform-light); gradient treatment averages 3.041, range 2–4. Spots treatment averages 2.420, range 1.900–2.902, and spots no-signal-motility averages 2.215, range 1.892–2.736. Thus the observed count is bounded by four roles, but a finite count can still have a positive or negative trend. For example, spots no-signal-motility `rolesPresentTrend` averages +0.057797 per 10⁵ steps (range −0.010469 to +0.177308), while gradient treatment averages +0.005418 (range −0.192082 to +0.189332). These are descriptive finite-window slopes, not evidence of indefinite or statistically supported growth. The code defines role count as `rolesPresent.length` on scheduled deep censuses (`experiments/endpoints.ts:417`, `tools/analyze.ts:344`).

`compartmentalised` is the mean count of individuals with a membrane-rich rim and biomass-rich core; it and its trend use scheduled deep-census observations (`experiments/endpoints.ts:407`, `tools/analyze.ts:335`, `tools/analyze.ts:343`). In the saved run summaries, gradient neutral is zero in 16/20 runs; the other four are positive at 0.002 each, with mean 0.0004 and maximum 0.002. Each of the other five gradient conditions is zero in 20/20. All five spots conditions are zero in 10/10 each. Thus 166/170 run summaries store zero, including all 20 gradient and 10 spots treatment runs; the four positive rows are all gradient neutral. The observer averages finite values from the second half of the available series (`tools/analyze.ts:274`); since this is a nonnegative count, an exact zero mean entails that every finite observation included in that mean was zero. The compact reports do not establish whether every scheduled census was present, and these observed zeros do not prove compartments physically impossible under the rules.

The stored heredity fields are exactly `muPooled`, `sigmaPooled`, `muMedianRun`, and `sigmaMedianRun`, alongside `runs` and `fissions` (`tools/analyze.ts:639`, `experiments/m4/gradient-m3.json:81`, `experiments/m4/spots-m3.json:81`). In gradient treatment, the 20 runs and 588,810 fissions yield pooled μ/σ sibling correlations 0.955/0.944 and median-per-run correlations 0.901/0.855. In spots treatment, 10 runs and 47,412 fissions yield 0.875/0.925 pooled and 0.863/0.928 median-per-run. The analyzer defines these as double-entry Pearson sibling correlations between the two pieces' growth parameters at fission; the per-run median avoids between-run differences inflating the statistic (`tools/analyze.ts:612`, `tools/analyze.ts:618`). They support sibling resemblance in these recorded parameters. Calling them realized genetic heritability, or evidence of a population-genetic response to selection, would go beyond the statistic.

The M3 bootstrap also needs precise wording: mean individual mass and speed are the two MAP-Elites archive cell descriptors (`packages/search/src/mapelites.ts:57`); individual count is measured in the evaluation output but is not an archive axis (`packages/search/src/evaluate.ts:67`). Quality scores survival, recovery, regeneration, and light dependence (`packages/search/src/evaluate.ts:86`). The no-mutation control sets `mutRate` to zero (`packages/runner/src/conditions.ts:24`); that prevents new mutation but does not itself disable selection, so selection on standing variation remains possible. The reports do not isolate that causal explanation by themselves.

Exact stored fields and definitions remain the analyzer's sibling correlations, not a direct functional-heredity test.

## Check of the individuality small-run write-up

The small-run section reports 12 analysis worlds and 4 disjoint calibration worlds, correctly labels `T=149` as far below the per-world 1,728 occupancy floor, and marks rows with fewer than eight worlds as descriptive ranges (`docs/individuality-info-theory.md:251`, `docs/individuality-info-theory.md:259`). Its table supports the conclusion that no default-profile nonClosure result clears Holm at 0.05. The full-family minimum adjusted p is 0.081, which is above 0.05, so it also does not clear the threshold (`docs/individuality-info-theory.md:324`). The two best-supported colony rows have n=4 and n=6, both below the stated interval threshold, and their reported p-values do not support cross-world coupling (`docs/individuality-info-theory.md:338`). Those conclusions are appropriately narrow: no evidence cleared the declared test threshold at this scale, not proof that higher-level individuality cannot occur.

The source report and raw bundles for that separate `tools/individuality.ts` small run are not among the two assigned committed M4 JSON files, so this check verifies the small-run write-up's arithmetic and caveats against its displayed table and stated method, not an independent recomputation of its underlying MI estimates.

## 2026-09-28: transplant pilot and seed-2 catalog

This bounded follow-up reads the completed seed-1 transplant manifest and seed-2 extraction report only; it does not reopen their large source bundles. The source-linked artifacts are `runs/foundations/transplant-pilot-gradient-seed-1-v1/manifest.json` (notably `#/shams`, `#/controls`, `#/candidates`, `#/execution`, and `#/codeFilesBefore`/`#/codeFilesAfter`) and `runs/foundations/extract-gradient-seed-2-v1.json` (`#/rule`, `#/provenance`, `#/times/*/components`, and `#/times/*/world`).

The seed-1 pilot is complete: all 18 candidate records and both controls have status `complete`; the three sham records are `matched`. Each garden has exactly 30 census records at steps 100 through 3000, and every one of the resulting 600 garden census rows has `conservationOk=true`, string residuals `matterResidual="0"` and `energyResidual="0"`, and `mutations=0`. All three shams report matched control/restored physics and artifact hashes, conservation, and observation hashes. The manifest also records identical source, code, catalog, and rule hash maps before and after the pilot. These are integrity and accounting checks, not independent replications of the source experiment.

Under the recorded rule—focal survival means focal biomass above zero at step 3000, positive growth compares that focal biomass with the placed packet's initial biomass, and late polymer requires P above zero at each of the final three censuses—16/18 candidates survived and all 16 had positive growth. The breakdown is 6/6 candidates sampled at source step 100,000; 4/6 at 500,000; and 6/6 at 900,000. Two candidates, both from the 500,000 sample, were extinct at the final census. Only 2/18 candidates had late polymer, and both also met the growth criterion. Among survivors, final focal B ranged from 217,395 to 1,772,712. Candidate packet B ranged from 1,448 to 5,141; these packet inventories describe the transferred material, while final B is the focal-lineage amount after 3,000 garden steps. It is not a matched initial/final whole-world resource comparison. The two single-run controls also differ: founder-9 survived and grew to focal B 85,741 without late polymer; the zero-controller control went extinct. Because these controls share the same physical initial state and seed and differ only by zeroing the 160 controller-weight bytes, this pair supports a causal effect of that controller intervention for this one founder-9 state and garden seed. It does not generalize across genotypes or seeds, or identify which weights or functions account for the outcome. The candidate results show persistence feasibility for many selected packets in this particular no-mutation garden, not 16 independent viable organisms or a population-level mutation/selection response. Each garden starts from a selected pure-genotype packet with mutation disabled; the resulting growth, rearrangements, and observer-inferred splits do not alone establish repeated biological reproduction, and there is no within-garden standing genetic variation for selection to sort.

The seed-2 extraction report is a usable, observer-compatible catalog-only result at 100,000, 500,000, and 900,000 steps, with the source run identified as treatment seed 2 and selection rule `perStratumPerTime=3`. At each snapshot it records three selected intermediate and three selected abundant components, with zero selected rare components (6 selected components per time; 18 across snapshots). All 18 selected components are tracker-join eligible and have no rejection reasons. At 100,000, one selected intermediate component (index 273) contains P=190; the other five selected components contain P=0. At 500,000 and 900,000 all six selected components at each time contain P=0. The corresponding saved whole-world P totals are 7,026, 1,540, and 349. Thus P occurs in one selected seed-2 component at the earliest sampled time, but not among the selected components at the later snapshots. This stratified sample is not a census of all components, and the catalog report alone says nothing about transplant outcomes for seed 2.

These are proposed next experiments, outside the current execution. They do not raise M4's entry requirements.

| M5: does a changed phenotype perform a causal ecological function? | Founder-2/7 nominees repeatedly change measured role shares while preserving the tested M3 outcomes. | Freeze candidates and a proposed resource interaction; compare recipient alone, conditioned resources, removal of the proposed contribution, matched restoration, sham and unrelated conditioning. | M7: can a collective reproduce its organization? | A1 distinguishes initial genome-copy origin from old-material transport; A2 exposes unresolved separation and reconstruction measurements. | First validate separation in multi-component scenes on constructed cases and new natural inputs. Then freeze a mixed-genotype propagule assay with cohesion, viable transfer, composition rebuilding in new structure, and repeated cycles. | Copy origin, growth, fragmentation, persistence of a structured inoculum or a single successful transfer do not establish collective heredity. |

The current evidence favors repairing specific measurement gaps before changing the substrate. If a later representation change is justified, compare one mechanism at matched search effort and ecological conditions; preserve the present rules as a baseline. A failed finite search cannot establish impossibility, and a constructed or searched positive example establishes feasibility without demonstrating spontaneous evolutionary origin.

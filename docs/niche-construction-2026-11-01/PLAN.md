## 1. Bounded question

In small `sim-ref` worlds with mutation off, does what a builder leaves behind in the field (structural polymer P and the nutrient/waste/energy pattern it shapes) causally raise a receiver's survival and receiver-labelled biomass inventory ("growth" only where flux attribution shows synthesis, 4.4), relative to (i) a nonbuilder history, (ii) a cost-retaining ablation of the builder, (iii) a matched-inventory spatial scramble of the same field, and (iv) the receiver alone at the same inoculum? Every transfer is a budget-audited analytical intervention with an exact conservation residual of zero.

A positive answer is "a dependency edge builder->receiver is supported for this pair, horizon and field". A negative answer ("no causal benefit detected") is a valid result and is reported as such. It does not say the second link is impossible, does not test whether mutations reach the builder or receiver (that is the next rung), and does not test A+B->C.

Rule that decides the edge label is pre-stated in 5, before any scientific run.

## 2. What is built (one PR)

1. **Evidence fixture with a negative case that fools the naive metric** (section 4). Naive metric: "receiver biomass after conditioning exceeds receiver biomass without conditioning". Negative fixture `energy-gift`: a field that differs from the control only by extra stored free energy E (no polymer, no structure). Naive metric reports a large "benefit"; the matched-inventory and inoculum-matched controls reproduce it, so the edge is labelled unsupported. Positive fixture `hand-wall`: a hand-built polymer structure (known history, no builder involved) whose benefit disappears when the same polymer inventory is scrambled, to show the assay can see spatial dependence at all; it is qualified at the frozen scientific scale on dev (G3), not only at unit-test scale. Further fixtures: `null-field` (identical start in both arms, zero effect), `takeover-only` (no synthesis possible, yet receiver-labelled biomass rises by relabelling existing detritus: the naive inventory metric is fooled a second way).
2. **Small CPU path.** An exact transfer module (ledger, relabel, inoculum placement, scramble), four-arm assay runner on `RefSim`, decision function, evidence sealing. Library in `packages/runner/src/niche/` (no new package, so no alias-map edits; the tool resolves `@bl/*` with the same `registerHooks` shim as `tools/predictability/atlas.ts`). Observers are read-only; a plain-vs-instrumented twin test shows the readout does not change physics.
3. **Manifest and machine-readable evidence.** `docs/niche-construction-2026-11-01/evidence.json` and `manifest.json`, `--write` / `--check` / `--replay-check`.
4. **Working local page** `apps/lab/niche/index.html`, kept off the deployed site like `/predictability/`: the arms side by side per candidate, ledgers, effect sizes with intervals, dependency edge drawn supported / unsupported / unavailable, plus the negative fixture panel. Reads `evidence.json` only; runs no simulation.

## 4. Design

### 4.1 Worlds and stages

Single tile, `tileW=tileH=24` (fixed; no 32x32, for the cost budget in G6), `mutRate=0`, `genes:"ladder"`, `kGene`, `geneFlip:0`, `geneDrive` and light/nutrient from dev calibration, `injury*` absent unless calibration shows polymer is otherwise inert (then `injuryPeriod/Radius/Prob/Shell` set identically in all arms; this is an explicit protocol choice, recorded). `T1` and `T2` are each at most 120. Two stages:

- **Stage 1 (conditioning),** `T1` steps from a **declared total budget** `(matter0, energy0)`: nutrient A everywhere, builder (or nonbuilder) founder cells in a fixed source patch, and a **receiver reserve** `R = (B_r, E_r)` carried off-lattice from step 0 and inert during Stage 1. Accounting follows `packages/schema/src/accounting.ts`, where bound matter B is valued at `eB` per quantum and A at `eA`: funding the reserve removes `B_r` quanta of lattice A (footprint-free cells, raster order), and removes from lattice E exactly `(eB-eA)*B_r + E_r`, so that `reserveEnergy = eB*B_r + E_r` is paid for exactly. Matter: `lattice matter + B_r` is the same in every arm and in the receiver-alone controls. The audited invariant is **lattice plus reserve plus cumulative analytic heat**, cumulative across both stages: `ledgerResidual({energy: energy0}, state) + reserveEnergy + cumulativeHeatAnalytic === 0n` (the helper itself counts neither external term, so the module wraps it and tests the wrapper). Insufficient lattice A or E to fund the reserve makes the block `invalid-or-incomplete`.
- **Stage 2 (assay),** `T2` steps after the transfer, receiver cells tagged by a reserved lineage-id set disjoint from builder ids (mutation off keeps tags stable). One seed per block gives the sim seed for both stages. **Absolute clock:** every Stage 2 arm and control (including the receiver-alone ones) starts at absolute step `T1` with the same sim seed, so the counter-based PRNG keys and the `(step+1) % injuryPeriod` schedule coincide across arms; receiver-alone worlds are constructed and their step counter set to `T1` rather than run from 0. A test checks that every arm in a block sees the same injury steps and RNG keys.

### 4.2 Arms (per candidate pair and seed block)

Conditioning histories (Stage 1): `builder` (BUILD+decomp), `nonbuilder` (decomp only, same genome weights, same founders, same budget), `ablation` (builder history, polymer converted to C at transfer per 3).

Transfers (Stage 2), applied to each history:

- **(a) co-culture:** receiver inoculum placed, living cells kept.
- **(b) builder-removed whole-field transfer:** living cells relabelled non-living: lineage and genome cleared, B/P/E/A/C/S kept in place (matter and energy unchanged, relabel recorded with before/after totals); then the inoculum is placed.
- **(c) matched-inventory scramble:** (b), then each channel (A,B,C,P,E,S) independently permuted over all lattice cells by a seeded integer permutation that does not depend on the history (same permutation for the same seed). Every channel total is preserved exactly; structure is not. Called "scrambled", never "unconditioned". The scramble also destroys E/B ratios, signal gradients and their alignment with receivers (controller inputs), so (c) alone cannot say that polymer arrangement is the cause. Two attribution reconstructions therefore run on the builder history: **(cP) P-only scramble** (permute only P; every other channel stays in place) and **(cNP) non-P scramble** (permute everything except P). They label the edge's mechanism (see 5.3); they do not gate supported/unsupported.

Controls independent of history:

- **(d0) receiver-alone:** empty lattice with the same total matter/energy budget in uniform A (nothing conditioned), same inoculum.
- **(d1E) energy-matched receiver-alone** and **(d1B) biomass-matched receiver-alone:** (d0) with, separately, (d1E) the receiver reserve free energy raised by `dE = max(0, sum E over region in (b) - sum E over region in (d0))`, or (d1B) the reserve B raised by `dB = max(0, sum (A+B+C+P) bound-matter excess over region)` with its chemical-energy debit `(eB-eA)*dB`; the region is the inoculum footprint **plus a halo of radius `h`** (halo declared in the protocol, so an energy gift in an annulus just outside the footprint is matched). The raise is taken from the lattice (debits recorded, totals unchanged, `validateState` and pool caps checked before writing). Inoculum placement is on empty cells, so the footprint itself has no bound matter by construction and the halo is what makes bound-matter matching non-vacuous. These are the "more initial stored energy / matter explains it" controls (DESIGN section 2 requires biomass and free energy varied separately). The decision uses the stronger: `Y_inoc = max(Y(d1E), Y(d1B))`. This is a screen on resource totals delivered as inoculum, not proof that no spatial resource effect exists; stated as a limit.

Inoculum placement (all arms): a fixed footprint of `Nf` cells (disc around a fixed centre, raster order) chosen before any run. Candidate cells are those with `B=P=0`, no lineage, and E and S below `POOL_MAX` minus the per-cell share in **every** arm of the block; the inoculum `(B_r,E_r)` is split evenly over the first `Nmin..Nf` such cells (remainder to the first cells). Nothing is overwritten: only empty bound-matter cells receive B and E, and if fewer than `Nmin=12` common cells exist the block is `invalid-or-incomplete`, reported, and makes the candidate `invalid-or-incomplete` (blocks are not dropped; see 5.3). Every transferred state passes `validateState`, with pool capacity and combined lattice-plus-reserve matter limits checked before any unsigned-buffer write. The receiver genome is the receiver founder, lineage ids from the reserved set.

### 4.3 Budget audit (`transfer.ts`)

Every step produces a `TransferRecord`: `before` and `after` over **lattice plus reserve** (A,B,C,P,E,S, reserve B and E, matter, energy), `living`, `moved` (relabel counts), `converted` (P->C quanta and `heatAnalytic = (eP-eC)*P`, a named analytical heat term, cumulative), `reserveDebit`, `matterResidual = after.matter - before.matter` and `residual = (after.energy + heatAnalytic) - before.energy`; both must be exactly `0n` (BigInt). Placing the inoculum moves quanta from the reserve onto the lattice, so only the lattice-plus-reserve sum is conserved, never the lattice alone. The cumulative invariant of 4.1 holds at every census of both stages. A nonzero residual, an insufficient debit inventory or a failed `validateState` invalidates the block and fails the test. Starting totals per arm are published in evidence.

### 4.4 Readouts (per arm, per block)

Receiver survival (any receiver-lineage B at T2, and at each census); **receiver-labelled biomass inventory** (receiver-lineage B at T2) as the primary endpoint, named that way because transport assigns one genome to a destination's whole incoming bound mass including non-living material (`step.ts` reintegration), so labelled B can rise by takeover of existing detritus with no synthesis (fixture `takeover-only`). "Growth" is claimed only when flux attribution (below) is available and the synthesis share (PHOTO+GROW) of the receiver's gain is at least half; otherwise the edge text says "inventory benefit, growth not attributed". Builder cost: builder-lineage B, lattice/ledger E and A deltas and cumulative heat from census totals; **`kGene` maintenance is not separable** from catalytic and ordinary maintenance in `react` and is reported `unavailable`, not inferred. Inventories before/after each transfer from the audit record. **Spatial dependence** = (b) minus (c) receiver outcome. **Flux attribution** uses the four `roles` counters (PHOTO, GROW, DECOMP, RESP) read by a read-only observer at **every** Stage 2 step (so sampling is complete); each counter saturates at 65,535 in the reference step, so attribution is `unavailable` for a block if any counter reaches 65,535 at any step, with the reason recorded. There is no `mulShr` clamp flag and the plan does not pretend one exists. Census samples are never integrated as if they were every reaction.

### 4.5 Naive-metric trap and fixtures

Unit-test fixtures use 16x16 worlds with `kernelRadius` 5 (schema validation requires dimensions divisible by eight and `2*kernelRadius+1 <= min(tile)`; 12x12 and the default radius 9 are rejected, checked) and few steps so they stay in unit-test time. The positive control is additionally qualified at the frozen scientific scale (24x24, T2, injury regime) on dev (G3); a pass at unit scale does not qualify the full assay.
- `energy-gift`: pair of fields identical except extra E. Three variants: uniform (scramble leaves it unchanged), inside the footprint (scramble removes it, so the scramble alone would mislabel it "spatial"; d1E catches it), and in an annulus just outside the footprint but inside the halo (the case the original footprint-only matching missed; d1E with the halo catches it). All three must be labelled `unsupported-within-tested-domain` while the naive metric `D_naive` is positive.
- `hand-wall`: two pre-stated hand-built structures, W1 a polymer ring around the footprint (gates A/C diffusion) and W2 a polymer floor under the footprint cells (local-P shield under `injuryShell`, which tests cell-local P after decay and so is not provided by a surrounding ring). Same P inventory in the scrambled comparator. The control passes if either shows spatial dependence (positive paired sign test on dev, same rule as 5.3) in the frozen regime; the one used is recorded. If neither does, the slice stops at "assay insensitive" (G3).
- `takeover-only`: synthesis disabled (PHOTO and GROW zero) and receiver-labelled B still rises from relabelling builder detritus; labelled-inventory endpoint is positive, synthesis share zero, so the edge text may not say "growth".
- `null-field`, `empty-receiver-reserve` (reserve spent twice detected), `overwrite-attempt` (placement on occupied cell refused), `pool-cap` (placement refused when a cell is at `POOL_MAX`, with a validateState-rejecting state never emitted), `underfunded-reserve`.

## 5. Pre-stated rules (frozen before the first fresh-seed run)

### 5.1 Candidates and splits
- **Builder:** the `genes:"ladder"` BUILD carrier of founder cluster in the panel `{0, 4, 9}` (clusters of M3 with distinct controllers; cluster 4 and 9 are also the I05 probe clusters, an overlap that is stated). Panel is fixed by id here, not by outcome. **Receiver:** the cluster-12 founder (no BUILD gene), a different controller family. 3 builder candidates x 1 receiver. If the runtime gate G6 fails, drop candidates in order 9, 4, never seeds; dropping is by this runtime rule only and the dropped candidate is reported as `not run`, never as negative.
- **Dev (`calibration`) seeds:** 4 blocks; **fresh (`confirmation`) seeds:** 8 blocks, disjoint from dev and from every reservation in the machine-readable `experiments/discovery/seed-registry.json` (which holds ranges, including 8,500,001-8,599,999, absent from `docs/plan.md`) and from `docs/plan.md`. The two numeric ranges are added to that registry **before** any run; the complete seed lists and the registry digest are bound into the protocol hash; a test calls `seedCollisions` against the file. Dev outcomes may set `T1`, `T2`, grid size, `geneDrive`, `B_r/E_r`, `delta`, footprint, injury yes/no. Fresh seeds are run once after the protocol hash is written to `manifest.json`; fresh results are reported separately and never used to tune. Candidate-level outcomes on dev are not used to drop a candidate (only runtime does).

### 5.2 Estimands
Primary: `Y = receiver-labelled biomass at T2` (survival indicator reported separately), paired within a block across arms. Estimand for each contrast: the probability over fresh block seeds that the per-block paired difference `D` is strictly positive exceeds 1/2 (a median-positive claim about the block-seed distribution, which is what the exact sign test addresses; no symmetry assumption is needed). Contrasts (integers, per block):
- `D_builder = Y(b | builder) - Y(b | nonbuilder)`
- `D_poly = Y(b | builder) - Y(b | ablation)`
- `D_space = Y(b | builder) - Y(c | builder)`
- `D_inoc = Y(b | builder) - max(Y(d1E), Y(d1B))`
- Attribution only (never gates): `D_spaceP = Y(b | builder) - Y(cP | builder)`, `D_spaceNP = Y(b | builder) - Y(cNP | builder)`.
- `D_naive = Y(b | builder) - Y(d0)` (reported to show what the naive metric says; never gates). Co-culture (a) reported alongside and labelled as co-culture.

### 5.3 Edge label (per candidate, on fresh blocks)
- **Validity:** all 8 assigned fresh blocks must be valid with every ledger exactly zero; a placement or ledger failure makes the candidate `invalid-or-incomplete` (blocks are never dropped, because placement exclusions depend on the conditioned fields and dropping them would select on treatment).
- **Per-contrast test:** exact one-sided sign test on the 8 paired differences, **zeros counted as non-positive** (conservative), `p = P(Binomial(8,1/2) >= k)` with `k` = number of strictly positive blocks: `k=8` gives 1/256, `k=7` gives 9/256. A contrast also needs a median per-block effect of at least `delta`.
- **Candidate p-value:** intersection-union over the four gating contrasts: `p_cand = max` of the four p-values (all four must be significant at the candidate's threshold, no within-candidate correction needed). `delta` and the survival rule below are part of the same conjunction.
- **Across candidates:** Holm over the (up to 3) candidates at alpha 0.05, thresholds 1/60, 1/40, 1/20 for 3 candidates (if candidates were dropped by the runtime rule, over those that ran). With 8 blocks the first rejection needs `k=8` in all four contrasts (1/256 < 1/60; `k=7` gives 9/256, above 1/60 and 1/40, below 1/20). Resolution is thereby stated, not hidden: this design can only certify a large, consistent effect.
- **`delta`:** positive and set on dev only: `delta = max(ceil(0.02*B_r), 1.5 * max|D|)` over a **stage-2 replicate control** (same conditioned field, different Stage 2 PRNG seed, nonbuilder vs nonbuilder and builder vs builder); identical-twin replays always give exactly 0 and serve as correctness tests only, never as noise estimates. Estimating `max|D|` from 4 dev blocks is a stated crude floor.
- **Survival:** the receiver survives in (b) in at least 7 of 8 fresh blocks.
- **Labels:** `supported` iff all of the above hold. Mechanism text: "polymer-arrangement-attributed" only if `D_spaceP` also passes the sign test and delta while `D_spaceNP` does not; "non-polymer field pattern" if the reverse; otherwise "combined conditioned field". `unsupported-within-tested-domain` iff valid, measurement adequate and any gating test fails. `unsupported-measurement` iff the positive control (`hand-wall`) shows no effect in the frozen regime, or the builder produced no polymer at T1 (builder P total at or below the dev replicate floor) on dev. `invalid-or-incomplete` for ledger, placement, validation or funding failures.
- **Panel verdict (three-valued):** any candidate `supported` => "causal benefit detected for pair X within this domain"; "no causal benefit detected" only when every candidate that ran was valid, adequately measured (positive control passed) and `unsupported-within-tested-domain`; any invalid or measurement-limited candidate yields an overall `invalid-or-incomplete` or `unsupported-measurement` verdict that names which candidates are negative and which are not usable.

## 9. Non-goals and limits

- No mutation search, no accessibility claim, no A+B->C test, no predation actuator, no organism abstraction, no declared-cell mode.
- No physics or rule change; no new `WorldConfig` key; GPU parity not claimed (CPU only; the transfers are analytical interventions outside the ecology and are not reproduction instructions).
- The pinned `polymerTransport` ablation is not used; the analytical P->C ablation tests a different (narrower) statement. Builder and nonbuilder differ in the BUILD gene and therefore in its maintenance cost; the ablation arm and matched scramble are the controls for that, not the nonbuilder alone.
- Non-living relabelled builder matter (arm b/c) may itself decay or feed the receiver; this is the transformed material and is exactly why (c), (d1E/d1B) and the ablation exist, not a claim that detritus is inert.
- Stage 2 uses one receiver genome and one conditioned field per candidate; a negative bounds this panel, horizon and field only. A candidate showing benefit on dev only is a calibration result, not evidence.
- Eight fresh blocks per candidate with exact sign tests and Holm across candidates can only certify an effect that is positive in all (or nearly all) blocks; a modest real effect will read as unsupported. Stated, and the result is exploratory.
- Small finite worlds (24x24, T at most 120 each): effects that need larger scales or longer horizons are out of reach; reported as such.
- Seeds, scrambles and placement are deterministic; timing numbers are informational and excluded from sealed evidence.
- Everything here is exploratory and not an addition to the frozen preregistration.

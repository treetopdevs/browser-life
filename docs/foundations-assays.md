# Foundation assays: feasibility protocol and interpretation

These are exploratory follow-ups to the frozen 2026-09-27 M4 registration. They do not change its endpoints, reinterpret the completed M4 ensemble, or support a confirmatory claim. `tools/lib/foundation-assays.ts` checks a proposed assay manifest before execution; it does not extract organisms, run simulations, verify files, or calculate an effect. A passing dry run means the *declared design* is complete, not that its provenance statements are true. File digests and biological outcomes still need independent verification.

## Source histories and extraction

Use at least five independent source worlds for a feasibility pilot; five is not a confirmatory sample size. A tile, organism, repeated census, reciprocal placement, or migrating member of one ring is nested within its source history. Keep source-world identity on every observation and resample worlds, not organisms, for uncertainty estimates. Freeze the source code revision and code digest, rule/schema/metrics versions, bundle digest, run ID, seed, and original terminal digest with its recorded hash mode before choosing samples. Reserve future confirmation seeds 700000001–700999999; do not use them for this pilot.

Sample each source at early, middle, and late checkpoints (proposed 100,000, 500,000, and 900,000 steps within a one-million-step history). Optional `.blck` checkpoints contain full cells, genome, and observer state. The runner's manifest lists checkpoint step, path and *state* hash; verify the checkpoint bytes and separately record a digest for the validated extracted artifact. The completed M4 sample bundles inspected for this work have `checkpointEvery: 0` and empty checkpoint lists. For those, reconstruct once per source history from its recorded initial state under the frozen code, retain validated copies at all three steps, and match the original manifest's `summary.finalHash` at one million steps under its **combined physics-plus-observer** digest definition before accepting the cache. The frozen runner at `b7fd4c1` uses `artifactDigest(final, observer)` here. Record a separate reconstructed physics `stateHash`; compare original observation files by content digest to assess observational parity independently. Do not replay separately for each assay arm. If exact reconstruction fails, mark that source unusable. This is an extraction requirement for those bundles, not a claim that every future assay needs replay.

Extraction can alter or destroy an individual's structure. Before using an extracted sample, quantify intact mass, viability and immediate post-transplant persistence against a sham extraction/reinsertion control at each sampled age. Record failed extractions and exclusions. Avoid choosing only unusually robust late forms. A checkpoint gives state and genomes, but the current `heredity.tsv` contains fission sibling traits without parent/child IDs; `life.jsonl` gives inferred event IDs, and a tracker checkpoint keeps alive individuals only. Linkable longitudinal parent/offspring traits may therefore need a validated replay observer or a new assay-side readout. No physics or core observer change is proposed here.

## Time-shift competition

Within each source history compare early and late inocula in identical assay conditions. Conditioned *chemical/pool* environments must be extracted separately from resident organisms, with resource quantities measured after preparation. Cross the environment's **time** (early or late) with its **origin** (own or foreign world), and add a standardized common garden. The environment-time axis is essential: an all-middle pool design cannot test time-shift adaptation. A resident-community challenge, if later added, is a separate treatment with its own inoculum and resource accounting; it must not be silently mixed into a pool-only result. Foreign worlds should be independently sourced and rotated across focal worlds.

For each world and environment-time block use both equal-frequency starts and rare-invader starts. In the rare design reverse which age is rare, so an early invader faces late competitors and a late invader faces early competitors. Record each inoculum's biomass and energy, keep total starting biomass and energy equal across arms, and reverse left/right placement for each focal age. Fresh assay RNG seeds 630000001–630099999 are separate from source selection, source replay and original run seeds. The dry-run planner requires the resulting four arms for each environment/start block and a viable extraction control. The assay endpoint should be a preregistered change in frequency or invasion growth over a fixed window, with extinction and ties handled explicitly. Analyze paired age contrasts within worlds and then aggregate at the independent-world level.

**Interpretation.** A late form winning in both own and foreign pools can be consistent with general adaptation, size or vitality selection, and extraction bias; it does not alone establish increasing complexity. Home advantage in a conditioned pool can reflect local abiotic adaptation or niche construction; it does not by itself establish a Red Queen process. A Red Queen claim needs a specific interacting biotic challenger, reciprocal time-shift challenges and a changing fitness advantage that tracks partner history. A null contrast does not prove no cumulative adaptation: power, extraction loss, saturation, and assay mismatch remain alternatives. Treat effect margins and the chosen decision threshold as provisional until a new, prospective registration; report inconclusive outcomes explicitly.

## Life-cycle heredity

The existing tracker infers fission through cell overlap and records `parent`, `generation`, `born`, and optional `birthTraits`; the observer also attributes nearby same-lineage births as budding. Fusions can merge identities, and the serializable tracker drops dead-history detail. A sibling correlation from `heredity.tsv` is resemblance between fission pieces at one census, not a parent/offspring regression. Parent and offspring share material and local environment; birth age, size, and observation timing can manufacture resemblance. Retain explicit parent/child IDs, consecutive generations, source world, birth and measurement steps, matched ages, masses, environmental block, censoring and fusion status for every pair. Prespecify a trait at a fixed age, mass adjustment, and handling for censored/fused pairs. At least three observed generations per source world is a pilot gate; it is not evidence of stable multigenerational inheritance by itself.

Use a common garden: move sampled descendants from different source times/worlds into standardized, resource-matched environments with randomized placement and fresh seeds 640000001–640099999. The dry-run design records own/foreign gardens, reciprocal sides, starting matter/energy and viable transplant controls. Where possible, split sibling material across gardens. Require a connected parent→child→grandchild chain before labeling a source as having three observed generations; a lone 2→3 pair does not satisfy this. Record sources with no chain or failed extraction in the source-world denominator instead of dropping them. Estimate parent/offspring resemblance with world and environment effects, then compare against matched unrelated pairs and a shuffled-parent null within world and generation. Reproductive success and survival need explicit follow-up windows; right-censored lives must not be coded as failures. Report raw resemblance separately from any adjusted estimate of transmissible variation. Fusions, budding attribution and overlap errors are sensitivity strata or exclusions, not silently clean pedigrees.

## Long-horizon decision

The 10⁷-step extension is already registered as a *secondary* analysis, not a rescue of the failed primary M4 endpoint. The concrete budget is gradient-m3 treatment, neutral and no-mutation at seeds 101–105, plus neutral-only threshold pilot seeds 1101–1110: 25 histories, 250 million steps at the existing census/deep cadence. Record the source code and analysis digests; commit and hash the schedule-aware threshold analysis before inspecting the extension's outcomes. Benchmark checkpoint size, reconstruction time and run throughput before starting this budget. A pilot assay or suggestive effect cannot turn the earlier result confirmatory. Any new confirmatory competition or heredity test needs a separately frozen protocol, independently reserved histories and a power calculation based on world-level variance.

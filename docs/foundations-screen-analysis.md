# Complete exploratory screen summary

`tools/foundation-screen-analysis.ts` is a CPU-only reader of the frozen 7,200-proposal plan and disjoint execution chunks. Supply every chunk with a repeated `--report` argument, plus `--plan` and a **new** `--out` path. It refuses to write a complete summary unless all 113 planned batches and 7,200 proposal rows are present. Each chunk must have finished its own declared contiguous batch slice, with successful first-local-batch exact-parent repeat controls. The reader rebuilds the deterministic plan, checks its saved selected-source file hashes against this checkout, and reuses validation's proposal, seed, tile, genotype and outcome-identity checks. The report records input byte hashes and its own analysis-code hashes.

The output gives overall, per-founder, per-scale and founder-by-scale counts. Every unchanged-after-clamp proposal and duplicate resulting genome remains in the raw denominator; separate unique-genotype exposure counts show how many distinct mutant genomes those proposals reached. Survival, recovery, regeneration and light-dependence pass and paired loss/gain counts use the raw one-replicate assays. Behavior summaries report any Evaluation or saved trace-summary difference, paired fixed-role available and changed frames, and assays without paired role evidence.

These are descriptive conditional assay counts. Proposals share 12 fixed founder backgrounds, a batch seed and tile coordinates, and repeated genotypes can occur in different assay positions. Unique-genotype counts are not independent outcome trials. No binomial confidence interval, mutation-benefit frequency, natural population frequency, novel function or inherited life-cycle conclusion follows from this screen. Independent validation remains governed by its prespecified fresh 32 seeds.

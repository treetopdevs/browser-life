# Serial transfer v2: prospective feasibility protocol

Status: **design draft, blocked on A1 natural diagnostic and seed audit; no v2 garden has run**. This is a new exploratory stream. It does not amend the frozen serial-v1 plan or reinterpret its completed outcomes. The source-1 donor remains the previously known late catalog component 53, so this stream is informed feasibility, not blind replication.

## Entry gates

1. A1 must validate neutral genome-copy colors against actual lottery winners, per-step physical/flux parity, full-genome/ledger parity at fixed checkpoints, and old serial-v1 terminal physics plus all six original observer hashes. A cap or failure leaves A2 pending or blocked; it is not a negative biological observation. Genetic copy origin from the initial inoculum is **not** an immediate organism parent.
2. Authenticate the source/cache/catalog/rule and exact selected packet again; bind all code, protocol and output identities in an immutable plan before GPU execution. Preserve all attempted runs and no-event states. Reserve new noncolliding seeds from the proposed `640020001–640029999` range only after Luna's seed audit; use one shared seed for the four stage-0 arms and one shared seed for each of stages 1 and 2. Never replace a seed after seeing an outcome.
3. Use source-1 component 53's intact packet as the common physical template. Stage-0 arms are evolved genotype, M3 founder-9 genotype on the **identical** physical packet, zero-controller founder-9 genotype on the identical packet, and an empty garden. Exactly one eligible root in each inoculated arm, none in empty, must be proved with the actual step-0 census. The founder arm is a genotype intervention on evolved morphology, not a natural founder-form comparison; empty is a background control, not resource matched. The authenticated CPU preflight already passes, but v2 must recheck it against the final code/input freeze.
4. Maintain mutation off, exact packet extraction/reinsertion shams, all seven cell channels and 44 genome words, toroidal mapping, source/destination resource exports, conservation, and an unsampled terminal-physics reference. Any mismatch stops the affected stage and records an uninterpretable technical failure.

## Fixed recruitment and transfer

Keep the original 25-step census and an A1-validated continuous copy/material diagnostic. Examine all components at steps 25 through 1000. A candidate must be a newly detected physically separate packet under a validated geometry/continuity certificate, have at least 256 bound mass, have a single-genotype original packet, and have complete, uncontaminated genome-copy linkage to the initial inoculum from placement through selection. A new tracker ID or same genotype alone is insufficient. An adjacent-step one-parent-to-two-component event is a **separate stronger diagnostic**, not a universal recruitment gate: diffuse copy-linked descendants may cross the component threshold without such an event. The source root's continued tracker identity is recorded but is **not** a universal biological requirement; root death, replacement or observer-ID change cannot retrospectively veto a valid packet. If the assay cannot distinguish translation or mixing from a newly separate packet, mark that candidate ambiguous rather than recruiting it. The exact geometry/copy certificate and deterministic hash tie rule over source/arm/stage/step/component must be committed before any v2 outcome. Exact extraction/packet validation follows candidate selection; its failure does not permit a replacement.

Choose the first eligible census, then the lowest frozen hash at that census. Retain every inspected candidate and rejection, including ambiguous, subeligible, mixed, invalid-packet and no-event cases. If extraction of the chosen packet fails or its source copy later dies/fuses, retain that outcome; do not choose the next candidate. Transfer the exact selected packet immediately into a fresh matched garden, then finish observing the source garden to 3000. Only donor and founder arms continue to stages 1 and 2, each on its own selected packet; zero-controller and empty stop after stage 0. A missing packet makes the downstream arm **unavailable**, not extinct. At most eight gardens constitute the initial stream. A later two-stream/five-source extension requires a new freeze and is not automatically triggered by a favorable pilot.

The current pure implementation uses a deliberately conservative geometry certificate: one previously detected, continuously copy-linked component must become at least two such components at the next 25-step census, with a unique incumbent established by same-site overlap. Other linked components at that census are candidate packets. Tied or zero overlap, a multi-component prior census, and uncovered copy labels remain ambiguous or unavailable. This may miss real propagation when structures translate, overlap, or cross the census threshold in a more complex way; it does not turn those cases into biological failures. The certificate does not require the old root's observer ID to persist and does not claim to identify an immediate genetic parent. The independently measured adjacent-step parent split is reported separately when available. The execution runner must verify the complete physical/color parity transcript and bind the frame digests; a caller-supplied status alone is not authenticated evidence.

## Measurements and claim limits

Report per arm and stage the complete 25/100-step observer traces, all candidate/rejection counts, copy-origin status, old-material transport shares where available, overlap/fusion/mixing, exact incoming packet and outgoing packet inventories, source-garden fate after copying, new-garden persistence and final B/P, full material/energy ledgers and conservation. Keep time since placement, time since detected separation and unknown biological birth age separate.

The garden-wide lower bound `max(0, final garden B+P − total initial inoculum matter)` shows some ambient matter entered bound structure **somewhere** in the garden. It does not show that the selected packet contains it. A conservative packet bound is `max(0, selected packet B+P − total initial inoculum matter)`; if zero and no validated spatial material trace exists, packet-level incorporation is unavailable. Pre-reaction source shares alone do not assign ownership of material after mixed reaction pools. Report repeated physical packet transfer/persistence separately from fresh-resource incorporation.

For this initial v2 pilot, **organization reconstruction is unavailable by design**: no calibrated informative non-mass trait, tolerance, and attribution to newly built packet structure have been frozen. Record incoming and selected outgoing packet inventories, their conservative bound-matter lower bound, and the original census morphology separately. A pattern merely carried in the intact packet is persistence, not reconstruction. A zero packet lower bound does not prove zero incorporation. Any later reconstruction endpoint needs a separately dated protocol and controls; this pilot cannot substitute mass, genotype identity, or fission count. One clonal source stream cannot estimate heritability or a reproduction probability. Time since garden placement, time since detected separation, and unknown biological birth age are distinct fields in each outcome.

## Cost and stops

First v2 execution is one stage-0 batch under a 600-second cap, after a plan-only review and the A1 gate. Run the four arms in fixed order, preserving completed rows if a cap interrupts later arms. Continue stages 1 and 2 only after stage evidence and source/code revalidation, with a new bounded invocation for each. Count failures and overhead against the track-A 1.5-hour allocation. Stop on source drift, missing exact shams, physical/ledger parity failure, output collision, device/cap failure or incomplete copy coverage. Keep technical incompleteness distinct from a completed biological extinction or no-event observation. No M4 confirmation seed or old serial-v1 artifact is touched.

## Executable preparation and evidence status

The fixed local seed suballocation is `640020101`, `640020102`, `640020103`, shared across arms within stages 0, 1, 2 respectively. The exact allocation row remains reserved under `A2 serial exact-stage seed suballocation`; the earlier broad local audit remains unchanged. A started, failed, or capped reservation blocks another fresh stream using that seed. There is no automatic retry; any same-input technical recovery requires a separately reviewed protocol and preserves the original receipt. The common allocation ledger points to these receipt paths as execution evidence rather than changing its bound allocation row. Stage 0 uses `tools/foundation-serial-transfer-v2.ts` with the verified A1 directory, seed-audit and seed-ledger paths, a create-new output directory and `--max-seconds` at most 600. Stages 1 and 2 use `tools/foundation-serial-v2-continuation.ts`, with one or two ordered prior output directories and verified complete seed-use receipts. Each stage gets a new create-new plan/result pair and at most two donor/founder gardens. A missing prior fixed packet is recorded as unavailable and no replacement is allowed. The earlier stage-0 plan-v1 was CPU-only and is superseded before GPU by the corrected source-closure and certificate protocol.

The runner checks the original source/cache/catalog, A1 result, code closure and exact seed allocation before execution and after the batch. Each inoculated garden has a 25-step extraction/reinsertion sham, colored/uncolored per-step physical parity through the 1000-step recruitment window, an original-runner trace through 3000, exact selected-packet extraction replay at the original checkpoint, mutation-off/conservation checks, and a separate unsampled 3000-step physics reference. The empty garden has the original-runner and unsampled-reference checks, with no copy-color source to recruit. The 25-step and 100-step observations, candidate/rejection rows and source-garden observer continuity are descriptive; observer continuity does not prove genetic or material ancestry. Runner manifests preserve actual adapter, cap/overrun, partial rows and technical failure. A live-process guard stops this runner if another known browser-life GPU assay starts; it does not stop the other process.

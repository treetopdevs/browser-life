schematic · no data Complexity rather than population: three views proposed in design §5

Wireframes only. None of the proposed measures has been computed; computing them waits for a dated decision that fixes the definition and expected directions.

1 · Traitgram

Ancestry as a path in time × complexity, dead siblings as stubs, the population's cell-weighted band behind it, the neutral twin beside it. Reads as "climbed" or "wandered".

2 · Selection skew

Δc of every minted mutation against the ones that spread. The gap is selection's push; doubling births leaves it unchanged. Neutral should show no gap.

3 · Decoupling

One dot per run (shape and colour by condition). Population can grow along x without rising in y; only rise beyond the neutral and no-mutation clouds counts.

# Prospective serial-transfer feasibility pilot

This protocol is for a **future, bounded pilot**. It asks whether a newly separated fragment can maintain itself, produce another eligible fragment, and repeat that process after two transfers into fresh gardens. It does not turn split counts, growth, polymer, or two transfers into proof of an inherited life cycle or reconstructed organization. The source-1 garden and lifecycle pilots have already been inspected; their outcomes are prior exploratory information. Candidate and outcome rules below must be frozen and hashed before any new `640010001+` assay is run.

Applying the **existing** `rankCatalogSelection("m4/gradient-m3/treatment/seed-1", "late", ruleSha, catalogTime)` gives rank 0 to component **53** (selection key `33b428eef552a04c4a6e9afa4debb48e2fd1e1d3184840c117cb8db56f6e3015`). This fixes the late donor by catalog identity and ranking, not by its garden outcome, polymer result, or subsequent offspring. The catalog's purity and size filters still condition this choice; no population-frequency claim follows. If the source, rule, catalog, checkpoint, rank, packet, or code identity differs, record the donor as unavailable and stop this source's pilot rather than substituting another component.

The comparison arm starts from M3 **founder index 9** in its own standardized garden and follows the same recruitment and transfer rule. Their intact inocula need not have equal cell count, shape or stored resources. Record those inventories and keep raw between-arm outcomes descriptive unless a later matched-inventory preparation is validated. Do not use the old `630...` garden seeds as fresh life-cycle seeds.

Use one paired pilot stream: cycle-0 donor, founder and zero-controller use seed `640010001`; donor and founder use `640010002` for transfer 1 and `640010003` for transfer 2. Reusing a seed across the parallel arms deliberately matches counter-RNG coordinates; the three cycle seeds are distinct. **Only this stream is planned.** Keep `640010011..640010013` unused; any decision made after seeing this pilot to run those seeds is a separately documented exploratory follow-up, not a prespecified replication to pool with it. These are nested gardens from **one** original source history, not independent source-world replicates. Each garden has a 3,000-step horizon and the same mutation-disabled, uniform-light garden overrides as the completed source-1 transplant pilot; a new assay-side spec builder must accept the reserved `640...` seeds without altering that pilot's existing seed contract.

## Recruitment and transfer rule

Observe each physical garden at 25-step censuses for recruitment, with a separate 100-step tracker/readout calculated from the same physical snapshots. Seed both assay-side trackers from the actual step-0 inoculum, using bound-mass threshold **48** and minimum component mass **256**. Require exactly one eligible imported root; otherwise retain an unavailable branch. These trackers differ from the runner observer initialized at its first scheduled census; preserve that distinction and do not compare their numeric IDs directly. The 100-step view is observer sensitivity, not another biological history. Restrict recruitment to steps 25–1,000 of each 3,000-step garden; continue observing to step 3,000 whether or not recruitment succeeds. A candidate is an **additional direct child of the imported root** in an emitted fission event, not the largest-overlap fragment that retains the root ID, and not an arbitrary later descendant. At the event census, its component must be physically separate, at or above mass 256, and unmixed by the independent **same-cell** previous-ID overlap check. This detector cannot rule out unobserved motion or exchanges between censuses. Reject a same-census logged fusion, prior observed mixing/fusion of the root through the link, and any missing label-to-ID join. Budding and unmatched birth labels are recorded but do not establish the required link. Record every detected candidate with its acceptance or explicit rejection reason.

Choose the **first census with a topology-eligible direct child**. If several qualify there, choose one by SHA-256 of the salt `foundation-serial-transfer-v1`, source/arm/cycle/seed, step, Tracker child ID and component index. Then attempt exact member-cell extraction from that census's full physics state immediately. Impure lineage/genotype, invalid packet or transplant failure stops that branch without trying the next-ranked child; these failures are not pre-selection exclusions. Do **not** wait one more census to see whether it persists, grows, or reproduces; that would select on the outcome. The parent garden continues as its own observed trajectory. Transfer a copy of the selected packet into a fresh matched garden with an explicit lineage-ID map and the existing `transplantCellPacket` displacement/export audit. Apply the same rule in transfer-1 garden to choose transfer 2. There is no third transfer, but record whether the transfer-2 root produces a topology-eligible, valid child packet. A root that dies, fuses or acquires observed overlap mixing **before recruitment** cannot recruit afterward. Once a child is selected, later events in that source garden do not veto its already selected transferred copy; the next garden has its own root and recruitment outcome. A cycle with no eligible event or failed selected-packet preparation stops its branch without replacement. Every garden already launched is observed to its horizon unless a technical or budget failure prevents it.

The existing `extractCellPacket` accepts a pure single-lineage, single-genotype component. A mixed collective may be biologically relevant, but this pilot cannot transfer it intact with that API. Count mixed and packet-ineligible events and preserve their images/label hashes and inventories where available. A future multi-lineage packet protocol would be a separate intervention. Track identities as `(source arm, cycle, garden ID, Tracker ID, packet SHA-256)` across transfers: a freshly constructed garden reuses local observer IDs, and a relabeled genome address is not an organism pedigree.

## Measurements and mechanical gates

For every cycle and arm, report initial packet and exported-cell inventories (all seven cell channels, packed genome, matter and energy), placement mapping, before/after state hashes, source/cache/catalog digests, rule/schema/metrics versions, garden spec and seed, and selected code hashes before and after. Recheck the source replay proof and exact source checkpoint/packet identity before execution. A same-state extraction/reinsertion sham must reproduce physics and observation hashes, with exact conservation. The 25-step observed continuation must equal an unsampled replay's terminal physics hash; a dropped mutation ledger, failed conservation or mismatched source identity blocks interpretation. Reserve new paths before GPU acquisition; cap a first bounded invocation at 600 seconds and retain partial, failed, censored and unavailable reports. No complete label may be emitted for an over-budget run.

At fixed times 100, 200 and 500 after placement or observed fragment selection, retain exact component-label membership ranges and trajectories of mass, biomass, polymer fraction, shape/spatial organization and local resource covariates. Movement is unavailable until a separate validated measure is added. Measure the same elapsed times for founder-arm fragments where available, keeping time since placement, observer-identity age and unknown biological birth age distinct. Record initial imported material separately from subsequent changes; bound-mass gain alone can reflect redistribution of garden matter. Retain the full 120 original 25-step runner census rows, with per-channel pools and interval reaction-flux rates, plus exact initial/final channel inventories. Since the bare garden contributes only A, report `max(0, final(B+P) - initial inoculum matter)` as a conservative lower bound on ambient matter incorporated into bound structure; this is descriptive resource accounting, not a life or organization success flag. Runner activity and deep morphology fields retain their actual observation cadence and should not be imputed where absent. Report age-at-death, fusion, independent overlap mixing, next eligible fission and right censoring. The 100-step sensitivity view reports event counts and label-based selected-child correspondence at the first shared 100-step census, including absent and ambiguous states; it cannot establish visibility of the entire 25-step event trajectory. Report all arms and cycles in denominators, including no fragment, no packet, failed transfer and observation end.

The smallest **self-maintenance feasibility** contrast is the prespecified donor-versus-founder paired stream, with zero-controller at cycle 0: does each newly transferred fragment remain active and produce another clean transferable fragment within the horizon, and does that recur after the second fresh-pool transfer? These are descriptive one-source outcomes. De novo polymer or a rim/core compartment is an **optional measured trait**, never a prerequisite for self-maintenance or organization. If no non-mass spatial/functional trait is measurable, report that organization reconstruction is unavailable rather than substituting mass or split counts.

A negative pilot means failure to satisfy this source selection, observer, topology, purity, transfer and horizon rule. It is not evidence that the whole substrate lacks life, self-maintenance capacity or a viable alternative transmission pathway. Failed mechanical controls make the affected biological outcome uninterpretable rather than negative.

The smallest future **organization** contrast needs one additional audited intervention: transfer the same selected genotype and aggregate per-channel inventory in (a) its intact new fragment and (b) a predeclared spatially disrupted packet into paired fresh gardens, preserving total matter, energy, genome words and placement envelope. Compare a fixed, detector-validated spatial or functional trajectory at matched ages and resources and the ability to yield the next clean transferable fragment. The present packet API can translate an intact packet and construct genotype-only arms on a fixed template; it **cannot yet certify** that spatial disruption preserves these inventories. That pure preparation and its sham/negative controls must be built and tested before this contrast is executable. If intact descendants outperform a valid inventory-matched disrupted arm and regain the same predeclared organization after another transfer, that supports structural transmission under these assay conditions. If both recover, genetic/developmental reconstruction remains plausible. If neither does, transfer injury or assay sensitivity must be checked before any substrate conclusion. Even a positive one-source result requires independent source worlds and a frozen effect criterion for inference.

## Execution use of the bounded runner

Run `tools/foundation-serial-transfer.ts` from the foundations checkout with Deno read/write access. The default mode is a CPU-only authenticated plan. It requires explicit `--source`, `--cache`, `--catalog`, `--rule`, a **new** `--out` directory, and `--stage 0`, `1`, or `2`. Stage 1 takes `--prior-manifests path/to/stage-0/manifest.json`; stage 2 takes the ordered, comma-separated stage-0 and stage-1 manifest paths. Each plan recomputes the frozen rank-0 late donor from the catalog, checks replay and original source files, hashes selected code and this protocol, records complete initial packet/resource inventories and exact pure restore, then writes an immutable `plan.json` alongside a current `manifest.json`. Paths already in use are rejected before a GPU is acquired. A plan is not a garden outcome.

Execution adds `--execute --max-seconds N` with finite positive `N <= 600`. Stage 0 runs exactly the donor, founder, and zero-controller gardens; later stages run each available donor/founder branch, at most two gardens per invocation. A branch with no selected valid packet stays explicitly unavailable, while other arms retain their fixed stage seed. A previous stage is reusable only after completion, all mechanical checks, and post-execution source/code revalidation; its `plan.json` and `manifest.json` are read and checked again. The runner does not use reserved `640010011..013` seeds.

For each measured garden, the runner first compares a 25-step same-state extraction/reinsertion continuation on both terminal physics and all six observation-file hashes. The full 3,000-step garden uses a 25-step runner observer plus assay-side 25/100 trackers seeded from step 0. Every 25-step encoded checkpoint feeds the assay-side capture. A separate fresh GPU simulation of the identical initial state advances without observer readback to the terminal step; its physics hash must match the measured garden. Conservation, zero mutation events, zero dropped events, initial and selected packets, all 120 original series rows, exact final pools, the bound-incorporation lower bound, all branch failures, cross-garden observer-child provenance, and actual GPU adapter information are retained. The 100-step sensitivity view uses the measured states and is not a second physical run.

Budget expiry or mechanical mismatch leaves a failed or partial artifact and bars use as prior-stage evidence. Completion requires a final source/cache/code/protocol recheck and a within-cap publication. The capture's morphology fields describe fixed-age structure and local covariates; movement is unavailable until a separate validated measure is added, and observer-identity age is not biological age. A valid child packet can be copied to the next garden even if its source-world sibling later dies or fuses. The output is a one-source feasibility record, with no confirmatory heredity or organization claim.

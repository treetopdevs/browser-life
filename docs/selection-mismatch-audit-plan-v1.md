Every stage records source evidence, evaluation counts, all failed criteria, advancement and evidence strength. Classify regeneration-only failure, other/multiple measured failures, non-advancement with known or unresolved reason, assessed eligibility followed by ranking loss, pooled-gate failure, inclusion, and missing/conflicting evidence. A missing assay never implies a failed assay.

Retest admission and final ties are order-sensitive. Record eligibility, admission position, compatible admission limits, the selected competitor and the decisive field or tie-break. `perStrong` is not stored in the historical retest; the default of four is not historical proof. Reconstruct compatible settings from the full observed roster. If different compatible explanations affect a conclusion, retain the ambiguity. Code-based explanations without historical version proof are labelled consistent with the pinned reconstruction.

Reconcile the complete admission, selected-candidate and replication rosters, and reproduce all 12 final genomes, order and founder-set identity. Any mismatch blocks a definitive funnel explanation and must be reported, not repaired by altering historical data. The seven were selected by a later exploratory outcome: no estimate of population-wide evolvability loss or causal effect of a quality-score weight follows.

## Gated roadmap only

**A — Operational unit.** A separate protocol defines the selected unit and viable continuation/reproduction, with validated controls. The material-observer limitation remains in force.

**B — Surrogate validation.** Measure comparable neighborhoods for additional candidates; partition development and validation by genetic cluster and disjoint seeds. Freeze features, score, acceptance and stopping criteria before validation outcomes; audit M5–M7 endpoint/proxy overlap. The original 12-founder Test 2 dataset cannot validate a 36-genome predictor.

**C — Founder policies.** Only after B, compare minimum-viability cluster-balanced random selection with validated accessibility selection from the same eligible pool. Initially record regeneration without ranking it. Repeat cohort sampling. The old founder cohort is a fixed historical reference, not a randomized third arm. Report inherited archive selection bias; archive-conditioned success does not establish unrestricted evolutionary capacity.

**D — Ecology.** Separately test co-culture with matched resource/partner controls, then seasonal environments with founder policy fixed. Do not jointly change selection, environment and physics. Mutation-rate loci, duplication, predation and other rule changes stay deferred; progenote remains an analogy.

The historical Variation verdict is preserved and does not select the next mechanism. This audit launches none of A–D.

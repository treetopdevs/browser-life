## Critique of the proposed reasoning

1. **Missing mechanisms: confirmed, with narrower language.** `docs/plan.md:32` promises duplication into silent slots and modular per-reaction blocks. `packages/schema/src/layout.ts:15` fixes a 10–8–8 network with 160 weights/biases. `packages/sim-ref/src/step.ts:645` and the GPU `mutate` function choose one of 163 existing loci. They implement point changes, not duplication or an explicit module/silent-slot representation. Functional modularity or unused weights could still emerge; absence of the promised operator does not prove the existing network cannot evolve useful complexity.
2. **Mutation magnitude/rate: qualified.** Defaults are `mutStep=24`, `mutRate=429497` (`packages/schema/src/config.ts:285`). The rate coefficient is approximately 10^-4 per synthesized quantum, implemented as a capped per-cell-step event threshold, not independent Bernoulli mutation of each quantum. Sigma changes use a scaled delta; all loci clamp, and a proposed nonzero change can leave the genotype unchanged. The simulation still mints a new lineage ID after such an event (`step.ts:512`). Record attempted mutation, changed genotype, changed phenotype and new lineage ID separately. Do not infer functional mutation severity from 24/127 alone. The proposal is also asymmetric: zero delta becomes +1, so at `mutStep=1` two of the three possible delta residues yield +1 and one yields −1. Sigma uses signed right-shift rounding. Comparing 1/4/24 therefore changes the distribution's direction and discretization as well as its magnitude; retain the rule-faithful screen and do not describe it as a purely symmetric mutation-size experiment.
3. **Role-count ceiling: confirmed; impossibility claim refuted.** `packages/metrics/src/ecology.ts:25` has four labels, including mixed. This bounds the statistic and limits long-term sensitivity. It does not prevent a finite positive trend, especially from a mean around three. The claim that every run has zero compartments is false: four gradient neutral summaries have compartment means of 0.002; 166/170 summaries are zero. All treatment summaries are zero; those zeros establish non-detection in that arm under this observer, not physical impossibility. The held-out negative stands; it was not mathematically forced solely by those facts. Validate detector sensitivity before attributing absence to either physics or measurement.
4. **Founder selection: corrected.** Size and speed are MAP-Elites archive descriptors (`packages/search/src/mapelites.ts:57`), not monotonic rewards. Quality combines survival, recovery, regeneration and light dependence (`evaluate.ts:79`); final founder choice is further conditioned on strict retests (`retest.ts:53`). Evolvability was not evaluated. Founder selection may bias accessible variation, but this is a hypothesis, not an established cause of failure.
5. **Sibling resemblance: limited evidence.** The registered statistic correlates sibling mu/sigma values, not functional reconstruction across generations (`experiments/preregistration.md:162`). Exact condition summaries belong in `foundations-evidence.md`. High resemblance can arise mechanically through shared genomes and spatial ancestry, including in neutral/no-mutation controls. Raw parent–offspring morphology regression also cannot by itself establish genetic heritability or major transitions.
6. **Turnover without accumulation: unresolved.** Existing activity and held-out summaries support turnover and failure to establish the specified organization claim. They do not exclude unmeasured functional gains, episodic gains, or niche-specific gains. A later competitor winning supports fitness improvement in the tested assays, not necessarily increasing complexity; home advantage supports temporal specialization, not uniquely Red Queen coevolution; a nonsignificant result is inconclusive unless an equivalence bound is resolved with adequate precision.
7. **Duplication does not necessarily require a new layout.** It could copy within an existing representation. A change to actual mutation dynamics requires a rule-version change and deliberate golden-hash updates; a changed serialization requires schema handling as well. New scientific distributions need distinct preset/provenance identities and calibration. Existing analysis cannot silently reuse old thresholds. Code-only observational utilities do not require a physics bump.
8. **Replay cost is conditional.** Bundles support optional full-state checkpoints. All 170 local original M4 manifests were inspected: every one uses `checkpointEvery:0` and has no checkpoints, so sampled original histories require reconstruction. Gradient seed 1 reports 1,383 seconds for 10^6 steps on its original host; there is no universal 17-minute replay cost. Recover a history once, cache all planned sample times, and reuse validated snapshots for many fresh assay replicates.
9. **Positive controls can be constructed or searched.** Both establish feasibility only; neither demonstrates spontaneous origin. Search targets and descriptors must be distinct from the final held-out outcome family. Failed construction or a budget-limited search is not impossibility evidence. Start with an individual life cycle; a reproducing collective is a later experiment contingent on demonstrated cohesion and transmission.
10. **Individuality document checked.** Its documented small run reports no Holm-adjusted test at or below 0.05; best reported adjusted p is 0.081. Candidate colonies also lack enough eligible worlds for calibrated intervals. This is limited, inconclusive evidence about higher-level individuality, not evidence that individuality cannot exist (`docs/individuality-info-theory.md:321`).
11. **Time-shift competition does not directly show that an innovation opens the next opportunity.** A historical fitness contrast can occur inside an unchanged repertoire of functions. Testing opportunity creation requires an intervention on the proposed ecological change and a separately measured response: for example, whether a recipient genotype gains viability in producer-conditioned chemistry, loses that benefit when the proposed product is removed, and regains it after restoration under matched resource accounting. This is a candidate follow-up after identifying a mechanism, not an assay already implemented or a result that can be inferred from later lineages winning.
12. **Selection among founders is a separate explanation.** The initial world contains standing genetic variation. Early–late differences can reflect changing founder frequencies, even without new functional mutations. A no-mutation world still permits ecological sorting and selection; it is not a fixed-organism control. Where mutation ancestry is complete, retain founder origin and compare reconstructed genotypes with their ancestral founder under matched conditions. A deeper mutation pedigree alone is not a larger functional repertoire.
13. **The selected unit may differ from the desired reproducing unit.** Genomes replicate with local synthesized biomass and compete through the mixing lottery. Whether those dynamics reward reliable whole-individual or collective reproduction is an empirical question. Before attributing failure to inadequate mutation, test whether individually successful propagules also win the local competition that the world actually supplies. A mismatch would motivate an experiment on transmission and ecological selection, rather than automatically adding genome capacity. This is a hypothesis, not an established cause of the M4 result.
14. **A measured structural trait may lack a net advantage in the tested ecology.** Building polymer consumes biomass and energy, and polymer slows both nutrient and waste diffusion; registered M4 adhesion is off (`packages/sim-ref/src/step.ts:341`, `step.ts:482`, and `docs/plan.md:77`). Polymer still changes the bound-mass field and material retention, so its net effect is unresolved. Scarcity of polymer-rich organization could reflect ecological tradeoffs as well as measurement, accessibility or transmission limits. Do not equate a membrane-specific negative with the absence of useful organization. A targeted cost/benefit intervention is a possible follow-up, conditional on the present assays, not another run launched by this plan.
15. **Treatment-only time shifts do not isolate selection from mutation bias or drift.** A later genotype's advantage establishes higher performance in the stated assay and is compatible with adaptation. Its comparison with its actual founder identifies a genotype-dependent difference under matched starting conditions, but neither comparison alone shows that natural selection caused the historical change. The implemented mutation proposal is directionally asymmetric, and founder search did not maximize this garden's final biomass. A future matched assay of evolved neutral genotypes, expressed under the same treatment evaluator, would help distinguish selected change from proposal bias/drift. Neutral-source replay under its original neutral controller would be a different test and would mask those genotypes' controller effects. This is a prospective causal control, not an omitted member of the currently fixed five-treatment-history feasibility matrix.
16. **A founder neighborhood is not an equal-viability founder comparison.** The twelve existing founders have different backgrounds and assay outcomes. Passing the same viability threshold does not make their viability equal. The screen and independent validation can characterize accessible changes conditional on each founder; they do not by themselves identify selection for evolvability or establish that otherwise matched founders differ in their capacity to innovate. Such a comparison needs an explicitly matched founder design and a functional outcome, beyond current size, speed and fixed-role changes.

## Decisions now

- Hold the fresh confirmatory M4 amendment ensemble until its dated endpoint, calibration and required gate family are frozen. The completed foundations investigation supports retaining current physics; organizational transmission can be studied alongside amendment design. Do not reinterpret the old ensemble as a new confirmation or require M7 success before testing the stated M4 claim.
- Keep current CPU/GPU physics, versions, founders, golden pins, frozen endpoints, threshold tables, and preregistration unchanged in this first slice.
- Use a source history as the independent evolutionary unit. Mutants, organisms, branches, repeated assays, device replays and census rows are nested observations.
- Record null, failed, extinct, clamped, missing and censored outcomes. Never turn missing data into zero or omit unsuccessful source histories.
- Use rule-faithful mutation proposals first. Smaller step sizes are deliberate perturbation conditions, not new natural dynamics.
- Do not start the 10^7 extension automatically. Its registered design is 15 extension histories (three conditions, seeds 101–105) plus 10 neutral-pilot histories (1101–1110): 250 million steps, not a free background run. At 1,000 steps/s that is about 69.4 GPU-hours; actual timing, storage and observer overhead must be measured. The protocol also requires schedule-aware threshold selection, its own pilot, and an analysis-code hash recorded before extension analysis. See `experiments/preregistration.md:155`. Even 10^7 steps need not reveal a plateau.

## A. Mutation neighborhood

Start with deterministic proposals from each of the 12 founders at step scales 1, 4 and 24. Retain exact parent genomes, proposed draws, locus, effective delta, clamping/no-change and duplicate identities. A few hundred proposals per founder is an eventual sampling budget, not proof of exhaustive coverage of 163 loci or all possible mutations. Report coverage and avoid counting duplicates as independent biological variants.

Evaluate unmutated parent and mutant at identical replicate seeds, initial matter/energy, tile position, environment and observation schedule, in separate matched worlds. Assay mutation is off to estimate the proposed genotype's effect. Position matching matters because the counter PRNG depends on cell coordinates. Repeating identical inputs tests determinism; evaluating founders across fresh assay seeds measures variation across those conditions. A parent result can be cached only under an exact config/seed/position identity.

The current batch evaluator measures survival, population recovery, individual regeneration, light dependence, mass, speed and detected reproduction. The new optional trace adds growth-census mass, membrane and fixed-role trajectories; it does not establish functional organization. Size/speed changes are candidate phenotypic differences, not automatically useful innovations.

In a clonal evaluator tile with mutation off, the lineage-level role classifier has only one lineage to classify: effective role diversity is consequently one whenever role evidence is available. It cannot measure ecological coexistence in that assay. Preserve all four role shares and their time-dependent differences to detect switching; test complementary ecological roles in a separate community assay.

Stage the expense: planning → small paired pilot → broad low-rep screening → independently replicated candidate classes. A 32-replicate probability gate belongs at validation, not thousands of initial candidates. Keep screening selection and validation seeds separate. Report founder-relative and absolute viability, and distinguish improved performance, preserved performance with a new behavior, degradation, and indeterminate effects.

## B. Time-shift assay

Begin with five source treatment histories selected by a fixed seed list before examining competitive outcomes, sampling 100k, 500k and 900k. This is feasibility, not a confirmatory sample size. Sampling one dominant lineage per time can miss rare innovations; record a predeclared abundance-stratified sample and extinction/unavailability. Compare both reconstructed genomes in a common inoculum and intact propagules when feasible, because resetting morphology can erase developmental inheritance.

Recover all times in one replay whose relevant source files remain fixed. Validate the replay against the original final hash before using intermediate cached states as authenticated reconstructions. Include rule/schema/metrics/preset/source identities and distinguish observer replay from physics replay. **Correction on 2026-09-28:** the frozen runner at `b7fd4c1` defines `summary.finalHash` as `artifactDigest(final, observer)`, covering both physics and observer state; our earlier physics-only description was wrong. Those runner, observer, tracker and accounting source files are unchanged between that freeze and this workspace's base. Record the separate physics hash and compare saved observation files as well. A fresh assay seed is recorded as an intentional intervention after reconstruction; it is not a continuation proof.

Compare early/late organisms in early-conditioned, late-conditioned and standardized common-garden environments. Separate abiotic chemistry from live resident communities: keeping residents tests a different interaction and requires its own arm. Match starting material and stored energy, use sham extraction/transplant controls, reciprocal placements, and both equal-frequency competition and reciprocal rare invasion if resources permit. Define identity tracking before competition; arbitrary lineage relabeling must not alter physics or lose descendants.

Estimate per-world fitness contrasts and the organism-time × environment-time interaction, then aggregate across worlds. Later advantage indicates relative adaptation under those assay conditions. Home advantage indicates temporal specialization. A Red Queen interpretation additionally requires evidence of reciprocal biotic change over multiple times and controls for abiotic drift. None establishes new capabilities without a separate functional assay.

## C. Life-cycle transmission

Follow at least three linked generations where observable. Record birth, split, fusion, death and observation end; mark ambiguous ancestry and right-censored next-fission time instead of assigning zero or excluding failures. Reproduction requires descendant viability and subsequent reproduction, not just a split detection.

Measure mass and membrane organization at matched developmental ages and resource states, plus time-to-reproduction with survival/censoring handling. Compare related and unrelated matched propagules in randomized common gardens and reciprocal environments. Separate clonal/genetic transmission, transmitted structure, and shared environmental effects. A multigeneration resemblance association is not narrow-sense genetic heritability in this asexual, mixing system.

Audit tracking at more than one census cadence and component threshold on a small replay subset. A lineage may be physically continuous while the observer splits it, or vice versa. Calibrate the observer with known split/fusion/transport examples before using its event counts as a biological endpoint. Build on the existing individuality and anticipation tracks without silently promoting their exploratory measures to confirmed outcomes.

These are proposed caps, not permission to consume cloud resources or launch all jobs now. Measure actual throughput and data growth before each stage; stop at the cap. No claim of completion depends on elapsed calendar time alone.

Provisional *engineering* gates: identical-parent assay controls reproduce exactly with identical inputs; every sample has explicit source identity and resource accounting; synthetic detector controls include both present and absent organization; all tracked outcomes report eligible/excluded/censored denominators. These gates are mandatory before biological interpretation.

Provisional *scientific* decision margins, to be revised on calibration data and frozen before validation: parent-relative viability loss no greater than 10 percentage points; competition fitness ratio at least 1.10 across the selected gardens to prioritize a general-adaptation candidate; absence claims require the interval wholly within a declared equivalence region (initial candidate 1/1.10 to 1.10). These margins are prioritization choices, not previously justified biological constants or M4 success gates. Fit the sample size to the desired interval width; five histories may be insufficient. Apply the existing 32-replicate rule when invoking a registered per-seed probability gate. Do not call a p-value alone a mechanism test.

| Evidence | Next decision |
|---|---|
| Functional transmission and accessible innovation are demonstrated, observer misses them | Repair/replace the measurement, validate against constructed cases, then register fresh confirmation |
| Feasible examples exist but sampled mutations/search seldom reach them | Compare mutation scales, representations and ecological opportunity, changing one mechanism at a time |
| Current substrate persistently fails prespecified feasibility probes while a comparator succeeds at matched effort | Prototype the smallest justified substrate change; do not claim impossibility of the original |
| Assays lack sensitivity, precision, provenance or budget | Inconclusive; name the missing evidence and the cheapest discriminating follow-up |

Only after that decision should an M4 amendment be finalized. A rule change becomes a separate scientific cohort with new provenance and calibration; old and new physics are never pooled. A substrate comparison can retain coordinator concepts, artifact interfaces and analysis methods, but its golden values, scientific calibration and any arithmetic assumptions must be revalidated.

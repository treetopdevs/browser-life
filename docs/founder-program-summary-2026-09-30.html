Founder program summary

browser-life, 2026-09-30. Covers M3, M4, the foundations review, and the ecology-first founder discovery (angles A, B, C, S5), with ranked next steps. All work behind it has finished: no GPU jobs are running and AWS is torn down.

1. Bottom line

M3 is met; M4 is not. The foundations review found that mutation makes viable variants but role change was rare from the M3 founders (1 of 12), and a diagnostic showed that this depends heavily on which genomes found the world (11 of 24 other genomes count). The ecology-first program tried to fix founders through ecology. Drifted clouds did not help (1 of 12, same as point founders). A producer-background search produced a large, diverse pool (1,476 confirmed genomes, 33 clusters) including 209 that cannot live alone in the standard medium, against 0 of 148 in the waste medium. But those 209 are effectively two independent groups (one cluster holds 144, all within 20 genome slots of one genome), and what they do is unknown. Founder sets with non-producers originate roles (S1 3 of 5, S5 5 of 5), but the same genomes do so alone, and most of what originates is non-producers gaining photosynthesis: one-shot niche filling, not evidence of open-ended radiation.

The entity and measurement questions (tracker parenthood unsupported; no eligible activity, novelty or role measure) still limit every claim. Most promising next step: find out what the obligates need and do, then test producer-plus-obligate worlds for consumer-resource dynamics, while a zero-cost recurrence readout on existing runs sets the bar.

What was actually established, and what was not

Established (under RULE_VERSION 1)Not established: measurement limits
  • M3's gate is met as specified, with conservation exact in every run.
  • Mutation produces viable, changed variants at about 13% per mutant.
  • Which genomes found a world changes how often roles originate: 10 of 12 non-phototroph genomes count, 1 of 12 phototrophs.
  • Competitive gains over time look like they keep accumulating (descriptive, both 10⁶ and 10⁷ horizons).
  • Reproducing entities. The tracker's parent links are not causal evidence; the local observer feasibility test did not pass. Every role-origination readout uses tracker births, so founder results inherit that doubt.
  • Measures. Test 6 found none of the four activity, novelty and role measures eligible (null checks flag 30 of 70 neutral runs for shadow excess; 40 of 70 for phenotype-bin novelty). Part of endpoint 1's margin is demographic.
  • Time-shift. The 10⁷ result is incomplete and its neutral control uninformative.
  • Why M4 fails. Unexplained: compartmentalised is 0 everywhere, and the detector was only checked on hand-built fixtures.

4. Honest cautions

Correction. A mid-run reading of "zero obligates" in the background medium was wrong. It was taken after 68 of 1,476 genomes had been re-screened (recorded in the plan's waste entry and in fa-waste.json as a projection). Obligates appeared late in the list: the first turned up at genome 92, there were 17 by 164, 48 by 324 and 209 at the end. An early zero said nothing, and the "a sample would decide the same" projection was a guess. That file still carries the stale note and was left untouched.
  • Exploratory and small. Five mutation runs per set or cloud, no registration. At that size a true rate of 0.5 and one of 0.2 are hard to tell apart, and the ecology readings were written up after the fact, although S5's reading rule was fixed before its runs.
  • The no-mutation controls already show several roles. Six of 24 diagnostic subjects, and the founder-2 cloud, S1, S2 and S5 sets do. Part of any "origination" is sorting of standing diversity. Leaving the six subjects out, 8 of 18 still count.
  • S1 and S5 do not add to the diagnostic. Their four genomes come from the non-phototroph archive stratum that was already counted, and each originates a role in 5 of 5 runs as a monoculture (subjects 0, 1, 3, 6). The sets' 3 of 5 and 5 of 5 therefore show that the genome class matters, not that the community does. S1 is effectively three lineages.
  • Most origination is one-shot niche filling. In the diagnostic, 82 of 107 different-role descendants are non-phototrophs gaining photosynthesis. Clouds and sets show the same: no set adds a role after step 100, and qualifying windows stay near 0.04–0.32 per 10⁵ steps. Nothing here shows recurring radiation.
  • The 209 obligates are about two lineages, and the dependence could be density (an Allee threshold), tile geometry, or the specific producer rather than anything a consumer would need. Roles are unknown.
  • Everything rests on the tracker. Role origination, first-window timing and windows per 10⁵ all use tracker lineages; the reset found tracker parenthood unsupported as causal evidence.
  • Separation rule. Role strata (phototroph or not, obligate or not) are a readout. They must never become a selection criterion for a future cohort, or M5 would be selected for. Any adopted screen is re-run under its own registered rule and seeds.

Success and kill thresholds below are proposals for discussion, not registered. Costs are estimates from the ledgers above.

1Follow the obligates: characterise, then found producer-plus-consumer worlds candidate a

What
Stage 1 (Mac GPU): the evaluation above for the 12 cluster representatives plus cluster 3's medoid and a few neighbours. Stage 2: set S6, founder 0 (the producer the obligates were screened on) plus one obligate representative per cluster, cluster 3 capped at one or two, on gradient-m3; 5 mutation and 3 no-mutation runs to 10⁶, with producer-only and obligate-only controls. Read consumer-resource dynamics (abundance lag and cycles) and whether new roles or lineages appear after the first fill.
Why
The only finding that yields genomes which need another lineage (209 of 1,476 against 0 of 148), and the nearest thing here to real interdependence, which M5 needs. It is also the program's natural answer to "one-shot filling".
Cost, time
Stage 1: about 1 hour, $0. Stage 2: about $2–4 on AWS (24 runs plus gardens) or about 9 hours on the Mac.
Success
Stage 1: a cluster-3 representative keeps its dependence on a second producer's background or on waste, and four times the biomass does not rescue it (under 8 of 16 survive alone). Stage 2: an obligate lineage holds at least 5% of living cells for at least 10⁵ steps in at least 3 of 5 mutation runs with the producer, and dies out in obligate-only runs.
Kill
Density alone rescues them (an Allee threshold, not interdependence), or they are extinct within 2×10⁵ steps in at least 4 of 5 runs (the dependence was an artefact of the 64-cell tile).
Risk
Effective n is about 2. Roles stay tracker-based. A positive result could still be a single fill with no recurrence.

Stage 1 result (added 2026-09-30, after the run). It ran on the Mac, in a form fixed in docs/plan.md beforehand (48 genomes, five media, 16 replicates; tools/obligates-x.ts, experiments/foundations/fo.json). All 12 cluster representatives reproduce their dependence on fresh seeds (at most 1 of 16 alive in the standard medium, 0 of 16 in waste, 16 of 16 on both producers' backgrounds), but the kill rule fired: at four times the seeding biomass, 11 of 12 survive 16 of 16 alone, including all three cluster-3 representatives. The dependence is a founding-density threshold, not shown to be interdependence, and the obligates are as photosynthetic as the others (10 of 12 phototroph) and displace the producer rather than eat its products. Stage 2 as written is not supported; cluster 9's representative (1 of 16 even at four times biomass) is the one candidate left.

Addendum, done 2026-09-30 (zero GPU, all 416 runs on disk: the 128 B/C runs, test 3's 96 solo runs, the diagnostic's 192; tools/recurrence-x.ts, experiments/foundations/fr.json, definitions fixed in docs/plan.md before counting). It uses a simpler event than the second-generation originations named above: a later role window that follows at least 10⁵ steps below 5%, or is held by a new founder clade, starting after step 2×10⁵. By that count S1 and S5 show none in any of 16 runs (so the "3 of 5 S1 or S5 runs" success test is not met, and the pre-stated reading is one-shot). The other families are mixed at low rates: B clouds 0.28 events per run [0.17, 0.40] against 0.06 without mutation, solo founders 0.18 against 0.00, but the diagnostic's subjects 0.06 against 0.17, so nothing here shows recurrence as a general property. What does occur is late first fills of new roles (0.3–0.65 per mutation run against 0–0.17 without mutation), not repeats of filled ones. The bar for step 1: an S6 world needs at least 3 of 5 mutation runs with an event, a mean above the B clouds' 0.28 per run, a difference from its own no-mutation runs whose 90% interval excludes zero, and fewer events in its producer-only and obligate-only controls.

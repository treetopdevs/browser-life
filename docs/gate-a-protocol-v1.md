## Operational unit and claims

A candidate is a spatial support of bound matter, independent of genome labels. Primary support: B+P >=48, eight-neighbor connectivity, within-tile periodic geometry; threshold 1 is mandatory sensitivity analysis. Every component is recorded at each durable100-step sample. Per-step split/merge/no-overlap transitions are logged, but transient new supports without predecessors may occur between durable samples; this is not a complete birth detector. A candidate is not an organism by definition. Select the largest primary component after preparation, minimum bound mass256; ties use lowest site. Selection is independent of subsequent response.

Supports are extracted and linked every physical step by overlapping sites only; durable summaries are emitted every100steps. A unique one-to-one match supports observed geometric continuity; zero overlap is unresolved rather than death, and splits/merges remain ambiguous. No overlap relation establishes ancestry, matter continuity or causal production. Threshold disagreement is segmentation dependence. Genome replacement cannot change support membership. Observation labels never enter physics.

Evaluate separately: present bound support; sampled continuation; local reaction activity; maintenance; mass recovery after perturbation; descriptive nonmass structure; supported production. Production is unavailable without independent causal evidence and independently functioning descendants. Neither a scripted split, a second component, genome spread, growth nor repair proves reproduction. A missing second event within the horizon is censored. Material-retention claims remain unavailable under the prior observer limitation.

# M4 finite-window growth amendment: design under calibration

Date: 2026-09-29. Status: development, before new pipeline pilot. The original M4 result remains not met. This document is not a confirmation freeze. Implemented contract: `experiments/amendments/m4-growth-v1.ts`; estimator and real/synthetic entry paths: `tools/lib/m4-growth*.ts`.

## Question and estimand

For each complete history, let g = [C_new(1,000,000) − C_new(500,000)] / 5. C_new is the existing ActivityTracker cumulative number of threshold-crossing episodes. This is not biomass, number of functions, or necessarily a count of unique genotypes: an extinct key is removed and a later recurrence may cross again. Use the exact frozen thresholds 10,008 (gradient-m3) and 14,613 (spots-m3), census 100, deep 10, with compatible source identities. The estimand is E[g_treatment − g_neutral] over independently drawn seeds within each preset.

Matching seeds intentionally shares the initialized world and counter-based random inputs; conditions change the controller mapping. Require recomputed initial-state parity after normalizing only the neutral condition config. State hashes ordinarily include config, so their unnormalized difference does not disprove shared physical initialization. The separate pair contributes one observation. Divergence after initialization is the intervention's outcome and does not invalidate pairing. No migration rings enter this analysis.

## Candidate statistical contract

Use a one-sided paired Student t mean test and corresponding lower confidence bound. This directly targets the mean contrast. It assumes independent seed pairs and normal differences for finite-sample exactness; approximation under other distributions is subject to the declared simulation checks, not guaranteed by a large n label. The numerical t integration is tested against analytic distributions and independent critical values. There is no randomized analysis seed or switch to a rank test.

The candidate smallest relevant excess is **1 threshold crossing per 100,000 steps**, equivalent to five additional crossings over the half-million-step window. This is an operational reporting resolution, not evidence for a biological novelty threshold. Test mean excess >1, and require the one-sided lower bound to exceed 1. A zero-variance sample has descriptive mean but unavailable Student inference, including identical positive differences.

Retain both presets. Bonferroni alpha 0.005 per preset controls the family of two amended growth claims at 0.01 under valid per-preset tests. Overall M4 requires both presets to pass this endpoint and the original endpoint 1 on the same fresh required data. Original endpoint 1 retains its original comparisons and Holm policy; no historical positive is carried forward. No additional held-out/M5 claim is made. The existing endpoint-1 rank method changes to its explicit normal mode above combined sample size 60; this must be reported and calibrated rather than presented as an exact test at the candidate allocation below. Shared seeds also mean its original unpaired test assumptions need calibration under paired inputs. Failure there blocks overall launch readiness even if endpoint 2 works.

Candidate allocation is **64 seed pairs per preset**, with all three conditions on each seed. This is a calibration target, not an adopted confirmation sample size. A design effect two units above the floor with paired SD four corresponds to standardized margin 0.5; normal planning gives about 92% per-preset power at n=64 and alpha 0.005. Actual Student power, skew/rare-event sensitivity and interval width must be measured before adoption. Target at least 80% power under the declared alternative with a one-sided 95% lower power bound above 0.80; target expected confidence margin no wider than the two-unit planning margin. If actual variability or calibration does not support this, report insufficient precision rather than launch this matrix.

Keep all completed extinctions as actual flat-tail curves. Missing, duplicate, unexpected, incomplete or conservation-failing histories make the requested matrix unavailable; no silent exclusion or zero imputation. Exact schedule and condition/seed coverage are checked. Technical retries repeat identical inputs and retain failed attempt costs; no replacement seed. Two-seed development estimates are descriptive and cannot qualify a confirmation allocation.

## Calibration contract to freeze before validation

First run numerical/curve controls: identical trajectories, shifted baseline, ties, unequal variability, extinction, real bounded advantage, saturation within and beyond the window, periodic turnover, delayed growth, missing histories and planted effects. Curve controls test only estimation. Constructed transport/physical controls and lineage-stream activity construction are separately identified.

Reuse existing `tools/lib/nullgen.ts` generators through the same new activity/analysis path with explicitly synthetic provenance. Real provenance checks remain mandatory and cannot be bypassed by the CLI. Synthetic runs use the declared frozen numerical thresholds and schedules but cannot claim to have been physically generated by the threshold pilot.

Development master seed: 650009001. Independent validation master: 650009002, subject to the common ledger audit. Initial validation: 200 trials per scenario/preset stratum, at most ten strata. Record scenario parameters and seed-index identities in a frozen manifest. Do not concatenate reruns or deterministic supersets as new data. The eight-hour CPU cap includes development and failed attempts.

Candidate acceptance envelope, fixed before validation: under each null stratum, the one-sided 95% upper binomial bound on false support must be ≤0.025; report its relation to the nominal 0.005 test. This is a coarse screen against inflation, **not proof of exact 0.005 size**. With 200 trials, even zero rejections has upper bound about 0.0149, so tight verification is impossible. A claim needing tighter calibration remains blocked pending a separately frozen precision extension. For sensitivity strata, use the power/width criteria above. Do not average away a failed stratum. Degenerate/unavailable results are tallied separately, not mistaken for successful inference. The final launch decision must explicitly judge whether this coarse envelope is adequate; validation alone does not silently resolve that limitation.

After freezing the design and passing provenance preflight, use two fresh development seeds per preset across treatment, neutral and no-mutation (12 one-million-step histories maximum). Benchmark one full history first; four GPU-hours maximum for this track, including attempts. Run the original endpoint-1 analysis and new endpoint-2 path, preserving all outputs. Any design revision makes these outcomes used development data.

## Remaining launch requirements

Independent numerical/provenance review; full-pipeline synthetic calibration; physical activity detector checks; authenticated pilot rehearsal; exact future seed matrix; compatible threshold proof; measured compute/storage estimate; immutable amendment/source hash and dated registration. Until these exist, the executable endpoint is a candidate and no fresh confirmation is authorized by this document.

Statistical reference: [NIST paired observations](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/prc/section3/prc311.htm) and [confidence intervals for differences](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/prc/section3/prc312.htm).

## Development checks completed before validation

The estimator/provenance suite passes 11 focused tests; a separate constructed-census-world test passes at both frozen thresholds. The latter uses prescribed snapshots and exact biomass-to-nutrient replacement, not a dynamically evolved organism. It confirms threshold crossing, extinction and repeated crossing after a key reappears, which limits C_new to episodes rather than unique innovations.

Twelve development trials used master 650009001 with distinct scenario/preset/trial identities (reports under `runs/foundations-next/m4-calibration-development-v1` through `v4`). The legacy neutral-drift benchmark took 22.73 seconds at 64 pairs and was degenerate. Periodic turnover took 10.64 seconds; six saturation/random-walk/bounded-advantage controls took 58.30 seconds. The four targeted mean-boundary/alternative controls took 1.82 seconds total. The constructed mean-3 alternative was supported; the three boundary controls were not. One trial per cell provides no size or power validation. The old bounded-treatment generator was not supported at the chosen floor; it is not the power-planning alternative.

Proposed independent validation strata (200 trials each): `pairedEndpoint1Null`, `boundaryRareNegative`, `extinctionBoundary`, `meanAlternative`, and `longPeriodLoop`, each in both presets. This retains a turnover control while directing most checks at the actual mean-floor boundary. Targeted controls use single-census lineage episodes with abundance frozen-threshold+1; they test the real activity construction, not physical feasibility. Symmetric error is rounded N(0,20²) episodes; late neutral count is 1000+floor(100U); treatment adds five episodes at the boundary or fifteen for the alternative. Rare-negative centered error is +10 with probability .95 and −190 with probability .05. Pair-shared baseline/random draws are explicit and each seed pair is independent. Extinction packs the same crossings before 750,000 and retains empty scheduled censuses thereafter. The old periodic generator retains its documented parameter law.

Scenario-specific forecast is approximately 85 minutes for all 2,000 trials, comfortably within the remaining eight-hour CPU cap; the single slow neutral-drift trial is not extrapolated to every targeted scenario. A source-bound manifest and independent review are still required before launch. The activity runner now also invokes the original `evaluateEndpoint(PRIMARY_ENDPOINTS[0], ...)` on the same full-horizon cumulative counts, recording the original two comparisons, method disclosure and joint per-preset result; this preserves endpoint-1 semantics without computing unrelated held-out metrics. Synthetic joint results are diagnostic, never milestone confirmation.

The final candidate validation set replaces the separate symmetric boundary cell with a paired endpoint-1 null stress; `extinctionBoundary` retains the symmetric mean-floor test. In `pairedEndpoint1Null`, treatment and both controls have equal marginal final-count distributions: shared baseline plus rounded N(0,20²) noise for treatment and minus that same noise for controls. The small shared neutral level varies by 0–9 episodes. This deliberately tests opposite responses to shared randomness and can make the unpaired rank test anti-conservative. It is a stress model, not an assertion about the actual ecological correlation. Require its original endpoint-1 false-support upper bound ≤0.025 as well as the declared endpoint-2 criteria; a failure is a methodological blocker, not grounds to remove the scenario. One development trial per preset checked execution only.

The validation runner enforces exactly these ten scenario/preset pairs and 200 trials each, fixed acceptance values, canonical source identities before and after every trial, explicit incomplete reports on failure, and an elapsed-cap check before declaring completion. It reports original endpoint-1 size only in paired-null and periodic-null strata; the other scenarios have real endpoint-1 differences.

## Checkpoint and resume policy

Calibration writes one append-only row per completed trial and immutable terminal `attempt-N.json` receipts. `--resume` requires byte-identical stored/requested manifests, current source hashes, an unchanged trial-log digest bound in the latest terminal receipt, and an exact prefix of the frozen trial order. It skips completed identities and carries forward cumulative wall time; it does not concatenate independent-looking copies or renew the CPU budget. Each attempt also has its own runtime record and report; a writer lock excludes simultaneous writers. `--max-trials` permits an orderly checkpoint without changing the scientific manifest. A killed writer without an authenticated terminal receipt, stale lock or partial final log line is refused pending explicit technical recovery; existing files are preserved.

The engineering resume check executed development trial 0, stopped, resumed trial 1 exactly once, retained cumulative costs, and refused a completed restart. Pure tests reject changed identities, non-prefix/duplicate trials, changed log or manifest hashes, nonterminal receipts and exhausted budgets. These are reused development identities, not additional independent calibration observations.

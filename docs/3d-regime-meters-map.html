3The regime map

One cell is one setting, read at T0 (20,000 steps). Colour is the divergence exponent e of the damage count (blue: the damage dies, neutral: flat, orange: it spreads). The outline is the class the rule gave the setting, hatching marks settings that were extinct, carpets or relics, and M4's label of the same setting at 50,000 steps sits in the corner where M4 has data. Two sliders choose the axes; the other seven stay at the values you set, starting at B0. Most cells of a grid are empty: the sweep varies one constant at a time from B0.

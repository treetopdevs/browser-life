## Geometry and subsequent copy persistence

At birth, all 275 focal/comparison branch pairs are disconnected under the tracker-like threshold48/four-neighbor graph and connected under the sensitive threshold1/eight-neighbor graph. Across eleven censuses from t through t+1000, 22 rows remain disconnected from every comparison under threshold48/four-neighbor geometry; zero do under threshold1/eight-neighbor geometry. Only one of those 22 rows has positive local attributed-parent copy contribution. These are cadence- and graph-specific observations. They do not establish continuous separation, and a tenuous bridge alone does not negate biological individuality.

At t+1000, focal birth-reference copies remain detectable for 91/200 rows and are no longer detected for 109. This measures a copy family, not viability, organism death, sustained metabolism, reconstruction or repeated reproduction. Material attribution after mixing and recycling, biological age, independently viable descendants, rebuilt organization and descendant fitness remain unavailable.

## Separate missed-event sensitivity check

The ten prespecified windows contain 10,000 consecutive candidate frames and **592 raw copy-associated fragmentation flags**, all with complete 100-step future descriptors. **397** retain known copy support in separate threshold1/eight-neighbor components at every one of those future steps. These are positive geometric observations worth investigating; they prevent treating the sampled-link result as an absence of all natural fragmentation.

Of the 592 flags, 110 occur in census intervals containing neither tracker budding nor fission, and 45 include geometric overlap with other pre-existing bound support. These are leads, not 110 missed births or 45 invasions. Flags may repeatedly describe the same structure; they are not independent episodes. Interval co-occurrence is not causal correspondence. Whole-branch copy persistence does not show which old material crossed the split or whether either branch reconstructs and reproduces. Coverage is fixed and limited; no universal tracker recall is estimated.

## Conditional probes and decision

The primary accepts the independent reviewer's recommendation that contact is the only immediately relevant causal direction, but **does not execute B with the present measurement**. The current sidecar follows the genome selected by transport, while B/P at a destination combines several sources and subsequent synthesis, loss and recycling. A contact-versus-sham difference in current copy-associated mass would therefore still not distinguish replacement over existing bound support from ordinary spread or newly synthesized matter. A bounded contact-versus-sham assay is feasible with the existing infrastructure and could add causal evidence about copy-family loss, spread and geometry, including a one-step pre-reaction genome/material mismatch. It would not resolve the outstanding entity-transmission or rule-target decision. Deferral is therefore a relevance decision about that stronger question, not a claim that no useful contact experiment is possible, a lack of remaining compute or a negative experimental result. The earlier `foundation-material-flow.ts` already computes exact one-step pre-reaction source shares; `foundation-component-material.ts` explicitly leaves reaction ownership unavailable and uses mutation-off recolored twins. Those helpers can expose a one-step genome/material mismatch, but they do not establish mutation-enabled entity continuity across contact and recovery. Extending that observation through mixed reactions with defensible bounds and controls is the missing step before B can resolve the transmission decision. The frozen data are not relabelled to supply that observable.

| Probe | Decision for this reset | Reason and prerequisite |
|---|---|---|
| B: identity through contact | Omit execution; specify as next measurement task | Require validated bounds on pre-existing bound-material contribution/replacement, then same-genotype and different-genotype contact versus sham separation, including mutation-on validation. Copy-only controls could establish a narrower causal effect, but would leave the entity-transmission decision unresolved. |
| C: repeated life cycle | Defer | Window flags offer candidates, but overlapping flags are not generations. Resolve descendant identity, material continuity and viability before natural recurrence; serial transfer remains a separate intervention. |
| D: reconstruction | Defer | Neither developmental age nor newly built organization is resolved. A nonmass trait measured on carried morphology cannot establish reconstruction. |
| E: inherited success | Do not execute | A and C have not yielded interpretable entity descendants. Genome-share or biomass competition would test a different quantity. |

**Gate result: measurement-limitation exit.** G2(a) is not established. Nor is this a trustworthy reproduction-negative result qualifying for an automatic feasibility search under G2(b). The next task is measurement repair: a narrowly bounded contact assay whose prespecified multistep material bounds and functional descendant observations discriminate competing transmission explanations. Exact molecular ownership is not required if conservative bounds suffice. Its design and controls must be frozen before new outcomes. Broad founder search, rule changes and a fresh M4 ensemble remain deferred. The historical Variation verdict is preserved; this audit does not select duplication, modules, inheritance changes or an ecological mechanism.

1. **P1 — The pure-energy fixture cannot make the existing gate return `supported`.**

2. **P1 — The credit direction rule is reversed.**  
   [PLAN:42–47](/Users/nicholas/develop/browser-life-i06-free-energy-matched/docs/niche-construction-fe-2026-11-02/PLAN.md:42) defines `credit = energy(b) − energy(f)`. Consequently, positive credit means the comparator contains **less** total energy; negative credit means **more**. The eligibility rule and named test assert the opposite, potentially excluding a stronger comparator from the maximum.  
   A credit can represent documented energy removal; a negative credit represents energy input. Merely defining it as the balancing difference makes zero residual an accounting identity, not proof of budget preservation.  
   **Fix:** correct the signs, specify admissible conversion/source operations, and distinguish physical conservation from comparator budget mismatch.

At a true positive-block probability of 0.75, one contrast passes that threshold only **40.5%** of the time; requiring four contrasts, delta and survival cannot improve that probability. [G-NOISE](//Users/nicholas/develop/browser-life-i06-free-energy-matched/docs/niche-construction-fe-2026-11-02/PLAN.md:55) measures conditional Stage-2 variation, not the entire block-level detection probability used by the [decision](//Users/nicholas/develop/browser-life-i06-free-energy-matched/packages/runner/src/niche/decision.ts:100).  
    **Fix:** predeclare a detectable effect/consistency target and evaluate the complete gate’s power, or explicitly restrict the interpretation of “usable negative.”

14. **P2 — The overall two-hour budget omits dominant work.**  
    [PLAN:130](/Users/nicholas/develop/browser-life-i06-free-energy-matched/docs/niche-construction-fe-2026-11-02/PLAN.md:130) correctly computes **4,280 steps/block** and about **12.7 minutes** for 48 fresh blocks at 3.7 ms/step. That excludes dev candidates, noise ensembles, builder scaling, fixtures and repeated calibration during [`buildPayload`](//Users/nicholas/develop/browser-life-i06-free-energy-matched/packages/runner/src/niche/evidence.ts:259). Calibration + write + replay approaches **107 minutes** before fixtures, tests, reviews or load penalties.  
    **Fix:** enumerate the complete workload, benchmark it before freeze, reuse frozen calibration during generation, and defer optional dose-response/UI work if necessary.

Shared Stage-1 histories are appropriate paired blocks here: summing R replicates does not inflate n when the sign test still uses 16 blocks. Absolute T1 alignment is also supported by the existing implementation. Founder scaling can use the existing protocol field without adding WorldConfig keys.

# Competition and solo-garden analysis

`tools/foundation-competition-analysis.ts` is a CPU-only, create-new analysis of the fixed five-source genotype-only competition plan. It requires the original **CPU plan** manifest and all successful execution-batch manifests:

```text
deno run -A tools/foundation-competition-analysis.ts \
  --plan /absolute/path/to/fixed-plan/manifest.json \
  --batches /absolute/path/to/batch-0/manifest.json,/absolute/path/to/batch-1/manifest.json,... \
  --out /absolute/path/to/new-analysis-directory
```

The analyzer hashes the exact plan and batch files. Every batch must match the plan's source, rule, environment, code, row identity and transplant audits; have actual device evidence; finish within its cap; and carry successful **post-execution** revalidation. A completed row outside its batch reservation, two completed copies of one ordinal, failed or partial run evidence, a changed run start, missing census, nonzero mutation or conservation residual, or a missing available row blocks analysis. The fixed plan's unavailable donors and dependent run rows remain explicit; they are never converted to extinction outcomes. Before publishing, the CLI rehashes each source bundle's manifest and six observer files, rehashes each cache manifest and catalog, fully verifies cached checkpoints, and confirms the source's artifact digest, revision, preset and observation compatibility. It also checks its selected analysis code files before and after. These hashes give a reviewable selected-file identity, not a complete dependency-closure proof. A failure leaves `failure.json` in the reserved output directory.

For each source history, the output reports four solo controls (early donor and its own founder; late donor and its own founder), their survival and log biomass growth where the final B is positive, and paired donor-minus-own-founder growth where both members survive. Extinction has no pseudocount and yields a null numeric contrast with explicit survival flags. The analyzer requires equal-founder controls in the same source, seed and initial state to repeat exactly in final physics/artifact hashes, every census point and the full solo trace. Run wall times remain separate and may differ. Such repeats are **technical observations**, not two independent ancestry controls.

Each of the five conditioned/standardized environments retains two reciprocal competition arms. The analyzer recomputes each arm's relative biomass-growth contrast from initial and final B, separates survival patterns when a genotype is extinct, and averages the two arms only when both contrasts are finite. It keeps sampled halo/contact opportunity for each arm and flags the pair if either arm lacks sampled opportunity. Own early, own late, foreign early, foreign late and standardized results remain distinct. The foreign five-cycle reuses source histories as environments, so those contrasts are descriptive and dependent. Descriptive min–max ranges are computed across the five **source-history rows**, never across components, observer families or reciprocal arms as though they were independent worlds.

For each completed solo run, the analyzer applies the existing pure lifecycle topology calculation to its captured 100–3,000-step garden window and wraps the result under `one-solo-common-garden-observer-traceability`. It preserves all raw and topology-clean three-ID chain counts and IDs, fusion, death, overlap mixing, age-200 observation/censoring, selected age-100/200 morphology pairs and nonlinked descriptive controls. A zero-variance membrane or other trait remains zero variance; the analyzer does not manufacture a regression slope, heritability estimate or p-value. Step-100 roots are left-truncated, and later ages are **observer-identity ages**. The largest-overlap fragment retains a parent's identity, family controls can share historical ancestry, and the physical inoculum can partition while all descendants share one garden pool. These traces can motivate serial-transfer assays, but they do not establish biological life cycles or inherited reconstruction.

The output is a feasibility description of five evolutionary histories. It does not establish increasing complexity, Red Queen dynamics, equivalence after a null result, universal fitness or genetic heritability. Resource levels differ between native-conditioned pools and were not normalized; an absent competitive edge without sampled encounter is exposure-limited.

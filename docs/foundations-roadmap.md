# What the foundations results should change

Updated: 2026-09-29. Exploratory interpretation and prospective design notes, not an amendment or a new milestone gate. The bounded investigation in `foundations-plan.md` is complete; consolidated evidence is in `foundations-results.md`. The original M4 negative result remains unchanged.

## Five explanations to distinguish

The present evidence does not identify one cause of the failed held-out organization claim. At least five explanations remain compatible with it:

1. **Measurement failure:** meaningful organization exists but the observer does not recognize it, or samples too slowly. Constructed controls establish some detector behavior. Source 1's natural continuations establish that cadence changes inferred fission counts even when the terminal physics is identical. Neither result alone shows an undetected biological life cycle.
2. **Accessible variation without the relevant innovation:** point mutations change behavior, but the changes may tune an existing function, degrade it, or produce stochastic trajectory differences. The complete neighborhood screen observes variation, including 866 role-changing proposal assays that preserve the three M3 outcomes in both arms. The additional population-recovery nomination filter excludes these from the preserved role-shift class. The completed fresh validation finds seven of 28 diagnostic nominees passing the six parent/mutant biological gates, with measured changes on multiple seeds for two of them. None of the passing nominees changes fixed roles; the excluded preserved-role subset was not validated. A functional assay remains necessary before calling changes useful innovations. A broader eligibility rule requires a separate cohort.
3. **Selection at a different level:** genomes that win local biomass competition may not produce dependable individuals or collectives. Test this by comparing genotype competition, intact-propagule persistence, and repeated reproduction under the same resource conditions. It is a hypothesis about the current rules, not yet a demonstrated defect.
4. **Restricted accessible organization:** the representation, transport, chemistry or transmission process may make the desired life cycles rare or inaccessible at the tested effort. A failed search alone cannot distinguish this from an ineffective search. A matched comparator that succeeds would make a targeted mechanism change more informative.
5. **The ecology does not reward the measured trait:** a feasible structure can be costly or unnecessary under the supplied conditions. Polymer construction consumes biomass and energy (`packages/sim-ref/src/step.ts:482`), polymer gates both A and C diffusion (`step.ts:341`), and the registered M4 presets leave adhesion off. Polymer also contributes to the bound-mass field and can retain dissolved material, so these facts do not establish that it is useless or always selected against. They do show why low membrane organization cannot by itself diagnose a lack of variation or representation capacity. If transmission works but polymer-rich forms remain absent, a bounded benefit-versus-cost intervention in the same resource environment would be more informative than immediately adding genome slots. Engineered actuator or spatial interventions would test a causal role, not establish that a single natural mutation can reach the engineered form.

These explanations can coexist. Replacing a detector does not repair transmission; adding genome capacity does not establish that selection rewards its use. Choose an intervention according to the failed causal link.

## How to read the next assays

| Observation | What it would support | What remains unresolved |
|---|---|---|
| A new genotype repeatedly changes a measured trait while preserving viability and lesion recovery | Accessible, tolerable phenotypic variation under that assay | New function, natural access rate, and evolutionary accumulation |
| An extracted component persists and produces viable descendants that reproduce again in standardized gardens | A reproducible life cycle under the transfer protocol | Spontaneous origin, genetic versus structural transmission, and success in its native ecology |
| A late genotype wins reciprocal placement challenges in early, late and standard chemical environments | Relative adaptation across the tested gardens | Novel capability, a greater functional repertoire, or a major transition |
| Genetically related offspring resemble their parents more than matched unrelated or shuffled pairs at matched ages | Transmissible variation under the tested controls | A narrow-sense genetic heritability estimate or collective individuality |
| A producer-conditioned environment enables a recipient, product removal removes the benefit, and restoration recovers it at matched resources | A specific ecological dependency and opportunity-creation mechanism | General ecological closure or open-ended expansion |
| A collective repeatedly reconstructs its composition through a small viable propagule | Higher-level transmission under the assay | Selection for collective reproduction and its spontaneous evolution |

For negative findings, record the tested conditions, effect floor, uncertainty, missing observations and power. No observed effect is an absence claim only if the assay could resolve the prespecified range. A trait with no measured variation has an unidentifiable resemblance slope; zero polymer in the 18 selected source-1 components therefore cannot be presented as zero membrane heritability.

A treatment-only time shift also leaves a causal alternative open: changed performance could reflect selection, drift or biased mutation proposals. Matching a descendant against its own founder separates its current genotype effect from founder identity under that assay, but does not isolate the historical selection process. Calling a finite biomass advantage "adaptation" should therefore remain conditional. A later neutral-genotype comparison would need the same active-controller assay environment for both origins; replaying neutral physics with its fixed reference controller would not expose the evolved controllers' effects. This control is a candidate follow-up, not part of the fixed treatment-only feasibility matrix.

Tracker ages also need a narrower interpretation. At a split, the largest-overlap fragment retains the parent's ID, birth time and generation; only additional fragments receive new IDs. Reported generations are therefore asymmetric observer-identity chains. Moreover, the current fusion event requires an absorbed previous identity with no separate continuation. A component can receive overlap from multiple previous identities without emitting that event when both identities continue elsewhere. A prospective clean-chain assay must inspect all previous-to-current component overlaps, exclude or stratify this mixing, and preserve the original event log unchanged. These facts follow from `packages/metrics/src/tracker.ts`; they do not yet quantify the resulting bias in natural histories.

The initial transfer pilot provides evidence of growth from sampled material: 16 of 18 selected source-1 packets grew in fresh gardens, with exact conservation and successful mechanical restoration shams. The founder comparator grew while its zero-controller reference went extinct. Separately, the completed five-source genotype assays use identical physical inocula and find context-dependent early–late growth differences. Neither result establishes that a source-produced offspring reconstructs the parent and reproduces again. The later fixed serial-transfer pilot shows growth but supplies no eligible direct-root offspring, leaving its repeated-transfer endpoint unavailable. These results make validation of the transmission assay a targeted next step; they do not identify genome capacity as the limiting cause.

## Implications for M4–M7

**M4: make the claim match the endpoint.** A future dated endpoint comparing treatment growth with neutral growth can allow the neutral process itself to grow. A candidate analysis would estimate a prespecified late-window change per independent history, then compare conditions with a declared effect floor and uncertainty. Specify window, units, missing/extinct-history handling, treatment of paired seeds, multiplicity and the minimum precision before opening fresh outcomes. Calibrate it against equal-growth nulls, bounded treatment advantages, delayed saturation and periodic processes, as well as positive controls.

Even a clean excess-growth result is evidence of a finite-window advantage in that observable. It does not by itself establish increasing functional complexity or lack of eventual saturation. Cumulative lineage activity also needs interpretation alongside mutation opportunities, unchanged-after-clamp events and standing-founder sorting; a newly minted lineage ID is not automatically a new genotype or function. Preserve the original registered endpoint rather than retroactively changing its meaning.

A useful prospective estimand is the difference between each condition's change over the same fixed window: `(A_treatment(t2) − A_treatment(t1)) − (A_neutral(t2) − A_neutral(t1))`. If seeds supply meaningful matched initial conditions, preserve that pairing; otherwise compare the independent-history change distributions without manufacturing pairs. Repeated census rows do not increase the number of independent histories. Mutation opportunities and biomass production belong in companion diagnostic plots: dividing the primary endpoint by them automatically could remove a real treatment pathway. The window, scale and effect floor remain to calibrate and freeze on exploratory data; this is not an adopted endpoint or a completed amendment.

**M5: test who enables whom.** The existing categorical coexistence and recycling outcomes are useful operational evidence. Strengthen the functional claim with interactions: remove a candidate partner or its chemical contribution, test the recipient's response, and restore the proposed contribution while controlling total matter and energy. Genomic specialization, spatially different roles within one genotype, and an inactive lineage assigned to `mixed` are distinct possibilities. A clonal mutation-screen tile cannot distinguish ecological coexistence using lineage-role diversity.

The registered role shares count nonzero lineage-labelled cells, including polymer-only sites, and classify each lineage using last-step reaction totals at deep censuses. Zero-flux cells can increase the share attributed to their lineage's role without contributing that function. Adding zeros does not change an otherwise active lineage's classification; a wholly inactive lineage falls into `mixed`. Alternate active-cell and integrated-flux views would clarify participation, while the original operational results remain intact.

The solo viability filter is also context-specific. An isolated founder or mutant assay cannot identify a function that requires a partner, and requiring every candidate to grow alone would exclude obligate dependencies by design. A solo failure can motivate a native-community or conditioned-pool rescue test; it does not automatically justify calling the genotype intrinsically defective. Such a rescue requires matched resources and a specific removal/restoration intervention. This is a prospective extension if the current comparisons identify a dependency, not a reason to expand every screen immediately.

**M6: scale authenticated experiments.** Carry over exact source identities, declared interventions, saved intermediate states, verified continuation hashes, failed-source retention and nesting by independent history. The successful five-history reconstructions authenticate those sources on this host; it does not establish the milestone's required number of histories or device coverage. Migrating members remain dependent. More devices or repeated replays verify execution; they do not add independent evolutionary histories.

After an assay has a measurable functional outcome, a separate size-and-duration comparison could help distinguish a limitation imposed by available space/resources from one associated with representation or transmission. Keep initial densities, energy input per area, observation rules and neutral comparators explicit; report both total activity and the chosen per-unit functional trait. A larger world that contains more copies is a different result from a more capable reproducing unit. Size changes require their own scientific calibration even when the local arithmetic rules are unchanged. This is a prospective scientific use of scaling, not part of the current bounded feasibility cohort.

**M7: require a whole life cycle at the collective level.** Physical association is an initial observation. The stronger sequence is cohesion, a viable propagule, reconstruction of the collective's composition, repeated reproduction, and evidence that collective reproductive success can differ from the success of its constituent lineages. Search and hand construction can provide feasibility controls. Neither demonstrates that the transition evolved spontaneously. Keep adhesion changes, new rules and new scientific cohorts separately identified and calibrated.

Also distinguish a visible rim from a functional boundary. The explicit P-dependent permeability gate acts on dissolved A and C; it is not a direct barrier to bound B/P transport or the incoming-mass genome lottery (`packages/sim-ref/src/step.ts:280`, `step.ts:315`, `step.ts:341`). Polymer still affects the bound-mass field and can alter motion, so an effective boundary could emerge indirectly. Before treating a membrane-rich rim as protected individuality, test whether it actually reduces material/genome mixing and preserves a reproducing composition. Genetically identical but separately labelled material could support a mixing assay, provided a paired control first establishes that the labelling intervention leaves physical trajectories unchanged. This is a prospective mechanism test, not an implemented result or evidence of impossibility.

## Where going back would be worthwhile

Return first to the smallest unsupported causal link: the observer, the transfer assay, the transmission process, the selection environment, or the variation operator. Preserve the exact-arithmetic simulator and verified evidence while investigating that link. If a new mutation mechanism becomes justified, compare one change at a time under matched effort and retain the original substrate as the comparator. If a new substrate becomes justified, require its positive result under the same feasibility question before treating the old substrate's failure as informative evidence about its limitations.

The bounded foundational assays and independent validation are complete. Retain the current substrate and improve the transmission/identity assay; accessible variation and genotype-dependent growth are observed, while inherited functional organization remains inconclusive. A separately frozen mutation cohort can correct the mismatch between M3 eligibility and the additional recovery filter. M4 amendment design can proceed alongside transmission work for its stated finite-window claim. The present observations justify neither declaring M4 validated nor choosing a replacement substrate.

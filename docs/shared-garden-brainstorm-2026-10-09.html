The shared garden

Roadmap bet 4 taken apart: the garden is a log, not a server, it keeps archipelago time with Tamagotchi honesty, it is paced, and its first world is the big storm. Visual companion to shared-garden-brainstorm-2026-10-09.md.

  • Storm numbers from the preset itself

1Four promises in one sentence

The roadmap line reads as one feature. It is four promises, and the repo has substrate under three of them. The one with none is the thing itself: an open-ended, publicly tended run that advances at a pace people can follow.

2Whose clock, which world, how fast

The one clarifying question was whose clock the garden keeps. The user answered it, then chose the world, then the pace.

Not chosen

Server time

A GPU always on. The count climbs while nobody watches. About $1 an hour for an A10G, about $700 a month, and it buys nothing a paced garden lacks.

Kept as the floor

Tamagotchi time

The garden sleeps when nobody tends it and says so. "The garden slept 14 hours." Honest, and cheap: one keeper is enough.

Chosen

Archipelago time with Tamagotchi honesty

Volunteer islands advance it, the step count is a public number, and it climbs only when someone tends it. Sleeping is shown, never hidden.

Chosen

A commons on a planet world

The big storm: a wandering sun, seasons, a readable signal, heritable shape and wounds, on a 512² jar. Not the breeder made social, which pond worlds would lock out of two of the three letters.

Chosen

Paced

The garden advances at a stated rate when tended, not flat out. One Mac is enough to advance it; a second island is what verification needs; more add the ring, not speed.

Provisional

One sun wander a day

131,072 steps, released at dawn and taken by whichever island shows up. Under five minutes of one Mac at the smoke run's rate. The stone horizon (section 6) can move it.

3A log, not a server

One canonical history: a chain of verified checkpoints and a sequenced log of letters. Anyone can advance it, verify it or fork it. Nothing in a log is lost, so a day can be re-cut without a bad letter; the public queue and the keeper's veto are for catching it first.

4A day at the storm's own scale

The storm is already the weather. The strip below is one sun wander of the big storm, drawn from the preset's numbers: how many seasons pass and how often a cell is wounded. A visitor's stone is one mark among them, which is why the prediction in section 6 says small stones will not carry and the sun will.

5Letters, the budget, the honesty surface

The bet says "visitors feeding a world". The user's sighting, one seed by eye, suggests the storm needed food to get going and made worms once food got scarce. The note's hypothesis from it: food makes the storm dull and hunger makes it build. So the budget is flipped, and what the page shows is the promise.

First letter

A stone

A lesion at a chosen place, landing at dawn. The world stays closed: bodies there turn to waste and their energy to heat. The storm wounds every cell about ten times a wander, so a small stone is one more; if test 6.2 bears that out, the letter that carries is a meteor, radius 32 or more.

Second letter

The sun

Dim, brighten or shift it for a span. Light is energy, so matter stays closed. This is the scarcity dial, and nothing in the repo changes light mid-run yet: it is a fourth intervention kind.

Founding only

Food

In the preset if a start-nutrient arm finds the middle ground (test 6.1: 32 starves, 64 carpets, 48 is populated on two seeds of three), so the garden is closed from step 0. Otherwise a founding feed, once, by the first visitors. After founding it is closed in matter either way. A feast by vote is a later garden's question.

The garden's, not yours

Budget

So many letters a day; the rest wait for the next dawn. One per device per dawn, kept on the device as a courtesy. No account, no cookie. The privacy page gains two lines: letters are public, and the courtesy budget lives on your device. Tending earns no letters: most visitors are on phones.

6Two tests before any coordinator code

Together they decide whether the big storm can be a garden at all and how fast it should go. Both run on a Mac for nothing and touch no physics. The first has run; its reading was written down first, and its result is below the diagram.

7What must be built, smallest first

None of it touches physics or a pin. The first item is also what test 6.2 needs.

1Lesions and feeds inside headless segments

Replayed from a log slice, with a test that a segmented run with a lesion equals the continuous one byte for byte.

2The garden in the coordinator

A run with a release policy (the pace), a letter queue, segment specs that carry the letters inside them, a public endpoint with the honesty numbers, a fork from any verified checkpoint.

3The garden page

The numbers, the queue, the letter form with its dawn preview, and "since your last visit" from a step kept on the device.

4The sun letter

A fourth intervention kind, applied in the runner and the lab, light being energy. No pin moves because no existing config carries one.

5The ring

A second garden and the metapopulation exchange that exists. Later: feeds as a collective letter, cuttings through the transplant tool, the breeder made social.

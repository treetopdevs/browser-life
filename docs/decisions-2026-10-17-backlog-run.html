D-02Slices are separate sessions, each running its own workflow; at most three at once

When
2026-10-17, before slice 1
Context
Usage at start: 5-hour window 17% (resets 00:20 UTC), weekly all-models 5%. A peer browser-life session ("Workflow progress planning") is running gene v2 arm runs on the GPU, and earlier sessions recorded load averages near 285 on this Mac.
Decision
The coordinator starts one session per slice (own worktree from the latest origin/main), each running a plan → implement → review workflow. Up to three slices run at once, on disjoint files, and at most one of them may need the GPU at a time.
Why
Parallel slices spend the reset budget faster; the cap keeps test runs from timing out on a loaded machine and keeps GPU goldens honest.

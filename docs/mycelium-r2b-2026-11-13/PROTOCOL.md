## 1. Question and what R2 taught

Does the inherit arm (children copy the parent's controller with mutation) make a world measurably different from (a) the heredity-blocked twin and (b) a deposition-matched trail-following (Physarum) null? "Different" is a difference in the readouts of section 4 under the test of section 8, not "better", and not adaptation.

R2 (five dev seeds, 20,000 steps, a 2-SD floor) could not answer. The facts this protocol is built on (R2 `RESULT.md`, `CALIBRATION.md`, `evidence/`):
- Inherit is seed dependent with two trail regimes. Mean trail mass in steps 10,000-20,000 per seed: 35,850 (seed 101, connected trail, 91% of particles depositing) against 60-1,650 (the other four, deposit fraction 0.01-0.94). Blocked is nearly deterministic across seeds (sum P 13-14k, age about 8,300). A 2-SD floor from five seeds is set by that spread alone, so no control and no contrast cleared it.
- Inherit's trail is not stationary at 20k. Sum P per 5k block falls severalfold per block (seed 102: 4,363, 1,794, 294; seed 105: 1,412, 253, 4) while seed 101 rebounds (7,451, 20,269, 51,431). Half-window drift of sum P was -1.94 to +0.87 for inherit against about -0.1 for blocked. Blocked itself still falls (14.5k to 13.1k per 5k at the end).
- The controls (light x2, decay x4) moved no readout past the floor, and the physarum null deposited one unit per particle per step (about 220k units and 2.2k particles against 4.7-5.7k particles), so it differed by construction.

Disclosure: the readout families below (trail mass, age, deposit behaviour, regime) were chosen after R2's dev seeds 101-105 were seen. Those seeds are retired from R2b. Nothing from any R2b seed informs readout choice after Freeze 1 except through the calibration of section 6.

**Pilot (before Freeze 1, disclosure only; it set no threshold and no rule).** Six smoke runs of 100,000 steps on the current rules (inherit and blocked, smoke seeds 1101, 1102, 1103, run with the draft's seed scheme, so world seeds 1101-1103 for both arms), looked at only for: is there trail in (50k, 100k], does the deposit measure `d` clear 1/16, do regime switches occur, how much of Moran's I is invalid, and how long a 100k run takes. What it showed: **all three inherit runs had lost their trail by the window** (sum P per 10k steps from step 10,000: seed 1101 1,037, 110, 69, then 0 from 40k; seed 1102 45 then 0; seed 1103 1,752, 565, 228, 189, 34, then 0 from 60k), so TM was 0, 0 and 0.0008, `d` was 0, 0 and 5e-6 (far below 1/16), and Moran's I was invalid at every census of W in all three; no regime switch was visible inside (50k, 100k]. The three blocked runs had trail (TM 1.7-2.1, `d` 0.017-0.022, which is also under 1/16, Moran's I valid at every census), population 4,700 and falling trail mass (sum P 7.4-9.9k at 100k). Six concurrent processes finished 600,000 steps in 92-112 s. **Prediction, stated before any dev run: if the three pilot seeds are typical, calibration ends MEASUREMENT-LIMITED** (the median inherit `d` is under 1/16 with fewer than 4 trail-on seeds, so 6.3 cannot match the null; MI is invalid at most inherit censuses, so 6.7 drops it and a PASS is unattainable). That outcome is a result about this window and these rules, not a failure of the instrument, and the protocol does not weaken any rule to avoid it.

**Order of events, so the reader can judge "set no threshold".** The review's findings came first and the pilot's first result lines were read before the following rules were written into this protocol: the Otsu gap criterion (6.4), the 25% match gate (6.5, 6.6), the one-Holm-over-controls and PASS-attainable rules (6.7), MI's definition and its 8-seed rule (4, 8), the PASS pair (8) and the intersection family (8). Each answers a review finding, none was chosen by looking at a pilot value, and each makes READY or PASS harder, not easier, than the draft (the one rule that eases a keep decision, REG's exemption from the 6-distinct rule, answers the review's finding that the draft dropped REG exactly when regimes were stable, and does not change what a PASS needs). That is a disclosure, not a proof: the pilot values were on the screen. Nothing in the timing gate was changed after its figure was read. This Freeze 1 was taken before any logged run (no dev, matching or confirmation run exists); re-taking Freeze 1 before the first logged run is part of Freeze 1, not an amendment under the cap of section 10.

## 2. Arms (all `defaultConfig()`, 128 x 128, only the listed keys changed)

So no two arms share founders or a counter-based PRNG stream, which is what makes the unpaired tests of section 8 rest on independent worlds instead of on a claim that a shared seed has no effect. Slots, logs and file names use the slot; the stored config shows the world seed.

The frozen arm is not run in R2b. World size stays 128 x 128 because R2's drift numbers are for this world; a larger world is an alternative not taken here.

**Matched physarum null.** R2's constants stay (EAT 127, SPLIT 127, gradient step 64, turn 16, wander 64). Only the deposit changes, through one new optional key `physarumDeposit` (integer 0..768, units of 1/256 matter per particle-step), absent from `defaultConfig()`, never inserted by `createWorld`, valid only with `controller: "physarum"`. Per particle and step: `u = (v >> 8) + (draw(pb, 64) % 256 < (v & 255) ? 1 : 0)`, `DEPOSIT = 32 * u` (so `(maxDeposit * DEPOSIT) >> 7 = u`, 0..3), energy and matter limits apply as for any particle. Slot 64 is unused by the existing rules. With `physarumDeposit` absent the R2 null (`DEPOSIT = 32`) is unchanged, so R2 digests and the pinned digest in `tools/test/mycelium.test.ts` do not move.

**Sensor-blinding control.** New optional key `blindTrailGradient` (boolean, absent from `defaultConfig()`): inputs 3 and 4 (trail gradients) are forced to 0 in the sense phase, in the CPU and the GPU. The heritable controller keeps evolving; only its trail-gradient perception is removed.

## 3. Engine, horizon, cadence, compute

- **Engine.** All arms run on the GPU (`gpu.ts`, bit-for-bit parity with `world.ts`, R1). Freeze-1 prerequisites (section 10): the physarum arm (with `physarumDeposit`) and `blindTrailGradient` are ported to `gpu.ts` and `world.ts` and added to `tests/deno/mycelium_gpu.ts` cases (both with a mid-run readback, encode, decode, upload resume); the physarum port is done by another agent and is not part of this document.
- **Horizon: 100,000 steps; measurement window W = steps (50,000, 100,000].** Justification: R2 shows inherit's trail still moving severalfold per 5k steps at 15-20k, one seed switching regime, and mean particle ages of 555-2,916 (the oldest about 3k), so (50k, 100k] is at least 17 mean lifetimes long and starts 30k steps (roughly 6-10 of the 3-5k-step trail e-folds seen in R2) past the 20k edge of R2. **This is an extrapolation from R2 (20,000 steps) and a three-seed pilot (above), neither of which shows a trail-on inherit run in the window.** It may still be too short for rare regime switches. The reports print half-window drift (second half (75k, 100k] against first half (50k, 75k], relative to the window mean) for TM, n and sum P, per seed, and the per-seed half-window agreement (section 6.8). The pre-registered result is about (50k, 100k], not a steady state.
- **Cadence.** Scalar census every 100 steps (the first at step 100; there is no step-0 census): `n`, cumulative births, refused, sum P, matter and energy ledger, 1,000 per run, 500 in W. Heavy census every 500 steps (**100 heavy censuses in W, 200 per run**): the per-cell fields, live particle ages, the controller probe, and the realised deposit. The ledger (matter and energy residual) is checked at every census and at the end, not at every step as in R2. It is a cumulative exact identity (sum A+C+P+M constant; energy = initial + lightIn - heat), so a step that broke it leaves a nonzero residual at the next census; only the step that broke it is localised less finely. Any nonzero residual is INVALID.
- **Guard cadence.** n is taken from the 100-step scalar census (500 samples in W); the guard (section 7) uses those.
- **Compute.** (Estimates written before the timing gate; the gate figure below governs.) Steps (each 100k except matching): dev 6 arms x 20 seeds = 120 runs = 12.0M steps; matching at most 3 iterations x 8 seeds x 30k = 0.72M; confirmation 3 arms x 16 seeds = 48 runs = 4.8M. Total about 17.5M GPU steps, plus 1,000 scalar and 200 heavy censuses per run (readback of about 1 MB each; budget 20 s per run, about 1 h serial over 168 runs, overlapped by concurrency). Two CPU re-runs of 100k steps for the digest cross-check (about 8 min each at the R2 rate of 4.4 ms per step, run beside the rest). **No GPU timing at 128 x 128 exists** (tests/deno/mycelium_gpu.ts prints ms per step but no figure is recorded; "about 33x" is for large worlds, and 128 x 128 with 5k particles is probably dispatch bound). Required: about 1,800 aggregate steps per second (17.5M / 9,700 s) to finish compute in about 2.7 h of the 4 h, leaving about 1.3 h for the serial parts (analysis, matching, readbacks, slack). Critical path: inherit dev (20 runs) then classifier and deposit target then matching (3 sequential rounds of 8 parallel 30k-step runs) then physarum dev (20 runs); blocked and the controls run beside the inherit batch.
- **Timing gate (before Freeze 1; timing only, no results read).** Run 8 concurrent smoke runs (seeds below 2001, 3,000 steps each, mixed arms) with `r2b.ts timing` and record aggregate steps per second. At 1,800 or more: this plan. At 1,200 to 1,799: the pre-stated reduced plan, 16 dev seeds per arm (2001-2016) and 12 confirmation seeds per arm (5001-5012), fixed in advance (then 9.6M + 0.7M + 3.6M = 13.9M steps, about 3.2 h at 1,200). Below 1,200: the protocol is not started and is redrafted. Nothing else may change on timing grounds. **The plan and the figure are recorded here, inside the hashed protocol**, in the block below (format of `FROZEN`'s lines), and `FROZEN` repeats them; the runner refuses a `FROZEN` whose plan or figure differs from this block, that states either twice, or whose plan does not follow from its figure by the mapping above, and every logged run carries the plan so a later plan is refused by the seed log.

## 4. Readouts

Each run value `v(r, arm, seed)` is a function of the run's stored series, over W. Six readouts, with the family and the by-construction label.

| id | readout | definition (W = (50k, 100k]) | vs blocked | vs physarum |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| TM | trail mass per particle | mean over the 500 scalar censuses of sum P / n | counted | counted (not exposed; deposit rate is matched, the stock still depends on survival and where trail lands) |
| MI | trail structure | mean over the 100 heavy censuses of W of Moran's I of P on the 128 x 128 torus, 4-neighbour: `I = sum_i (x_i - m) * sum_{j in nb(i)} (x_j - m) / (4 * sum_i (x_i - m)^2)`, `m` = mean of P. Exactly invariant to scaling P. A census is valid with sum P >= 500 (0.03 per cell or more) and nonzero variance, **and a run has an MI only if every one of its 100 heavy censuses in W is valid; otherwise MI is undefined (null)**. Neither alternative is allowed to define it: counting invalid censuses as 0 made MI a mass indicator below the cutoff (the same pattern at 320 units counts 0 and at 640 units counts its real value), and averaging only the valid censuses lets the mass cutoff choose which spatial observations are averaged (a quarter of an arm's censuses clustered under 500 units and the rest isolated above it give a different mean from the same arm without the first quarter). With every census averaged and each scale-invariant, trail mass cannot move MI; it can only make it undefined. A readout needs 8 measurable seeds per arm (section 8) to be tested | counted over the measurable seeds | counted over the measurable seeds (not exposed) |
| REG | regime fraction | fraction of the 500 scalar censuses with TM_t >= `tau` (section 6.4). A proportion: 0 or 1 per seed when regimes are stable, so the "6 distinct values" rule of 6.7 does not apply to it (its keep rule is the classifier's adequacy). It is TM thresholded per census, hence the same factor as TM | counted | counted (not exposed) |
| AGE | median particle age | mean over heavy censuses of the median age of live particles | counted | counted, **exposed**: the null always eats at full output and always splits when able |
| TURN | turnover | `1000 * (births(100k) - births(50k)) / (50,000 * mean n over the scalar censuses in W)`, births per particle per 1,000 steps | counted | counted, **exposed**: same reason |
| SIG | heritable deposit signature | mean over heavy censuses and live particles of the fraction of the 6 fixed probe inputs (below) on which output 3 (DEPOSIT) is above 0, computed by `probe`-style forward passes on the stored genome | counted | **excluded**: the physarum genome is carried and never read, so SIG measures nothing there |

Probe inputs (six vectors of ten input values each, `PROBE_INPUTS` in `r2b-protocol.ts`, hashed): all zero; nutrient 100; trail 100 here with gradient east +100; trail 100 here with gradient west -100; waste 100; energy 100, mass 100, crowding 100. They fix the situation so SIG measures the genotype, not the field around it.

**Families, and why a PASS asks for two.** The six readouts are four factors: MASS (TM and REG: trail per particle, REG being TM thresholded), STRUCT (MI: spatial structure of the trail, exactly scale-invariant), LIFE (AGE and TURN: near-inverses in R2, R4 0.37-2.78 against age 2,900-556, and exposed against the null because it always eats at full output and always splits when able, so they separate inherit from physarum by construction, and from blocked as well in R2), GENOME (SIG, blocked contrast only). The draft's PASS ("two readouts in Both, one of them TM, MI or REG") collapsed to one non-exposed factor plus a nearly free LIFE readout. **PASS now needs the two non-exposed families, MASS and STRUCT, both separating from both arms** (section 8). The report prints Spearman correlations between kept readouts over the pooled confirmation values and within each arm (pooling mixes arms, so a between-arm difference inflates it) and flags any pair above 0.8 in either; a flag does not change the label.

**Descriptive only** (every arm, never in a decision): n, sum P, births, refusals, mean M and E, live genotype count, the realised deposit per particle-step `d` (below), drift, per-seed half-window values.

## 6. Calibration (dev seeds only; nothing from 5001+ is read)

6.1 **Batch A** (parallel): inherit, blocked, pc-light, pc-upkeep, pc-blind on all dev seeds, 100k steps. Population guard (section 7) on inherit and blocked trips: calibration ends MEASUREMENT-LIMITED. A control that trips is unusable and ignored; if all three are unusable, no readout can be kept and the verdict is MEASUREMENT-LIMITED. A control that touches the cap (`refused > 0` in W; light x4 probably does) is valid, flagged, and still used.

6.2 **Deposit measure.** `d(state)` = (sum over live particles of the matter each would deposit this step) / n, a pure function of a readback state. It is computed by **copy-and-step**: the state is copied with trail decay set to 0, one `step` is run on the copy, and `d` is the change in sum P divided by n (so it is exactly what `step` deposits, including the energy and matter limits, the eating grants, upkeep rounding and, for the physarum arm, that step's own random draw; it costs one CPU step per heavy census and adds nothing to the state, so the checkpoint encoding and digests do not move). It is evaluated at each of the 100 heavy censuses in W; the run value `d_s` is the mean.

6.3 **Matching target.** `T = median over the dev inherit seeds of d_s`. If `T < 1/16` (a null that deposits under 1/16 unit per particle-step is a random walker, not a trail-follower), `T` is replaced by the median of `d_s` over inherit seeds classified trail-on (section 6.4), **but only if the classifier of 6.4 is adequate**; a rejected classifier supplies no trail-on seeds (the draft used the raw cut even when it was rejected). If that median is still under 1/16, or fewer than 4 seeds are trail-on, the null cannot be matched and the verdict is MEASUREMENT-LIMITED (the protocol does not weaken the null to proceed). Trail mass is not matched, so TM stays a readout; deposit rate is matched, so `d` is equal by construction and is not a readout. **The fallback is a qualifier, not a free pass:** the target is then the trail-on seeds' deposit, which is larger than a typical inherit seed's (in the pilot the median `d` was 0 to 5e-6). The calibration report and the confirmation report print `T` over the median `d` of all dev inherit seeds, say "FALLBACK" when it was used, and a PASS under the fallback is read as "differs from a null matched to the trail-on seeds", which for the trail-off seeds the null exceeds by construction on TM, MI and REG.

6.4 **Regime classifier (dev inherit only).** For each dev inherit seed, `x_s` = log10 of (median over W scalar censuses of TM_t, plus 0.01; the 0.01 floor puts every trail-off seed in one cluster, which is the intent: under 0.01 per particle the trail is absent). Cut `t` = the Otsu threshold on the 20 values (the cut that maximises between-class variance; ties take the lower cut). **Adequate** if both classes have at least 4 seeds, between-class variance over total variance is at least 0.70, **and the gap between the highest low value and the lowest high value is at least 0.5 (log10 units, a factor of 3 in trail per particle) and at least the larger within-class standard deviation (population form)**: two regimes are classes further apart than they are wide. (The share rule alone passes a continuum: 20 evenly spaced values give classes 10/10 and share 0.75.) Otherwise REG is dropped and the 6.3 fallback is not used (not a failure: if 100k steps leaves every seed in one regime, or a continuum, there is no regime to read). `tau = 10^t - 0.01` (at least 0) in TM units, written to `CALIBRATION.json`, applied identically to every arm. The gated quantity is the per-seed fraction (REG), not a binary class; the binary proportion (REG fraction >= 0.5) is also printed per arm with an exact Fisher test, as a description. `tau` is fitted on the same dev inherit seeds the keep rule uses (stated in section 12).

6.5 **Matching runs.** Slots 2001-2008, 30,000 steps, physarum, starting `v0 = clamp(round(256 * T), 0, 768)`. Per round, `d_phys` = median over the 8 seeds of the mean of `d` over the heavy censuses at steps 10,500 to 30,000 (40 of them, the window (10k, 30k]). Update `v <- clamp(round(v * T / d_phys), 0, 768)` (if `d_phys` is 0, the null cannot be matched: MEASUREMENT-LIMITED). Accept at the first round with `|d_phys - T| <= 0.10 * T`; at most 3 rounds. Matching **stops without acceptance** (`converged: false`) after round 3 is outside the tolerance, or earlier when the update would repeat the round's own `v` (the bound 768 or the rounding limit); the recorded `v` is **the deposit that last round ran with** (the one whose `d_phys` was measured), not the update computed from it, and the achieved `d_phys / T` is recorded and printed. A non-converged `v` is not a verdict by itself, but it must still pass the **match gate**: `|d_phys / T - 1| <= 0.25` at the last round and, for the dev physarum runs of 6.6, `|median over the dev physarum seeds of d_s / T - 1| <= 0.25`; otherwise calibration is MEASUREMENT-LIMITED ("the null is not matched"). The 25% is the same bound the report uses to flag a ratio of inherit to another arm. The physarum deposit stock reaches equilibrium within about 5k steps in R2, so 30k steps should be long enough; the 6.6 gate checks that at the protocol length. The accepted `v`, `T`, every round's `d_phys` and the 8 matching run digests go in `CALIBRATION.json`.

6.6 **Batch B:** physarum at `v` on all dev slots, 100k steps (after 6.5). Population guard on physarum applies, and so does the match gate of 6.5.

6.7 **Positive-control keep rule.** For each usable control `c` and readout `r` still in play (REG only if kept by 6.4; MI only if at least 8 dev inherit seeds have an MI (and 6 distinct values among them), tested over the measurable seeds of each side and needing 8 on the control's side too; SIG included, physarum is not involved), test control values against inherit values over the dev seeds with the test of section 8 (unpaired exact or Monte Carlo Mann-Whitney, two-sided, `|Cliff's delta| >= 0.33`). **One Holm at `alpha = 0.05` runs over every (usable control, readout) test together** (up to 18), because a readout is kept if *any* control separates it and the union of three separately corrected families is a larger chance than any one. A readout is **kept** if at least one usable control separates it. A readout is **dropped** if it has fewer than 6 distinct values among the dev inherit seeds (REG excepted, see section 4), is dropped by 6.4, or is MI with fewer than 8 measurable dev inherit seeds (or fewer than 6 distinct values among them). **Fewer than 2 kept readouts, or a kept set from which a PASS is unattainable, is MEASUREMENT-LIMITED and confirmation is not run.** A PASS is attainable only if the kept set holds a MASS readout (TM or REG) and MI (section 8): gross controls (light x4, upkeep x1.5) move n, AGE and TURN trivially, and a calibration that keeps only those would spend all 48 confirmation runs on a protocol that can return only NOT-SEPARATED or PHYSARUM-EXPLAINS. One predicate, `passAttainable`, is used by calibration, by the check before confirmation and by the decision itself.

6.8 **Horizon diagnostic (reported, gates nothing).** Per readout, the Spearman correlation across the dev inherit seeds between the first-half (50k, 75k] and second-half (75k, 100k] values, **with the per-seed half values printed for every readout** (a readout can shift a lot between halves and keep its seed ordering at rho 1), and the drift table for TM, n and sum P. A low correlation with a wide spread says the per-seed window value is not a stable property; the reader sees it before Freeze 2.

6.9 **Dev contrasts (informational, nothing reads them):** inherit vs blocked and inherit vs physarum on all readouts, 20 vs 20, printed with effect sizes, so the strength of a blocked or physarum difference and the matching quality are visible before confirmation.

6.10 `CALIBRATION.json` holds: plan (full or reduced), dev slots, `tau` (or REG dropped), `T` and whether the fallback was used, `v`, matching rounds, the dev physarum deposit ratio, kept set, drop reasons, control tests (p, adjusted p, delta per readout and control), dev contrasts, half-window values, the code and protocol hashes, run digests and **the SHA-256 of every dev and matching `series.json`** it read, so `calibration_sha256` of Freeze 2 binds the dev data as well. The dev contrast tables are printed in `calibration-report.txt`, which is kept (section 10, amendments).

## 7. Population guard

A run trips if `n < 200` at any 100-step scalar census in W. Applies to inherit, blocked and physarum in dev and confirm, and to controls in dev (a tripped control is unusable, not fatal). A trip on inherit, blocked or physarum, anywhere, makes the whole comparison MEASUREMENT-LIMITED: no alive-versus-extinct verdict is drawn. Cap-touching runs (`refused > 0` in W) are flagged and valid.

## 8. Confirmation (slots 5001-5016, after Freeze 2)

Runs: inherit, blocked, physarum (matched `v`), 16 slots each, 48 runs, batch-only and silent, then `r2b-analyze.ts confirm`. Run configs must equal the calibration (`v`), checked by the runner.

**Test (one function, used by 6.7 and here).** For readout `r` and two arms: unpaired two-sided Mann-Whitney U on the per-seed values (midranks for ties). The p-value: with no ties, the exact null distribution of U by dynamic programming; with ties, a seed-label permutation Monte Carlo with 10^6 permutations drawn from the repo's counter-based PRNG with fixed keys (`PERM_KEY` 0x72326270 for the permutations, `BOOT_KEY` 0x72326262 for the bootstrap, in `r2b-stats.ts`, hashed), `p = (1 + #{|2U* - n1 n2| >= |2U - n1 n2|}) / (1 + 10^6)`. The smallest tie-free exact p is `2 / C(2n, n)`: 3.3e-9 at 16 v 16, 7.4e-7 at 12 v 12, 1.5e-11 at 20 v 20; the Monte Carlo floor (any tie) is about 1e-6. Both are below the smallest Holm threshold used (0.05/6 = 0.0083 in a blocked family of six, 0.05/5 = 0.01 in the intersection family). Effect sizes: Cliff's delta `2U/(n1 n2) - 1` and the Hodges-Lehmann shift, each with a 95% percentile bootstrap interval (10^4 resamples, both groups resampled independently, fixed key); reported. **The delta threshold is a safeguard that rarely binds:** Holm at 0.0083 already needs `|delta|` of about 0.55 at 16 v 16 and 0.63 at 12 v 12, so a rejection with `|delta| < 0.33` is not expected. Mann-Whitney is a dominance test: a bimodal inherit against a near-constant blocked arm whose value sits between the modes can give delta near 0 with large distributional differences. REG (the per-seed share of trail-on censuses) is the readout that sees that case; no distribution-level test is added.

**Separation: per readout, against both arms at once.** The claim of a PASS is "this readout differs from blocked AND from physarum". Two separate Holm families (one per contrast) do not control that claim: with AGE truly different from both arms, TM different only from blocked and MI only from physarum, each family rejects its strong alternative and then tests its one remaining null at 0.05, so a false rejection in either puts a non-exposed readout into Both (about 10% with independent null p-values; the draft was wrong to call this an intersection-union). So: for each kept readout eligible for both contrasts (SIG is not: the physarum genome is never read) take the **intersection p-value `p = max(p vs blocked, p vs physarum)`**, apply **one Holm at `alpha = 0.05` over these p-values** (the step-down: sort p, reject while `p_(i) <= alpha / (m - i + 1)`), and call a readout **separating from both** if Holm rejects it and `|Cliff's delta| >= 0.33` against each arm. `Both` = those readouts. `SepB` = kept readouts rejected by Holm within the blocked contrast alone (SIG included), together with every member of `Both` (a readout rejected in the stricter intersection family has a blocked p no larger than its intersection p). `SepP` is the same for physarum (SIG excluded); it is descriptive, no label reads it. The two within-contrast Holm tables are still printed.

**Sensitivity statement.** With 16 v 16 and the first blocked-family Holm threshold `0.05/6 = 0.0083`, one readout in one contrast has roughly 0.7 power for a shift of 1.2 SD and about 0.9 for 1.5 SD (normal approximation, rough). **That is not the power of a PASS**, which needs two readouts (MASS and MI), each against two arms, and is lower; it is not computed. Smaller heredity effects are not detectable, and NOT-SEPARATED is not evidence of absence.

**The PASS pair.** A PASS needs `Both` to hold a MASS readout (TM or REG) **and** MI. Two non-exposed families, both separating from both arms, after one Holm over the readouts. TM with REG is not a pair (one factor), and AGE or TURN never complete one (exposed). MI is tested over the measurable seeds of each arm (section 4), and **each arm needs at least 8**: with fewer the confirmation is MEASUREMENT-LIMITED ("the structure readout cannot be tested"), as a calibration that keeps no MI is.

**Labels** (mutually exclusive, evaluated in this order, so exactly one applies; written before any data):
1. **INVALID**: a nonzero matter or energy residual at any census of any run; a mismatch of the protocol, code, calibration or confirmation-run hash against `FROZEN`; a `series.json` that is not the file the seed log recorded (SHA-256), or with a defect (section 9); a seed-log irregularity (an `invalid` entry, a fresh seed outside the confirm phase, a refused seed run, runs under two plans); or a mismatch between the final `stateDigest` of a GPU confirmation run and its CPU re-run (section 10).
2. **MEASUREMENT-LIMITED**: fewer than 2 readouts kept, or a kept set from which no PASS is attainable (calibration); fewer than 8 seeds of some arm with a measurable MI; the physarum null unmatchable or not matched within 25% (6.3, 6.5, 6.6) or no longer within 25% of `T` in the confirmation window; any population-guard trip on inherit, blocked or physarum; all controls unusable; or fewer runs finished than planned (an unfinished batch gives this label, not an error).
3. **NOT-SEPARATED**: `SepB` is empty. At least two readouts were kept and a PASS was attainable, so each showed a response to a physical control, but none separates inherit from blocked. This is "no difference seen at this horizon and sample size", not absence.
4. **PASS**: `Both` holds a MASS readout (TM or REG) and MI. Inherit differs from both contrasts on trail mass and on trail structure, the two readouts the null's fixed eating and splitting does not construct, after one Holm over the intersection p-values. The PASS text states the null's deposit in the window over `T` and whether the 6.3 fallback was used.
5. **PHYSARUM-EXPLAINS**: everything else, that is `SepB` is not empty but PASS is not met. It does **not** say the null reproduces inherit. The report prints every sub-case that applies (they overlap): (a) `Both` empty (no readout separates from both arms); (b) `Both` non-empty but only AGE and TURN (exposed); (c) `Both` holds a non-exposed readout but not the pair; (d) `|SepB| = 1` (blocked-separated on one readout only, the case R2 needed a PARTIAL label for; it lands here by design and is reported with its sub-case).

**Ordering (reported, conditions nothing).** For each readout in `Both`, inherit is beyond (same sign of the Hodges-Lehmann shift against blocked and against physarum) or between (opposite signs). The confirmation report prints, for every contrast, the ratio of inherit to the other arm for n, sum P and TM and flags any beyond 25%; the flag changes no label. It also prints the per-arm median MI invalid fraction, the null's deposit over `T`, and the correlations of section 4.

## 9. Output and analysis

`runs/mycelium-r2b/{dev,confirm}/<arm>/seed-<slot>/series.json` (scalar and heavy census series, the final digest, ledger-check count, the config with `v`, the code hash), matching runs under `match/round-<k>/physarum/seed-<slot>/`, `stage-a.json` (classifier, tau, T, v0), `matching.json`, `cpu-check.json` beside each re-checked run, and `confirm-provisional.json`, `confirm-agreement.json`, `confirm-result.json`, `confirm-report.txt`, `calibration-report.txt`. The report holds: calibration tables, per-seed values with n and sum P beside them, p, adjusted p, deltas with intervals, labels, sub-case, ordering, drift and half-window agreement with the half values, redundancy correlations. The analysis is a pure function of the run files, the seed log, `FROZEN` and `CALIBRATION.json`; it recomputes every run's readouts from the stored series and refuses a file whose cached values differ.

**The series are authenticated.** The seed log's `finished` entry records the SHA-256 of the exact bytes of the run file; the analysis refuses a file whose hash differs or whose finished entry is missing, and `CALIBRATION.json` records the hash of every dev and matching file, so Freeze 2 binds the dev data. (An analysis that only recomputed cached values from the series stored in the same file could not see an edited series; the final-digest CPU re-run does not either. The seed log is itself an accident guard, section 12.)

**Two analyses, and what "agree" means.** `confirm` writes a **provisional** result (`confirm-provisional.json`, with a summary: label, sub-cases, kept set, Both, SepB, SepP and every test's p, adjusted p, delta, Hodges-Lehmann shift and method), not a label. An independent analysis, written separately (another language), produces a summary in the same format; `r2b-analyze.ts agree --other <file>` compares them under **the tolerance, stated once**: equal label, sub-cases, kept set, Both, SepB and SepP; equal method of every test; every delta equal to 1e-9; every Hodges-Lehmann shift equal to 1e-9 relative; an exact p and its adjusted values equal to 1e-9 relative; a Monte Carlo p within five standard errors of the difference of two independent estimates, `5 sqrt(2 p (1 - p) / 10^6)`, plus `2 / 10^6` (a second implementation cannot reproduce the repo's counter-based draws without porting `cellBase` and `draw`, so it uses its own), and adjusted and intersection p-values within that bound times the family size. `agree` first re-runs the whole confirmation analysis on the files as they are then (freezes, hashes, log, CPU receipts, decision) and requires its summary and run digests to equal the stored provisional ones, so a run file, freeze or receipt changed since `confirm` makes the provisional result stale; a missing or non-finite number in the other summary is a disagreement (every comparison is written as "not within tolerance"). Any earlier `confirm-result.json` is removed whenever `confirm` or `agree` runs again, and only an agreeing `agree` writes a new one: it writes the receipt `confirm-agreement.json` and `confirm-result.json` (the label); on disagreement or staleness it writes the receipt with the problems and no label, and the more conservative label stands until the difference is settled. **The independent analysis is not built in this repository at Freeze 1**; the provisional summary and the tolerance are what it must meet. An unfinished batch (MEASUREMENT-LIMITED) or a known breach (INVALID) is reported by `confirm` without the CPU cross-check files; a complete batch with no breach needs them before any of the three substantive labels.

- `code_hash`: SHA-256 of a manifest of file contents (one `<sha256>  <path>` line per file, sorted by path, as R2's) over R2b's own file set: **the import closure, under `tools/mycelium`, of `r2b.ts`, `r2b-analyze.ts` and `r2b-freeze.ts`** (so `world.ts`, `gpu.ts`, `observe-r2b.ts`, `r2b-seeds.ts`, `r2b-protocol.ts`, `r2b-decide.ts`, `r2b-stats.ts`, `r2b-report.ts` and what they import of R2's: `r2-freeze.ts`, `r2-protocol.ts`, `observe.ts`), `tools/mycelium/RULES.md` (this protocol cites it), every `.ts` under `packages/schema/src` and `packages/sim-ref/src`, and `deno.json`. **Files the runs do not import are outside the freeze**: the lab viewer's `tools/mycelium/brush.ts` and `apps/lab/*`, and the unlogged CLIs `run.ts` and `r2.ts` (changed only to refuse R2b's worlds). `gpu.ts` is in the closure and is shared with the viewer work: a later viewer edit to `gpu.ts` or to a view accessor invalidates Freeze 1, so the viewer work must be committed before Freeze 1 and `gpu.ts` not touched after (stated in the freeze report). A test checks that the closure contains the files listed and excludes `brush.ts`. `FROZEN` lives in this directory; R2's `FROZEN` is not touched.
- **Freeze 1** (after review and implementation, before any dev run). Prerequisites, each recorded in the commit message and the report, not in `FROZEN`: the research kernel diff (`packages/*`) is empty; `RULE_VERSION` and every golden pin unchanged; `defaultConfig()` has none of `controller`, `physarumDeposit`, `blindTrailGradient`; the pinned digest in `tools/test/mycelium.test.ts` unchanged; `deno run -A --no-lock tests/deno/mycelium_gpu.ts` passes with the physarum and blind cases (CPU and GPU digests equal at every checkpoint); the physarum arm with `physarumDeposit` conserves matter and energy and replays from a checkpoint; each readout and the Moran, Otsu, Holm, Mann-Whitney and Cliff functions have hand-computed fixtures; `pnpm typecheck` and `pnpm test` pass; the timing gate (section 3) is done and its figure written in the FREEZE-1-RECORD block. `FROZEN` gets `protocol_sha256`, `code_hash`, the manifest, `plan: full|reduced` and `timing_steps_per_second: <figure>`, each once (the runner refuses a repeat, a plan that does not follow from the figure by section 3's mapping, or a plan or figure that differs from the block in this file; and the seed log refuses runs logged under another plan). - **Freeze 2** (after calibration, before any confirmation run). Append exactly one line `calibration_sha256: <hash of CALIBRATION.json>`. The runner **refuses every confirmation slot (5001+) unless** `FROZEN` holds Freeze 1 hashes matching the files on disk, `calibration_sha256` matches the bytes of `CALIBRATION.json`, and `CALIBRATION.json` carries the same code and protocol hashes and plan, a verdict that is not MEASUREMENT-LIMITED, a kept set from which a PASS is attainable and a matched `v`. `calibrate` refuses to rewrite `CALIBRATION.json` once `calibration_sha256` exists. Calibration and confirmation run under the same code.
- **Seed log** `docs/mycelium-r2b-2026-11-13/seed-log.jsonl`: every dev, matching, confirm and cpu-check run, `started` then `finished` (with the plan and the SHA-256 of the run file) or `invalid`; refuses all later runs after an `invalid` entry, a fresh slot outside confirm, or entries under two plans. Confirmation takes no `--arm` or `--seed`, **starts runs in a fixed order (arm major, slot ascending)** (with `--jobs` they start in that order and may finish out of order; log appends stay ordered because concurrency is inside one process), prints only digests, resumes after a crash (finished pairs skipped, a `series.json` without `finished` needs a decision recorded by hand).

## 11. What a PASS would and would not show

A PASS shows that, in this engineered 128 x 128 world over steps 50,000-100,000 and across independent worlds (16 per arm, or 12 in the reduced plan), copying controllers with mutation changes both how much trail is carried per particle and how that trail is arranged in space, relative to both fresh random controllers and a trail-follower whose deposit rate was matched to the target `T` (the median inherit seed, or under the 6.3 fallback the trail-on seeds, in which case the null deposits more than a typical inherit seed), with a medium-or-larger rank effect that survives one Holm correction over the readouts' intersection p-values. It stands on two non-exposed families (MASS and STRUCT), not on the lifetime readouts the null's fixed rules construct.

It does **not** show:
- adaptation, that any behaviour was selected (SIG is a descriptive fingerprint of a drifted or selected population, not a proof), individuality, or open-ended evolution;
- that the cause is heredity rather than the blocked arm being a different population (new random controllers each birth, mean age about 8,300 in R2, 60,000 in the pilot), or rather than selection on founders, which the dropped frozen arm would have separated; the positive controls show the readouts also move under gross physical changes (light x4, upkeep x1.5), so "differs" is not "differs because of heredity";
- that the null is a fair test of "any trail-follower": it is one memoryless gradient follower with a matched deposit rate, not matched in realised energy budget, eating or splitting, and its two fixed rules (always eat at full output, always split when able) are exactly what the exposed readouts measure; "matched" means matched on the target `T` and held within 25% of it, not that the null deposits what each inherit seed does;
- that the readouts are independent (section 4), or that the window is a steady state, or that 100k steps is long enough;
- that the controls show heredity-sized sensitivity: they are gross physical changes, so `kept` means "responds to a large perturbation".

NOT-SEPARATED and PHYSARUM-EXPLAINS are equally limited: 16 worlds per arm see only large effects, and the label does not say what the null does.

## 12. Known weaknesses (accepted, stated in advance)

- **The pilot predicts MEASUREMENT-LIMITED** (section 1): in three inherit smoke runs the trail was gone by the window, so `d` is far below 1/16 and Moran's I was invalid throughout. If the dev seeds are alike, calibration ends limited at 6.3 or 6.7 and no confirmation run happens. Nothing in the protocol was changed to avoid that.
- The matched deposit rate is a single choice (median over 20 dev seeds of a bimodal quantity); the fallback in 6.3 can still leave a null that deposits more than a typical inherit seed (flagged, not refused). `T` and `v` are fixed, not tuned for separation.
- Regime fractions, Moran's I below 500 units of trail, and exposed readouts are limited by construction as stated; `tau` comes from dev inherit alone, from the same seeds the keep rule uses, and is applied to all arms. A regime that the 20 dev seeds do not show cannot be classified.
- MI exists only for seeds whose every heavy census in W has at least 500 units of trail, and a PASS needs 8 or more such seeds in each arm. R2 and the pilot suggest the trail-off regime does not give them (a seed that dips under 500 units even once, or whose trail has a regime switch inside the window, has no MI), so a PASS may be unattainable at this horizon for a reason that has nothing to do with heredity. The rule is the price of an MI that trail mass cannot move: it removes the zero-fill and valid-only selection effects instead of bounding them.
- Mann-Whitney is a dominance test (section 8): a bimodal inherit between constant arms can hide a large difference; REG sees part of that, no distribution-level test is run.
- The GPU timing was unknown until the gate; 100k steps costs about 17.5M GPU steps and the plan can shrink to the reduced plan, never below 12 confirmation slots.
- 100k steps is a prediction from 20k data and three pilot seeds. If a confirmation seed trips the guard or inherit's regime keeps switching, the label says MEASUREMENT-LIMITED or the sub-case reports it; nothing is repaired afterwards.
- The independent second analysis (section 9) is specified and its comparison built, but not written; no label can be written before it exists.
- The embargo, the log, the file hashes and the hash chain are accident guards, not a defence against someone who writes their own script or edits the log.

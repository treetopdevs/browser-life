## Outcome and scope

Prepare an evidence-based route to the next M4 confirmation while testing the specific missing link for later milestones: whether a measured unit produces a separately viable descendant that reconstructs its organization. Retain current physical rules. Missing duplication is an implementation gap, but the completed assays do not identify it as the cause of the M4 result.

Run three tracks after a shared provenance check:

| Track | Question | Deliverable | Dependency |
|---|---|---|---|
| A: ancestry and transfer | Can we distinguish movement, genome-copy descent and repeated transmission? | Calibrated ancestry diagnostic and a bounded serial-transfer result, or a precise unavailability report | A1 controls before A2 transfer |
| B: M3-aligned mutation cohort | Do screen-observed role changes recur while preserving the intended three M3 outcomes? | New frozen nomination and two-position, 32-seed validation report | Independent of A |
| C: M4 amendment | Does treatment's activity increase more than neutral's over a declared window? | Calibrated endpoint, dated amendment, executable analysis and costed fresh-ensemble manifest | Independent of A and B; confirmation follows its own freeze |

Completion of this plan means the three deliverables and a decision record exist, including explicit inconclusive outcomes where warranted. It does not require a positive biological result. The fresh M4 ensemble is a subsequent, separately costed execution stage; a calibrated amendment must exist before it starts. M7 success is not an added prerequisite for M4.

The conceptual distinction between exploring existing behaviors and expanding the repertoire follows [Taylor's framework](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.01883). Here it translates into separate claims for variation, growth, functional dependency and repeated organizational transmission; no single proxy substitutes for all four.

## A1. Validate ancestry before assigning biological parenthood

**First deliverable:** an assay-side diagnostic that reports four separate quantities with their availability: old-material transport, genome-copy ancestry, connected-component identity, and organization measured at a fixed age. It must permit disagreement between them. Genome-copy colors do not supply the old-material measurement.

The current transport lottery copies one source genome into a destination while biomass can contain material from several sources. Reactions also synthesize new B from ambient A. An old-material tag is therefore not a complete genetic ancestry tag; a shared genome is not a unique parent identity; a new tracker ID is not proof of reproduction.

Implementation steps:

1. Add a pure initialization preflight using the actual step-0 census thresholds (bound mass 48, component mass 256). Enumerate all components, genomes and inventories before any GPU work. A purported single-root control must actually contain exactly one eligible root. The old founder disc fails this requirement and must not be reused silently.
2. Build synthetic cases with known executed transport/lottery outcomes: translation with no same-cell overlap; an asymmetric split; split/fusion while both prior identities continue; unrelated same-genotype founders; old material moving while another source wins the genome lottery; new biomass with little remaining original material; toroidal transport; and empty/zero-controller worlds. Require correct uncertainty as well as correct positive identification.
3. Prototype behaviorally neutral genome-copy labels in a separate mutation-off twin. Retain the exact mapping and source snapshot. Scientific recruitment requires continuous copy-source coverage from placement, or authenticated coverage starting before every eligible event; an event predating coverage is unavailable. A label applied after a birth cannot identify that birth's source. Compare unlabelled physical channels, genotype words excluding only the deliberately changed labels, flux/ledger totals and appropriately mapped observations. Do not require identical raw lineage hashes after intentionally relabeling them, or compare lineage-activity statistics across such twins as if untouched.
4. Validate the copy labels against known lottery winners in constructed cases. If labels affect physical evolution or cannot resolve the source, stop this approach. A new observational trace hook is permissible only with before/after bitwise physical parity and no added random draws; any actual rule change becomes a separate proposal. Do not infer exact ancestry from sparse snapshots alone.
5. Add a separate mass-conserving material-provenance audit using actual transport shares and reaction accounting. Validate it on known movement, synthesis, conversion and mixing cases with physical parity. The rules aggregate material; if they do not identify which source contributed a consumed quantum after mixing, report conservative attribution bounds or an explicitly conventional tracer, not invented exact ownership. Where this cannot be measured, mark old-material ancestry unavailable. Endpoint inventories alone provide a total incorporation bound, not spatial material ancestry.
6. Replay the short source-1 serial diagnostic covering the already observed births/fusion/fission around steps 150–250, with tracing begun before the event being attributed. Authenticate it against the saved serial-v1 physical continuation and original observation trace before calling these the same events. This is deliberately selected debugging data, not a fresh scientific replicate. Retain the unchanged old tracker alongside the new diagnostic and show exactly which links it resolves and which remain ambiguous.

**Acceptance:** all declared constructed cases have their expected links or expected ambiguity; every technical twin has identical physical evolution after masking only the intentional labels; all channel accounting is exact; the natural diagnostic retains every candidate and rejection. These checks validate an attribution method, not the existence of biological reproduction.

**Stop:** missing flow/winner information, parity failure or unresolved ancestry produces an explicit limited result. Track B and M4 design continue. Do not relax ancestry requirements until an old event becomes a desired offspring.

Proposed files: `tools/lib/foundation-copy-ancestry.ts`, `tools/foundation-copy-ancestry.ts`, matching tests/fixtures, and `docs/foundations-copy-ancestry.md`. Preserve the old observer and completed serial-v1 protocol/results.

## A2. A new serial-transfer protocol with two distinct claims

Proceed only after A1 passes its relevant controls. Freeze a new protocol and seed list; never amend serial v1 in place.

Start with the same source-1 late packet (catalog component 53) as an explicitly informed feasibility case. Preflight four stage-0 arms: intact donor, its ancestral founder expressed on the same physical packet geometry/inventory, a zero-controller version on that geometry, and an empty garden. The founder substitution is a genotype intervention, not evidence about the founder's naturally developed form. Identical matter/energy, placement and garden inputs are required for matched contrasts. The empty arm diagnoses background formation/contamination; it is not resource-matched to an inoculated arm.

Use mutation off and the existing 3,000-step garden schedule initially. Keep the initial recruitment window at steps 25–1,000 and record the original 25-step census view plus a finer diagnostic validated in A1. Select using information available at the event, not later survival:

- Choose the first separately detected component that satisfies the frozen packet requirements and validated source-copy link; break ties by a frozen hash rule. Keep direct-root overlap fission as a separate readout rather than making it the only possible link.
- Record all components, including copy-linked births, ambiguous/mixed ancestry, no event, invalid packets and technical failures. Purity is a limitation of the current packet API, not a claim that mixed collectives are irrelevant.
- If the chosen packet cannot be extracted or later dies, retain that result. Do not take the next-ranked packet. Copy the selected state into a fresh garden immediately; later events in the source garden do not veto or improve its outcome.
- Repeat selection through two fresh-garden transfers. Continue each launched garden to its horizon. Stage 0 has four arms; only donor and founder substitution continue, giving at most eight gardens in the first stream. No eligible packet yields an unavailable downstream stage, not a simulated extinction.

Report two outcomes separately:

1. **Packet propagation feasibility:** a copy-linked, physically separated packet persists, incorporates fresh resources and produces another eligible packet through the fixed transfer sequence. This remains a limited, intervention-dependent result.
2. **Organization reconstruction:** measure a predeclared non-mass spatial or functional trait in the incoming packet and developed output at matched ages. Require evidence that the output organization is built after transfer, rather than merely preserved from an already structured inoculum: for example, a propagule lacking the complete pattern develops it in demonstrably new structure, with validated material attribution/bounds and detector controls. The protocol must fix that criterion, trait, tolerances, age and comparisons on calibration data before the assay. A same-geometry founder control isolates a genotype intervention; it does not control for all inherited structure. If reconstruction cannot be distinguished from persistence, report structural persistence or an unavailable reconstruction endpoint. Neither mass, identical genomes nor split counts substitute, and a single clonal stream does not support a heritability slope.

Maintain extraction/reinsertion shams, full input/output inventories, physical-parity references and original observer records. Explicitly distinguish time since placement, time since detected separation and unknown biological birth time.

**Conditional replication:** if the single-source assay is interpretable and affordable, freeze one late donor per existing source history 1–5 using the original catalog ranking, plus two new assay streams per source. Keep unavailable sources without replacement. At most 80 gardens under the same four-initial/two-continuing-arm design; ten assay streams still represent only five historical sources. No probability-of-reproduction gate is claimed from this sample. Failure or ambiguity in the pilot is a decision point, not permission to search donors until one works.

Proposed files: a new `foundation-serial-transfer-v2` runner/analysis and protocol using validated packet/sham utilities. Integration tests must cover invalid initial geometry, ambiguous ancestry, failed chosen packet, extinction, unavailable stages, same-genotype unrelated controls, source drift and budget interruption.

## C. Design, calibrate and freeze the M4 endpoint amendment

This track can proceed while A and B run. Preserve endpoint 1, the old endpoint-2 verdict and the fully evaluated held-out negative. The new claim concerns a finite observation window; a bounded process with a real treatment advantage can satisfy it without establishing open-ended innovation.

### C1. Specify the estimand and statistical contract

Draft `docs/m4-growth-amendment-design.md` and a separate, versioned amendment module/analysis entry point. Do not change the frozen generated text or original analyzer semantics.

Default design for calibration: retain the 10^6-step horizon and current preset/schedule; use cumulative new activity computed with the provenance-compatible frozen threshold. For history i, propose

`g_i = [C_new_i(1,000,000) - C_new_i(500,000)] / 5`

in activity units per 100,000 steps. The half-window is an explicit candidate to calibrate, not an already adopted endpoint. The proposed treatment effect is the difference in mean `g` between treatment and neutral. A test on ranks targets a different quantity and cannot silently stand in for that mean contrast.

The primary agent must settle the following in the design document **before the pipeline pilot is opened**:

| Decision | Required resolution |
|---|---|
| Statistical unit and pairing | One history per condition/seed. Verify whether matched seeds actually share the intended initial state/random inputs. Choose paired differences or an independent-group estimator from design evidence, not whichever produces a smaller p-value. |
| Test and interval | Name an implementable test/interval for the chosen estimand, assumptions, ties/degenerate samples and deterministic analysis seed. Calibrate through the same production analysis path. The current Mann–Whitney implementation automatically changes from exact to normal above 60 combined observations; a larger sample must not silently change the declared method. |
| Practical effect | State a smallest relevant positive difference in the declared units, with a reason and precision target. Choose it from development/calibration information; never tune it to confirmation results. |
| Presets and multiplicity | Default: retain both gradient-m3 and spots-m3 with separate estimates. Freeze the exact per-preset and overall claim, all comparison slots and correction policy. A gradient-only claim would be an explicit narrowing, not recovery of both original preset results. |
| Milestone gate | Endpoint 1 and the amended endpoint 2 must both hold on the fresh required data. Old endpoint-1 positives do not count as fresh confirmation. Declare whether any broader held-out claim is in scope; if so, all its controls and fixed family remain required. |
| Missing/extinct histories | Keep completed extinctions and their actual activity curves. Technical incompleteness is unavailable, never zero or silent exclusion. Freeze a retry rule that repeats identical inputs for technical recovery without replacing biologically unfavorable seeds. |
| Sample size | Determine from power/precision calibration, not the former 20/10 allocation alone. Document effect/variance assumptions and attainable power; insufficient precision can stop confirmation. |

Threshold reuse is allowed only when its recorded rule, schema, metrics, preset, horizon, observation schedule and calibration-distribution identity remain compatible, with the recorded calibration pilot seeds disjoint from the new ensemble. Otherwise prepare and freeze an independent neutral calibration pilot with a separate cost estimate. Do not recalibrate using the confirmatory neutral arm.

### C2. Rehearse and challenge the actual analysis

Use old M4/foundations data as explicitly exploratory development data. Extend the existing null-generator infrastructure through the new analysis entry point rather than reimplementing its statistics in a calibration script.

The current `tools/nullcal.ts` deliberately uses `ignoreFrozenThreshold:true` and each synthetic ensemble's in-sample neutral quantile. Reusing that mode would not validate this amendment's frozen-threshold pipeline. The new calibration path must use the exact declared per-preset threshold, activity calculation, window and schedule while retaining an explicit synthetic provenance identity; do not forge real-pilot provenance or weaken production input checks. Direct curve fixtures test the estimator only and must be distinguished from end-to-end tests of activity construction plus the estimator.

Required cases: identical curves; equal-growth treatment/neutral with varying baseline levels, variance, ties and extinction; bounded treatment advantage; saturation within and beyond the window; periodic turnover; delayed improvement; missing conditions/histories; and a constructed effect on the selected statistic. Bounded genuine advantages are sensitivity cases for the new finite-window claim, not automatically false positives. Constructed physical worlds validate measurement behavior; synthetic curves validate analysis behavior; neither substitutes for the other.

Start with deterministic small fixtures. Benchmark a separate development sample, then freeze the independent synthetic validation manifest: **200 trials per declared scenario/preset stratum, initially at most ten strata**. At the old measured 8.97 seconds/trial, 2,000 trials take about five CPU hours; more strata, sample sizes or windows require an explicit revised estimate before execution. Record the parameter distribution of each stratum and do not pool away a failing case. Match seeds-per-condition to the actual proposed confirmation allocation, not the tool's default of 20.

Reserve distinct synthetic master seeds and trial identities for development and validation, rather than reusing the default master seed 1. In the existing generator, increasing replicate count creates a deterministic superset: it does not create independent new data to concatenate. Report uncertainty on rejection rates and power, not just point counts. Target the registered 0.01 significance scale and prespecify the acceptable calibration envelope before this validation set; 200 trials may be insufficient for a narrow rare-event claim. If precision is inadequate, stop with that result or freeze a costed extension before its outcomes. The existing numerically verified cap is 500 trials per stratum; larger analyses require a stable, independently tested interval implementation. Do not cycle through fresh synthetic sets until a preferred method passes.

After the design is frozen, rehearse the full pipeline on a small **development** matrix: two fresh seeds for treatment, neutral and no-mutation in each of two presets, at most twelve 10^6-step histories. This is an execution/provenance rehearsal with a strict budget, not a powered confirmation. If its outcomes motivate any design change, identify the revision and do not reuse this matrix as unseen validation.

## Decisions that follow the results

| Result | Next action | Claim retained |
|---|---|---|
| Copy ancestry is measurable, but the packet sequence cannot be interpreted | Improve only the failed ancestry/recruitment/control step; keep B and C moving | Transmission unresolved |
| Linked packets repeat the transfer cycle but no organization trait is informative | Design a trait/functional reconstruction assay | Repeated packet propagation under intervention, not organizational heredity |
| A role-changing nominee preserves M3 outcomes in both position panels | Select it prospectively for a functional challenge | Accessible variation in this assay, not repertoire expansion |
| Parent baselines fail or positions disagree | Report the conditional failures and investigate baseline sensitivity separately | No automatic mutation-harm or universal-neutrality claim |
| M4 design passes calibration with adequate precision | Freeze and run its fresh, costed ensemble as the next execution stage | Finite-window excess growth only |
| A feasible reproducing form exists but loses in the actual ecology | Test one ecological incentive or transmission mechanism with a matched comparator | Selection-level mismatch becomes testable |
| Current substrate misses fixed feasibility probes while a specified comparator succeeds at matched effort | Propose the smallest rule/representation change, with new identity/calibration | Evidence for that change, not impossibility of the old substrate |

Duplication, modules, symmetric mutation proposals, adhesion or a replacement substrate remain conditional experiments. Change one mechanism at a time; preserve the baseline and count search failures and effort. Hand construction and MAP-Elites can both supply feasibility controls, but neither establishes spontaneous origin and a failed bounded search does not prove impossibility.

If the next claim attributes the historical growth gains specifically to selection, add a separately budgeted time-shift control using genotypes from matched neutral-source histories. Express both origins in the same active-controller garden with matched physical inocula, retaining founder ancestry and fixed candidate selection. Running the neutral-source genotype under its original neutral reference controller would conceal its genetic phenotype. This control is not needed merely to report the already measured genotype effects and is outside the current eight-hour allocation.

## Bridge to M5–M7

These are follow-on protocols, not additional requirements silently added to M4 or jobs included in the eight-hour allocation.

- **M5 — causal ecological function:** choose a candidate interaction from measured flux/response evidence; compare recipient alone, recipient with producer-conditioned resources, removal of the proposed product, and restoration at matched matter/energy. Include a sham and unrelated conditioning control. Retain partner-dependent candidates even when they fail a solo viability gate. Predeclare an effect and uncertainty criterion before new outcomes. A role label alone is not the mechanism.
- **M6 — authenticated scaling:** count 100 verified independent histories across at least three device types against the existing milestone contract; device replays are verification, not new histories, and a migrating ring is one dependent unit. Reuse provenance/checkpoints. A later size/duration experiment needs matched density and input per area plus per-unit function, so more copies are not mistaken for more capable units.
- **M7 — collective reconstruction:** after an individual-level assay is interpretable, add mixed-genotype packet support and validate cohesion, material/genome mixing, viable propagules, composition reconstruction and repeated collective reproduction. Only then compare collective and constituent reproductive success. Adhesion-enabled rules/presets need separate registration/calibration. No membrane requirement is inserted merely because it is easy to count.

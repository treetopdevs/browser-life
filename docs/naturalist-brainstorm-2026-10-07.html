An AI naturalist

1Three products in one sentence

The roadmap line reads as one feature. It is three, each with its own hard part and its own riskiest assumption. The repo has material for the first and the third; the middle one has no baseline yet, and its one first look did not test it.

2The first look, 4 October 2026

The naive version has one first look. A vision model was shown a contact sheet of the sixteen ponds, an album of its own past picks and its notebook, and asked for the four ponds that looked most unlike anything it had seen. One world, one seed, sixteen cycles, one draw, against the arm's own rule and a seeded random picker. It asked for novelty and measured movement, so it did not test noticing, and one seed cannot separate the picker from the divergence of one chaotic history.

Three things it lacked. They are hypotheses about why, not an ablation: nothing has varied them one at a time. The ladder in section 6 would test the first two, and the detector's calibration the third.

aIt saw stills

Movement, heredity and lineage turnover are invisible in a contact sheet, and a thinned pond looks new. The movement overlay was withheld as a leak. For a naturalist it is the point.

bIt had no notion of normal

The only reference was its own album. Without a baseline, "unfamiliar" collapses into "different from what I picked before", and decay is always different.

cIt had no control

The lineage inspector decided every story sits beside a neutral twin, after phenotype-bin novelty flagged 40 of 70 neutral runs and failed calibration. The picker had no control at all.

3Noticing is a baseline problem

"Unfamiliar" has to mean departed from a stated normal. The picker compared the world with its own album. The repo can compare it with a control twin: a neutral or no-mutation replica forked from the same checkpoint, re-applying the same interventions. The lab's present replica verifies replay under the same config; a control twin is that mechanism under a control's config, and is new work.

4Seven shapes it could take

Two axes organise them. The eye: does it narrate numbers, detect against a baseline, or see across the ensemble? The hand: does it observe, propose, or steer? Lit dots are the recommended path. Hover or focus a dot for its one-liner.

5The recommended loop

Code notices. A model explains and proposes. A person says yes. The world replays. Every box but one already exists in some form.

6The first test: the blinded twin test

The riskiest assumption of the whole bet is that unfamiliar can be told from normal in these worlds. The cheapest honest test has two parts, scored separately, because a code detector and a model reading digests are two instruments. It uses the M4 replays already on disk. No lab work, no new simulation, no physics. The model's call budget, a few dollars, is paid compute and gets a yes first.

Second test: the picker redone, one input at a time

A ladder of model arms on the same world: the first look again; plus the movement overlay; plus per-pond numbers; plus a trait brief; and an inverse brief as a sanity check. Three seeds, two draws each, beside rule and random. Each rung isolates one of the first two hypotheses; the missing control is tested by part a instead. About 480 calls, which is paid compute and gets a yes first.

Set aside for now

The visitor docent, until a bench version has an eye. The live ensemble loop, until the coordinator knows the breed arm and picked runs can be segmented. Anything that selects what gets registered.

7Decisions open

Four choices change what gets built. They belong to the person running the project.

1Who is it for first?

The experimenter at the bench, or a visitor at cadence.garden. This note recommends the bench; the visitor version falls out of a working one.

2May it act, or only propose?

Proposals as run specs keep a person at the gate and fit the rule that paid compute needs a yes. Acting means a naturalist that forks and runs on its own.

3What counts as a first win?

A note the experimenter would have written, or one they would not have thought to write. Only the second deserves the name.

4May its taste be human taste?

A pretrained model's "interesting" is human interesting. Fine for a companion, a confound for a claim. Report it beside the measures, or start code-only.

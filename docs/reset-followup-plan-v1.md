## Gate O: can the missing quantity be observed usefully?

Derive bounds from actual integer transport and reaction order. For an amount q leaving a pool of size N with tagged amount in [L,U], the marginal outgoing interval is [max(0,L-(N-q)), min(q,U)]; preserve conservation constraints wherever practical. Different outgoing marginal upper bounds cannot be added as if independent exact allocations. Reaction synthesis, loss, polymer conversion and recycling must be explicit. Net mass changes alone do not identify gross synthesis or material age.

Distinguish continuously retained pre-existing bound material, atoms that were formerly bound and later recycled, and newly assembled bound material. If an observable cannot be bounded, report unavailable. Neither new mass nor newly assembled polymer establishes newly constructed organization. Genome ancestry is a separate passive copy path, including mutations; identical genomes do not identify material origins.

Before reading biological outcomes, run known-history controls: pure retention, complete replacement at fixed total mass, partial mixing, selective loss, synthesis followed by loss, B-to-P conversion, recycled material, repeated splitting/mixing and transport rounding. Exhaustive small-state allocation or independent explicit tokens must lie inside all reported intervals. Check conservation and reject invalid inputs. On executed reference worlds, passive observation must leave full physical state and mutation events identical to an uninstrumented twin. Mutation stays enabled; a forced-mutation control checks the path, while realistic controls use normal mutation settings.

Soundness is mandatory. Informative measurement means the controls can distinguish predominantly retained bound structure (at least 80% continuously retained) from predominantly replaced structure (at most 20%) without choosing a mixing convention. These are engineering contrast controls, not a definition of reproduction. Test endpoints after 1, 10, 100, 1,000 and 1,100 steps, including an actual-rule mixed world, and report interval widths and unresolved cases rather than dropping them. The intended contact and recovery horizon is 100 plus 1,000 steps, with tagging before contact; shortening it after observing interval collapse cannot establish a pass for that use. Establish extreme-control histories independently through explicit valid token allocations or an exhaustive oracle; the observer output cannot define their ground truth. Tokens witness compatible histories, not simulator-defined unique molecular identities.

Stop downstream biological assays if the valid bounds admit both contrast explanations at the recovery horizon. Before stopping, independently check whether that failure is avoidable through a cheap aggregate conservation bound or corrected implementation. Report algorithmic looseness separately from true non-identifiability. Passing easy unmixed controls alone does not pass Gate O. Reviewer and root record the gate result; no threshold tuning on fresh evaluation cases.

An intermediate interval such as [0.3,0.7] is not a failure to distinguish the two extreme controls. A deliberately adversarial abstract fixture alone cannot establish failure on all intended physical supports: report where the counterexample applies. Even if Gate O passes its controls, each later natural case that admits both explanations remains ambiguous; passing controls never resolves that case automatically.

## Gate C: small contact and recovery, conditional on O

Use same-genotype and different-genotype pairs with contact and matched separated controls. Freeze the exact geometry, placement, light/resource matching, RNG pairing, candidate selection, mass/support definitions, recovery manipulation and functional horizons before evaluation. Separation controls must not accidentally change available area/resources or create a new physical barrier. Measure and disclose unavoidable geometry effects. Mutation remains enabled in all biological arms.

Define the material observation support independently of the surviving genome. Genome replacement must not erase the region being assessed. Prespecify merged/split support unions and unresolved identities. Include genome-bearing spread across initially genome-free bound material as a control against mistaking colonization for reproduction of a viable entity. Same-genotype comparisons still require passive copy-origin tags.

Use naturally occurring candidates from the earlier fixed windows for development, retaining source-history clustering and ambiguous identities. The 592 flags are not unique births. Deduplicate or report clusters before selection. Development may choose workable support and functional measures; those cases cannot count as fresh evaluation. Freeze code, protocol, inputs and a disjoint fresh-case rule before evaluating new cases. New RNG seeds on the same selected candidate are replicates, not independent fresh entities.

Report four separate quantities: genome-copy replacement; retained pre-existing bound material (and recycled-origin material if available); demonstrable new nonmass organization; subsequent function under recovery. Survival, metabolic maintenance, reconstruction and another supported separation are separate outcomes. Failure to observe a second birth in a short horizon is censored, not proof of inability. Analyze all chosen cases, including failures and unresolved identity. No population-level or M4 confirmatory claim follows from a small developmental assay.

## Decision

- Supported functional reproduction: design a diverse-founder investigation using criteria audited against M5–M7 held-out endpoints before search.
- Measured transmission failure undermining otherwise viable entities: propose a narrow inheritance intervention with matched baseline, separate dated rule decision and fresh cohort if physics changes.
- Ambiguous measurement or identity: record the precise limitation and reconsider the operational entity definition before changing physics. An unavailable measurement does not select a mechanism.

Final delivery includes the consolidated dated record, executable observer and controls, reviewed O decision, conditional contact results or explicit gated disposition, resource receipt and intervention decision. No unexecuted stage is represented as completed.

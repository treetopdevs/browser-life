Engineered life · seed 1201 · 26 October 2026

Seed 1201: uniform scarcity kept turning over; founder patches died out.

We gave the same growing, reproducing shapes three food supplies. The world with scarce food spread across the whole map was alive and turning over at 3,000 ticks. With the same offered budget concentrated around founders, the population disappeared at tick 2,040.

Stop after the first seed. The plan required the patch world to survive to 3,000 before extending. It failed that prerequisite. No seeds 1202 or 1203, no food redesign, and no longer run were earned.

The uniform world is an interesting observation: it still has births and deaths late in the run, spends little time at the population ceiling, and has smaller occupied area than the original. Its 94 late births are matched by 94 deaths. This is continuing turnover, not evidence that survival improved or that changes will continue indefinitely.

At the fixed 1,500-tick view, the original occupies 693 cells, uniform 576 and patches only 210. The patch world has larger empty gaps and a few separated family groups; by its terminal frame no bodies remain. At 3,000, the uniform world still has separated groups and depleted trails, while the original is fuller. These are descriptive visual differences in one seed, not a successful patch treatment.

Equal offers did not mean equal usable food

Both scarce worlds offer 768 food units every five ticks, with identical initial reserves. At the common tick 2,040, each had offered 313,344 units. The uniform world accepted 229,313; the patch world accepted only 83,086. Offers landing on full tiles overflow and are lost. Accepted food also depends on grazing: depleted populations leave more full tiles, increasing overflow. Location and accepted supply differed here; this run does not isolate which mechanism caused extinction.

We deliberately supplied patches around known starting locations. Reproduction, inheritance, mutation, the age rule and the population ceiling were already engineered. None of these ingredients is claimed to have emerged from nothing.

Why no rescue revision?

The agreed revision applied only if both scarce worlds died before their first children, or both stayed ceiling-bound. Uniform survived; patches reached generation 8 before extinction. Neither trigger applies. The uniform result is retained as a possible future direction, without moving the gate after seeing the result.

What changes when one food band is split?

The accepted stationary control feeds columns 0–47. The new condition feeds two stationary bands, columns 0–23 and 48–71. Both cover 4,608 tiles, with 384 scheduled food offers per band each pulse. Food begins on every tile and remains after each pulse.

Recorded states only. Once published, this page will pair the accepted baseline with one new seed-1203 split-band recording. It opens paused and visits saved frames every 20 ticks plus the actual terminal tick if needed. It does not simulate, interpolate, or continue a world. Seed 1203 was chosen because an accepted historical contiguous-band recording exists; this is one selected-seed comparison, not a representative random sample. Equal offered food and area do not imply equal usable energy.

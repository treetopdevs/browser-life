## Reading these observations

Density and nutrient are proxies in an engineered open-energy field toy. Walls are supplied reinforcement; this is not evidence of organisms, heredity, adaptation, or evolution. Increasing component count does not mean division. Component counts use periodic four-neighbor connectivity and density ≥0.35; only regions of four or more cells enter the main count. Tiny regions are retained separately. Late-change means average the ten intervals ending at ticks 2100 through 3000 for runs that remained live; an extinct run is labeled with its extinction tick and is never entered as zero activity. Late mask flips are the mean fraction of cells crossing the 0.35 threshold per interval, averaged over those same ten intervals.

The JSON keeps the full scheduled history for every seed, exact extinction rows, and frozen scheduled rows after extinction. Every tick checked finite [0,1] field values, wall-disabled invariants, both per-tick diffusion residual bounds, clamp bounds, cumulative density accounting, and no density revival after extinction.

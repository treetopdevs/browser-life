# Independent motif gallery screenshot review

Inspected the actual seed-1201 screenshots `runs/motifs/browser/tick-0.png`, `tick-1502.png`, and `tick-3004.png`. All twelve compositions are present at their captured comparison ages. This review used still images only; the WebM was not continuously viewed.

## Readability and layout

The desktop gallery is styled and coherent: twelve consistently sized square fields in three columns, ingredient badges, explicit initial-composition disclaimer, shared controls, per-card statistics, and history strips. All initial fields look matched. At native 1440px width each card has sufficient spatial area to distinguish dispersed particles from compact clumps. A full-gallery screenshot is very tall and its fit-to-screen rendering makes metrics small; focused views are useful for close temporal inspection. This is a presentation tradeoff, not a missing stylesheet or broken geometry. These stills do not independently verify phone layout, keyboard use or favorites/export behavior.

## Spatial observations

The strongest visible difference is chase versus gather. By ticks 1502 and 3004, gather recipes have concentrated particles into compact clumps near the supplied food layout. The number, family colors and placement of some clumps differ, particularly with travelling or seasonal habitats, but the overall structure remains a food-associated clump pattern. Still images alone cannot establish whether an individual clump is stationary, continuously turning over, or moving between captures.

Chase recipes retain a more distributed mixture of individual particles and small clusters. Their arrangements visibly differ between 1502 and 3004. Chase-independent-travelling remains broadly dispersed, while chase-independent-seasonal has several conspicuous compact family clusters within a more open field. Chase-aligned variants show a mix of small clusters and scattered particles, rather than gather's uniform food-centered pattern. No pair of stills establishes persistent shape identities, replication or evolution.

The fixed gallery is therefore informative as a composition comparison: interaction choice strongly changes spatial organization, and habitat/coordination alter the displayed arrangements. It is not evidence that twelve distinct autonomous structures have emerged. Food markers must remain visible because they explain much of the gather pattern.

For a brief closer look, chase-independent-travelling and chase-independent-seasonal provide a useful dispersed-versus-clustered comparison; gather-independent-travelling provides a contrasting food-associated clump example. These are descriptive inspection choices from the complete gallery, not ranked winners or evidence of adaptation.

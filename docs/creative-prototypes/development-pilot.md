# Developmental programs pilot evidence

Fixed descriptive evidence for the explicit inherited developmental toy, generated by Node 24.2.0 on darwin/arm64.

## Method

Before the fixed batch, the runner tests each supplied genome in the same single-founder fixture at tile 4656, heading east, with food at 40 per tile. It records the founder's body size at every tick through 240, first birth tick, and normalized footprint at tick 144. The assay passes only when all three founders reach at least 18 cells, each gives birth by tick 240, and their tick-144 footprints are distinct. The assay checks that the specified ingredients operate; it does not test emergence or adaptation.

The fixed batch is seeds 1201, 1202, 1203 crossed with mutation off and on through 3000 scheduled ticks. Each condition is run with an independently initialized full repeat. Full snapshots are compared every live tick; snapshots are retained at scheduled ticks 0, 1000, 2000, 3000. Every live tick checks integer bounds, body connectivity, unique occupancy, population/body caps, parent and genome mutation truth, no revival, and an exact zero energy-ledger residual. Census records are retained every 20 ticks and at exact extinction. After extinction the comparison clock continues through the fixed horizon while the world state remains frozen.

All six outcomes remain in the record. If all worlds are extinct before tick 500, the fixed slice stops at its specified horizon and is not extended or tuned. Parent ordering, age, and reproductive thresholds are engineered properties of this model.

Source SHA-256 values were captured before the assay and verified again at the end. The JSON stores the exact rules, genome programs, runtime, all census rows, birth and lineage records, and complete milestone snapshots. No survival benefit or adaptation claim is made.

## Interpretation limits

This is a descriptive record of one engineered toy, three seeds, and two mutation settings. Connected bodies, growth, reproduction, inheritance, food supply, and mutation are explicit model mechanisms. The results do not show natural emergence, adaptation, selection benefit, or general survival. Differences between mutation settings are not a survival-benefit estimate. The age and size thresholds, growth queue, founder ordering, and cap create fixed demographic biases; those rules were not adjusted after seeing the batch.

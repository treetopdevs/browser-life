# Motif composition pilot evidence

Fixed descriptive batch, grammar version 1, generated by Node 24.2.0 / zlib 1.3.0.1-motley-780819f.

## Method

The complete matrix contains all 12 canonical recipes crossed with seeds 1201, 1202, and 1203. Each baseline and an independently initialized full replay advances through tick 3000 or freezes at exact extinction. Baseline state hashes are compared at tick 0, every 100 ticks, and exact extinction. The replay must match at every recorded point. Each world is checked every tick for finite bounded positions and energy, population at or below 240, consistent family counts, and energy-ledger residual at most 1e-6.

At tick 1500, a separately initialized perturbation replay must match the baseline full snapshot before the smallest living particle ID is moved by +1/12 in x with torus wrapping. The ordinary tick-1500 sample is pre-move. The evidence stores separate before and after records at tick 1500, then paired comparisons every 100 ticks through 3000. Every paired record includes histogram total variation and a full snapshot hash comparison. If a world becomes extinct, its state freezes and its actual tick remains visible.

Initial geometry hashes exclude inherited attraction rows. Across the grammar, seed, positions, velocities, ages, lifespan, energy and food geometry are shared; the initial attraction traits intentionally vary with interaction. The two still/aligned-or-independent preset controls compare full base snapshots against the existing pursuit or flocks preset. The preset controls validate the composition mapping and observation path, not new physics.

Occupancy uses 12×12 periodic bins and three families, stored as 432 integer counts at each baseline and perturbation sample. TV globally normalizes by each world's population and uses the observer's empty-population convention. The compression proxy is gzip level 9 over those 432 count bytes, with the 255 cap applied; it is coarse fixed-resolution compressibility and largely tracks occupied cells. The record contains scalar trajectories, histogram counts, state hashes, and paired perturbation evidence, without per-tick snapshots.

## Interpretation limits

These results describe this fixed batch and the existing engineered toy model. A motif names initial composition: individual attraction rows can mutate and be inherited, while coordination and habitat remain supplied recipe settings. Chase does not mean predation. Seasonal and still worlds have the same offered food renewal total; capacity clipping can change realized input. Population survival, texture change, and gzip size do not establish adaptation, evolution, increasing complexity, criticality, or general survival. Post-perturbation divergence combines motion and demographic changes; because births consume sequence-based random draws, different birth paths can desynchronize subsequent random draws. Equal TV does not imply equal full state. No monotonic-growth assumption or watchability score is used.

The batch is complete through 3000 ticks (or exact extinction) and is not extended before review. Node and zlib versions plus source hashes are recorded in the JSON. Regenerate the Markdown from that JSON with `--check`; `--replay-check` reruns the fixed batch and compares the full output.

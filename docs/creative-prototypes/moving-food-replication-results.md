# The moving-food screen repeats in both new seeds

Seeds 1202 and 1203 both pass the unchanged 3,000-tick screen: survival, multiple founder families, continued births and deaths, little time at the population ceiling, and visible changes in occupied body arrangements. Including the previously frozen 1201 pass gives 3/3 descriptive passes. The new evidence is 2/2; 1201 was not rerun.

Each arm was offered 460,800 food units. Moving-band accepted less than uniform in both new seeds. It preserves more families in 1202 and fewer in 1203; it has fewer distinct programs in 1202 and one more in 1203. These tradeoffs do not establish superiority. A three-body surviving family in 1202 is still fragile.

Moving 1202 has births/deaths 91/79,85/93,87/89 in the three late 500-tick windows; ceiling occupancy 24,38,17ticks. Moving 1203 has 99/86,77/83,89/91 births/deaths and 15,17,11 ceiling ticks. Every window meets the frozen thresholds. Both uniform controls also remain alive and changing.

Occupied-body images show central and right-side groupings shifting, opening gaps and reforming. In1202, the upper-right arrangement clears while a broad middle-right cluster develops. In1203, a right-middle vertical arrangement becomes a higher diagonal chain and bottom-right occupancy grows. Both seeds show concrete differences from their same-age uniform controls at all three reviewed times, plus changes from 2,500 to 3,000. Parent judgments are unblinded and qualitative. The [uniform between-seed reference](moving-food-replication-media/uniform-between-seeds.png) also shows large ordinary differences without changing the rule; distinctness alone does not prove that movement improves the world.

Stop here: both scheduled replication seeds are complete, no extra seed or longer horizon was run, and no rules were changed. Retain the moving-band design as a promising prototype. This establishes survival, turnover and visible variety through 3,000 under an engineered replenishment rule, not robust evolution, adaptation, food tracking or superior survival. Food remains where it was deposited; concentration and motion are not separated by this comparison.

A useful next separately reviewed slice would compare this moving band with the same broad band held still, at one seed and the same 3,000-tick horizon. That would reveal whether motion contributes useful visible behavior beyond concentrated food, before adding another mechanism. This is a proposal, not an authorized run within this frozen slice.

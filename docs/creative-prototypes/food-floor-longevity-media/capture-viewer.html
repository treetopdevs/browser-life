What follows the decline?

At 3,000 ticks, this food-floor world had fallen to 16 bodies. Continue the same seed and unchanged rules to 6,000, or actual extinction, to see whether it rebounds, stays sparse, or dies out.

Saved states only. Ticks 0–3,000 exactly reproduce the accepted historical record. Only ticks 3,001 onward are new observations. This page shows one world, with no invented control tail. It opens paused and never simulates or interpolates. The declining trajectory was selected deliberately; it is not an unbiased seed.

Parent visual comparison found concrete differences at all three planned late times: the moving world forms a tall grouping in the right half and opens a large left-side gap, while uniform remains more dispersed. The moving arrangement also changes from 2,500 to 3,000. These judgments were recorded before overlap diagnostics in the [SHA-bound pair receipt](moving-food-index.json).

The smallest test is complete and stops here. This is one varied trajectory under the whole moving-band design, not proof of tracking the food, improved fitness, or open-ended evolution. The smallest remaining family has only two bodies; persistence is still fragile.

Both arms were offered 460,800 food units. Uniform accepted 309,822 and overflowed 150,978; moving-band accepted 283,182 and overflowed 177,618. Existing food was never removed when the region moved, and both started with the same reserves. Equal offered food is not equal usable energy. A stationary-band control was not run, so concentration and movement are not separated.

The uniform arm exactly matches the accepted baseline through 3,000: all 151 sample/state/frame projections and all 3,000 tick rows, plus the available full checkpoint at zero. Both arms match independent repeats, have zero conservation residual and retain all observations. Historical files remain untouched.

Next proposal: repeat this unchanged paired test at seeds 1202 and 1203 under a new reviewed plan, preserving the 3,000-tick horizon, width and speed. A second or third seed can reveal whether the attractive first outcome is fragile; no parameter sweep or automatic longer run is proposed.

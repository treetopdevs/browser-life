## Exact mechanics
Extend ParticleEnvironment with {kind:'seasonal', period:600|1800}. Here period means ticks per phase; a full cycle takes three phases. At step t, phase = floor((t-1)/period)%3. Row (floor(patchIndex/3)-phase+3)%3 receives renewal multiplier [1.75,1,0.25]. The first phase covers ticks1 through period; next phase starts at period+1. Patches retain positions, stock and radius. Apply ordinary capacity clipping and feeding; do not reset reserves, relocate organisms, reseed or rescue extinction. The three equal-size rows always offer the same total renewal as still food (9 times recipe.foodRenewal), but realized renewal may differ because full patches cannot accept food. Still/travelling paths preserve every old arithmetic operation and RNG draw. Bump toy model version to4; accept canonical picker histories from versions2,3,4 with an explicit migration notice, all still-world recipes.

## Measurement definitions
Family losses are the first exact tick with zero members. A sampled low-count rebound starts at a census with 1–9 members and ends at a later census with at least20; record both ticks and counts, and do not count overlapping episodes. Extinct families cannot rebound. Per-family population minima/maxima and extinction ticks are measured every tick; stock/position/census histories remain sampled. These thresholds describe visible demographic swings, not fitness. Existing food-seeking targets the nearest patch, not the richest patch, so migration toward a wet row is not guaranteed or specially engineered here.

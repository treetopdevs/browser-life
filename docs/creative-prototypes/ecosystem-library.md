# A local home for the creative worlds

Open `/playground/` locally to browse fourteen entries: live particle and field worlds, the recipe picker, recorded ecosystem observations, and the stopped vision diagnostic. The labels distinguish those uses rather than presenting every page as a successful evolution experiment.

The existing ecosystem is now in the same checkout at `/ecosystem/`. It retains the exact reviewed v4 model: reproduction, inherited attack/defense, mutation, costly protection, refuges, staged rivals and seasons. Manual buttons still intervene directly. The page starts paused, and returning from a hidden tab does not silently resume it. Model source matches immutable commit f1f98f39 byte for byte; this integration changes access and presentation, not simulation rules.

At `/ecosystem/archive/`, all three earlier20,000-tick runs are available with smaller copies of their movies, population histories and within-family trait histories. Seeds1101 and1102 retain five-family competition;1103 settles into a Protected-heavy three-family regime. Late mutations mostly revisit known combinations. The archive does not claim continual invention, adaptation or indefinite persistence.

These are historical observations brought forward, not a new20,000-tick batch. Current verification checks file provenance, all201 censuses per seed, trait-bin arithmetic, summary reaggregation and movie metadata. A new bounded replay verifies the three3,000-tick censuses with the unchanged model. The movies are sampled and lossy; their hashes do not independently prove correspondence between every pixel and a census.

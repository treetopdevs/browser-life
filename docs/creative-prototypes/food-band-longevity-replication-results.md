# Both designs keep changing on two more seeds

Both moving and stationary food bands passed the frozen 6,000-tick screen on seeds 1202 and 1203. All four worlds stayed alive with two founder families, births and deaths in every late window, and visible rearrangement of occupied bodies from 5,500 to 6,000. The two new pairs therefore give **both-pass: 2; moving-only: 0; stationary-only: 0; neither: 0**, with each mode passing 2 of 2 valid pairs. The screen did not distinguish the designs; this is not evidence of equality or a motion advantage.

The clearest new story is the stationary seed-1202 minority: its lilac family grew from 2 bodies at tick 3,000 to 22 at 6,000. The same seed's moving world ended much more unevenly, with 3 gold and 41 lilac bodies, and lost its mint family by the tick-4,340 sample. This is a descriptive rebound and seed-sensitive family balance, not proof of adaptation or improved fitness.

Seed 1203 remained active in both designs. Moving's dense upper cluster gave way to a diagonal middle group and broad lower branches. Stationary's upper group dispersed and new middle and lower groups developed. These are changes in occupied tiles, not merely recoloring. In seed 1202, moving's lower chain broke into separated groups; stationary's lower-middle chain cleared while a lower-left cluster appeared. Parent judgments were not blind: both seeds' numerical outcomes and historical prefix media were known before the receipts were written.

No numerical window failed. Both seed-1203 worlds end at 48 bodies but spent only 4 and 12 ticks respectively at that ceiling in the final 500 ticks; endpoint saturation conceals ongoing turnover. Minority families can still be fragile under this permissive screen.

First observed absent-family samples, at 20-tick resolution: moving 1202 mint at 4,340 (new tail); stationary 1202 mint at 2,740; moving 1203 mint at 1,340; stationary 1203 gold at 2,680. The latter three were already in the known prefixes. Other families remain present at every sampled time. Accepted food totals were 612,938 / 570,522 for moving / stationary 1202 and 581,503 / 576,193 for 1203, from 921,600 offered per arm. Equal offered budgets do not mean equal usable food.

Historical seed 1201 also passed both arms, but is separate evidence and was not rerun here. Only ticks 3,001–6,000 in these four arms are new; every 0–3,000 prefix exactly matches its own accepted record. Two scheduled paired attempts ran with independent repeats and no retries, invalid pairs or extra seeds. All new trials stop here.

Keep both designs as interesting prototypes. More unchanged replication is unlikely to reveal a richer visual vocabulary by itself. A separate next proposal is to split the stationary feeding region into two separated 24-column bands, preserving its total replenished area and offered budget, against the current contiguous 48-column band. That could encourage distinct colonies and local recoveries without returning to the earlier very sparse food patches. Start with one seed and a new reviewed plan; do not run, tune or extend that idea as part of this result. No claim of open-ended evolution, causal motion benefit or lasting coexistence is warranted.

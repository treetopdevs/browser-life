Uniform-food endurance · seed 1203 · frozen horizon 9,000

A tougher test of a fragile family.

Seed 1203 begins with only five purple bodies against 32 mint bodies at tick 6,000. It was deliberately selected to stress family persistence; it is not a representative random seed. This continuation asks whether that fragile group remains part of the changing world for another 3,000 ticks.

Recorded states only. The replay opens paused and uses saved samples at 20-tick intervals, plus the exact terminal frame if extinction falls between samples. It does not interpolate, simulate, or continue the world. A family-only loss counts as a diversity-stress failure, not as a dead world.

Overall decision

All three worlds keep changing at 9,000

Seed 1203 passed and earned seeds 1201 and 1202; both also passed. Each retains two families, ongoing births and deaths, and changing occupied regions. Stop at the frozen 9,000-tick horizon. This supports a lively baseline, not indefinitely increasing complexity.

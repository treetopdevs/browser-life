Does the little world keep changing?

Three unchanged v4 worlds, extended to 20,000 ticks. Each exactly reproduces its earlier 3,000-tick census. No tuning, replanting or added interventions. The original staged arrivals and automatic seasons remain enabled.

Still active, increasingly familiar: seeds 1101 and 1102 keep five-family competition and large late population swings. Seed 1103 settles into a Protected-heavy three-family community. Mutation continues, but most late trait combinations have appeared before.

Animations sample every 25 ticks and play at 20 frames/second (500 ticks per second). They show population fronts and turnover, not every individual event. Colors follow founding families; brighter cells have greater defense. Refuges and drought rules are active but their outlines are omitted here.

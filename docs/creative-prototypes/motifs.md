# Motif workshop

Open `/particles/motifs/` locally to compare twelve combinations of three ingredients:

- **Initial interaction:** chase another family and avoid the third, or gather with your own family.
- **Coordination:** move independently, or align with nearby particles.
- **Food:** fixed patches, travelling patches, or seasonal renewal.

Each card shows its initial composition. Offspring inherit attraction traits with occasional mutations, so those traits may drift away from the starting recipe. Coordination and food rules remain supplied settings. Chasing does not eat other particles. This is a small grammar built from existing particle mechanics, not a claim that every life-like rule family can be mixed interchangeably.

The worlds share a seed and comparison clock. Pause, step, or reset them together; choose up to three favorites to include their full observations in a download. Favorites identify recipes and survive a reset. Downloading saves observations, not a resumable simulation or a new evolutionary lineage. The existing picker handles recipe ancestry separately.

Population and motion tell you whether a world remains busy. The spatial-change number compares family occupancy in a fixed12×12 grid over the displayed interval. It can change because of movement, births or deaths, and can stay low while individual particles move inside a bin. The command-line report also shows coarse compressibility and a paired displacement test. None is an evolution detector or a universal measure of interestingness.

The twelve recipes share initial positions, velocities, ages, energy and food. Their initial inherited attraction rows differ deliberately. Food renewal offers the same total budget, but patch capacity can change how much enters each world. Do not read population differences as a clean causal test of one biological trait.

The fixed three-seed comparison kept all36 populations alive. Chase remained more spatially active than gather. Adding alignment plus seasonal food produced an active new chase combination, but independent chase already performed similarly on this coarse measure. Aligned seasonal gathering lost one family in one seed. These are useful contrasts for watching, not a ranked evolutionary success.

# A stationary food band stays varied in both new seeds

Both newly scheduled stationary worlds stayed alive and visibly changed through 3,000 ticks. Both moving controls also pass, exactly reproducing their historical trajectories. This permissive screen did not distinguish the arms; it does not establish equality or a benefit from motion.

All four arms had births and deaths in each late window and stayed below the frozen ceiling threshold. Each also passed its own occupied-body change judgment from 2500 to 3000. In 1201, stationary groups fragmented and expanded toward the upper-left; in 1203 a dense middle-left column cleared while upper- and lower-middle groups developed. The paired replay and body-only images show those changes.

The frozen aggregate is **2/2 new stationary screen passes**. Historical seed 1202 passed separately, giving a descriptive 3/3 stationary total. The moving controls are predetermined regressions against PR 103/104, not new independent successes. Visual rejudgments were not blind to historical moving media. Minority persistence remains uncertain: the earlier stationary 1202 ended with only 2 minority bodies; these two new stationary runs ended with 6 and 12 in their smaller surviving families. The gate requires merely nonzero families at 3000, not enduring diversity.

Stationary accepts slightly less food in both new pairs. Population and program counts differ in opposite directions across seeds. These are descriptions, not fitness or superiority measurements. The fixed band replenishes only half the torus; moving food reaches other territory, so any eventual stationary failure would mix motion and coverage effects.

Exactly the two scheduled pairs ran, once each with independent repeats. Both moving histories matched all ten frozen payload fields, including 151 saved samples/frames, 3000 rows and five snapshots. No repair attempt, additional seed, rule change or horizon extension occurred. Parent judgments were recorded before overlap diagnostics and do not depend on overlap scores.

The stop rule is reached. Retain both prototypes. The next proposal is a separate reviewed longevity slice: unchanged moving and stationary seed 1201 to 6000, requiring its 0–3000 prefix to match these records and freezing new late-window/visual stops before running. That would test whether continued turnover preserves variety longer; it is not authorized as an automatic extension of this experiment and has not been run here.

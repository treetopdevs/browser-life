## Plan-review resolutions

- Late window is steps5,001–6,000 inclusive, measured after every executed step. Record executed late-step count and living late-step count separately; if the world freezes before this window, all late means are null, not fabricated zero observations. Summaries: mean of per-step meanSpeed and meanAbsVz over executed living steps, mean occupied125 bins and mean occupied5 depth bands over those same steps, min/max/final population, and total births/deaths delta over the actually executed window. Late cap fraction is executed living steps with population exactly240 divided by living-step count. A world dying during the window retains its extinction tick and partial-window coverage; do not compare partial coverage as a full-window result. Early and late occupancy/motion appear separately so initial random spread cannot pass as sustained motion. These are descriptive observations, not success gates.
- Display playback1x=60 simulation ticks/second,4x=240. Accumulator accepts at most100ms elapsed per visible frame, executes at most16 whole ticks, then discards excess whole-tick debt while retaining the fractional remainder. Pause/reset/hidden clears accumulator and last timestamp. Model physics always advances by integer ticks; rendered speed never changes per-tick rates. Camera orbit is never automatic. UI may render slower than requested playback; actual tick label is authoritative.
- Claude review resolutions: invariant failure writes a separate failure record, exits nonzero and invalidates the entire batch; no success artifact replaces a failed batch. The depth metric name is `occupiedZBands`; initial randomness is not evidence of late motion. Keep the explicitly specified60/240Hz accumulator: fixed refers to physics ticks, not refresh rate. Render may import types and pure geometry helpers, but never mutate or step a world. Always-paused start is intentional; no reduced-motion exception or automatic camera motion. Label the live chart as recent3,000 ticks, while pilot samples call census directly for the full horizon.
- Norm uses Math.sqrt(dx*dx+dy*dy+dz*dz) consistently for interaction distance, velocity cap, food seeking, movement cost and meanSpeed. Same-runtime replay only; browser clips are actual browser trajectories, not claimed bitwise matches to Node. Pin initialization draws x,y,z,vx,vy,vz,age,lifespan; birth draws offsets x,y,z then lifespan. Mutationoff does not consume mutation stream. RULES.version1/sourceVersion4. Invariant/ledger checks every executed tick, maximum residual recorded. Food overlaps and first-patch priority are deliberate; nearest-center seeking may produce two depth layers and must be reported, not hidden. Bin index=floor(coordinate*5). Census additionally reports meanAbsVx and meanAbsVy; pilot reports z-to-horizontal absolute-speed ratio meanAbsVz/((meanAbsVx+meanAbsVy)/2), null if denominatorzero. Descriptive only.
- Camera contract: exported Camera={yaw:number,pitch:number}, radians. Default yaw .65, pitch -.35; yaw slider[-180,180]deg, pitch[-75,75]deg. Perspective camera centered on(.5,.5,.5), distance2.5, verticalFOV45deg. Rotate yaw about y then pitch about x; camera depth=2.5-rotatedZ; project returns normalized x/y and positive depth, scale. Larger depth drawn first, id breaks ties. Cube edges drawn first in muted stroke; no opaque faces, so not an occlusion claim. Particle markers scaled by perspective; do not scale world coordinates by energy. Food defaults off, when on show centers with small rings (not full nutrient spheres); captions state spherical physics radius .30. Raw unit cube positions, no ghosts; torus wrapping visibly jumps opposite faces. Slice includes abs(torusDelta(z-center))<=.10. Never mutate arrays while sorting to render. Test rendering/camera changes leave a world snapshot unchanged between identical stepped trajectories, in addition to browser paused state.

The three cards, controls, phase headings, population totals and family legends are readable and stay inside their containers. Family names are explicit alongside the colors. Phase row strokes differ visibly from the stock fill, so the two encodings can be distinguished. The per-patch stock/multiplier text is readable on inspection but dense and much smaller than the primary metrics; it is suitable as detail, not as the main explanation. The explanatory panel correctly separates offered from actual renewal, names nearest-patch steering, states the 3,000-tick history limit, and disclaims adaptation and recovery fitness.

The particle groups remain compact and concentrated near patch centers in all three images. Positions and family counts change somewhat, and the long-season history changes which family is most populous, but these images do not show broad spatial reorganization. The long-season row strokes visibly rotate between all three phases. The short-season images all land in phase 1, so these particular stills do not independently demonstrate its intervening phase changes. Stock fill changes are visible, including full unused patches beside heavily depleted occupied patches.

At tick 3,609 the long-season family legend reads Mint 28, Amber 14, Lilac 71; at 7,201 it reads 44, 61, 12; at 10,803 it reads 57, 25, 78. These are visible demographic changes only. All cards show 100% inside food patches and no extinction at these instants. A screenshot cannot establish between-frame losses, rebounds, oscillation regularity, continuous movement, or sustained novelty. The numerical sweep is separate evidence and should not be inferred from these images.

The headline in these captured images still says “What happens when the harvest moves?” above “Food stays in place.” That copy should be clarified in the final UI. These captures precede the pending phase-label refresh fix; their displayed phase labels and row strokes agree at the sampled ticks, but they cannot establish frame-by-frame agreement around a boundary. Tick 10,803 is deliberately captioned as captured, not rounded to the 10,800-tick numerical endpoint.

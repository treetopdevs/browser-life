# Particle visual check

Local Chrome, 1200×1000 viewport, seed 1101, each preset reset independently and run to tick 650 or later at 4× pace. The final capture asserts that the selected preset remained unchanged. An earlier capture coincided with development hot reload; those recordings were replaced before inspection.

- **Flocks:** tight groups cluster around the nine supplied food patches. Motion continues, but this is the calmer and more repetitive display.
- **Pursuit:** dispersed particles and small groups occupy much more of the field, with clear velocity vectors and differing directions. This is the strongest next exploration lead.
- **Colonies:** retained as the strongly cohesive comparison; the pilot shows intermediate spatial occupancy and speed.

These are watchability observations, not adaptation scores. The fixed food layout visibly organizes the world. A next environmental slice should vary that opportunity rather than conceal it.

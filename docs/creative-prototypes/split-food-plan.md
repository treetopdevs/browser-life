## Stops and interpretation

Connectivity means each organism’s occupied cells remain connected by four-neighbor toroidal adjacency, as in the prior assay; it does not require habitat bands to connect. Hard stop at3000 or actual extinction. No horizon escalation, speed/width/food tuning, new seed, or rescue in this slice. Invalid source, state, accounting, bounds, connectivity, repeat, history truncation or baseline parity halts the slice and makes it inconclusive; preserve failed attempts. Only a reviewed assay bug repair permits retry of the same invalid test. A valid ecological failure is never retried. Keep24MiB per-arm and10,000 lineage-node limits; capacity exceedance is inconclusive, never raise limits here.

New variant numerical gate: alive at3000, at least two nonzero founder families, births AND deaths positive in EACH window1501–2000,2001–2500,2501–3000, at most99 ticks at the48-body cap in EACH500-tick window. Biologically unreached or partial windows after valid extinction fail; assay-caused missing data is inconclusive. Extinction at3000 is failure, not survival.

Spatial habitat gate: at2500 AND3000, at least10 occupied body tiles in EACH of the two fixed replenishment bands. Use tile%96: 0–23 is band one, 48–71 band two, and gaps count for neither. Count tiles, not organisms; bodies crossing regions are not double-counted as separate organisms. This asks whether both feeding regions are inhabited, not whether they are independent colonies or refuges. Missing endpoints fail.

Parent visual gate: body-only saved frames at2500 and3000 must show a concrete shifted, expanded or cleared cluster or gap between those two frames within the split world. Colors, IDs, food or outlines alone do not pass; uncertainty fails. Parent also describes same-age comparisons with the historical contiguous baseline at1500/2500/3000, but these comparisons are descriptive, not a superiority gate. No required appearance is invented after outcomes. Visual review is explicitly not blind to the known baseline or new numerical outcome. Commit the SHA-bound receipt before any overlap diagnostics; diagnostics are optional and unnecessary for the decision.

Promising means ALL numerical, both-band occupancy and manual own-arm change gates pass. Otherwise stop and label the actual failed gate(s), distinguishing biology, unclear visual change and assay invalidity. Passing earns only a proposal for unchanged replication at1203 and1204 under a separate reviewed plan, not automatic trials now. Even if promising, do not claim adaptation, improved fitness, durable coexistence or independent colonies. Equal offered food/area does not mean equal usable energy.

## Discrete world and explicit ingredients

New standalone apps/lab/src/development/model.ts. World96×96 torus, four cardinal neighbors. Integer food0..40 per tile, initially20 everywhere. Every fifth tick supply+1 per tile capped40; count actual added food. Cells belong to one organism ID, each has absolute tileindex, heading0..3 and depth. An organism has ID,parentID|null,founderFamily0..2,generation,age,store integer,genome,ordered growth queue and cursor, births count. Occupancy disjoint. Cells retain fixed positions; they are not moving particles. Body ID is engineered, not inferred from connected components.

World starts12 one-cell founders at a seeded permutation of12 fixed evenly spaced sites (4 columns×3 rows, x=12+24col,y=16+32row). Family sequence0,1,2 repeated; all models share locations/headings/ages/food. Founders age0/store24. Initial eachbodycell contains4 structural energy units. Cap48 living organisms,32 cells/body, total1536 occupied tiles. Food is an explicit external source; no conservation-of-matter claim. No spontaneous neworganisms or hiddenrescue.

Genome exactly {turnEvery,turn,branchEvery,maxCells}: integer turnEvery2..8,turn in{-1,0,1},branchEvery2..6,maxCells16..32. Founders cycle three supplied programs: branching {turnEvery:4,turn:0,branchEvery:4,maxCells:24}; curling {turnEvery:4,turn:1,branchEvery:3,maxCells:24}; bushy {turnEvery:4,turn:0,branchEvery:2,maxCells:24}. Constructor accepts seeduint32 and mutationboolean; seeded Mulberry32 for ecology, separate stream(seed xor fixedconstant) for mutation so disablingmutation consumes no ecologydraws. Same-runtime determinism only. Every newborn gets a new frozen genome copy; parent genome neverchanges.

## Tick ordering and development

Per single tick: (1) supply iftick%5==0; (2) take starting organism IDs ascending; (3) for each starting organism, age+1; absorb at most2 food per occupiedtile into store, visiting cells in insertionorder, store cap160; pay upkeep ceil(cellCount/8) from store (only actualavailablestore counted as dissipated); if insufficient upkeep OR age>=600, remove wholebody, count remaining store+4*cells as deathLoss, free occupancy and skipgrowth/birth; (4) grow ifalive and age%6==0 and store>=4 and cellcount<genome.maxCells; (5) reproduce ifalive and age%80==0 and cellcount>=ceil(.75*maxCells) and store>=24 and livepopulationincludingpendingchildren<48; (6) append newborns afterallstartingparents, so newborns don'tfeed/grow in theirbirthtick. Counts/idorder are explicit scheduling bias.

Growth uses a FIFO queue of parent-cell expansion attempts. Each cell contributes a predetermined ordered list of directions whencreated: forward=heading+turn (mod4) if depth>0 and depth%turnEvery==0, elseheading; then leftandright relative toforward if depth>0 and depth%branchEvery==0. Root contributes forward,left,right regardless ofbranchinterval so eachprogramcanstartbranching. Each growth opportunity consumes queued attempts until either one unoccupiedneighbor is found or queueexhausted; failuresdiscardedpermanently. Onsuccess consume4 store, add onecell with heading=attemptdirection and depth=parentdepth+1, appendthatnewcell'sattempts. No celllosswithinlivingbody. Existingparentcellkeepsitsoriginalcoordinates; wrappedadjacency preservesconnectivity. Queue entries contain parentcellindex anddirection, bounded by3attempts/cell. Once maxCells reached no further growth; unusedattempts mayremain. Reproduction does not resetparentdevelopment.

Birth: try16 candidatepositions drawn uniformly from integer dx,dy in[-8,8] relative to the root tile (including0offset; occupiedfails), acceptfirstempty; candidate RNGdraws happen only whenage/size/store/capconditionsallow. Ifnoneempty, recordblockedSpaceBirth and charge0; ifcapblocked, recordblockedCapBirth andconsume no RNG/cost. Onsuccess parentstore-=16, childinitialstore12 plus1 structuralcell4; thus transferexact16 with no birthdissipation. Newrootheading drawn0..3 AFTERplacementaccepted. Newbornfamily samefounderFamily,parentID/generation recorded. Mutationenabled: probability.25 on successfulbirth; chooseoneof4genefields uniformly, ±1 with equalprobability, clamp toallowedbounds (turn -1..1). Record attempted and effective mutations separately; clamp-nochange not effective. Mutationdisabled consumes no mutationdraws. Mutationsalterprogramforchildonly, not alreadygrownparent.

Energy ledger integer: initial = initialfood+sum(store+4*cells). Expected=initial+supplied-dissipated-deathLoss. Actual=sumfood+sumliving(store+4*cells), residual EXACT0. Growthtransfersstoretostructure; reproductiontransfersparentstoretochild. Removal countersincludeallremainingstructuralenergy. Absorptionclippedbystorespace leavesunusedfoodinplace. Survivalunderthese rulesnotproofadaptation.

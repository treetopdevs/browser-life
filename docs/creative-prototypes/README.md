# Creative worlds

This track builds deliberately engineered life-like systems for exploration. It does not alter the conserved-matter research simulation or claim open-ended evolution.

- [Running decisions and queue](decisions.html)
- [Current plan](plan.md)

First world: **Particle playground**, local route `/particles/`. Three families move according to inherited attraction rules, eat from renewable food patches, age and split. Pursuit is motion, not predation. Family color follows ancestry; it does not encode every mutation.

Run `pnpm dev` and visit `/particles/`. These experimental pages are intentionally excluded from the production site image until separately approved for release.

## How we choose what comes next
Keep variants that remain watchable: motion, turnover, distinct group patterns and visible response to opportunities. Record extinctions, saturation and repetitive outcomes too. A mutation counter proves that mutations occurred; it does not prove improved survival. Human-selected recipes are explicitly different from selection among organisms inside the world.

## First observations

The [nine-run pilot](pilot.md) kept all three families alive across all nine 3,000-tick runs. Pursuit is the strongest activity lead: it spreads across more of the world and moves several times faster than the cohesive presets. Flocks tend to gather around the supplied food patches. See the [visual check](visual-check.md) for its limits and local recording locations.

Important engineered choices: food patches renew from an external source; every family receives the same weak attraction toward the nearest patch; food competition is resolved in particle ID order; a 240-particle ceiling blocks and counts birth attempts. These choices help form the observed ecology and are not hidden discoveries.

# Both worlds keep changing through 6,000 ticks

The unchanged moving and stationary food bands both passed the longer screen on seed 1201. They stayed populated, retained multiple families, kept turning over and visibly reorganized between ticks 5,500 and 6,000. This screen did not distinguish the arms; one seed cannot establish equality or superiority.

| Arm | Bodies at 3,000 → 6,000 | Family sizes at 6,000 | Programs at 3,000 → 6,000 | Maximum-ever generation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| Moving | 38 → 48 | 21 / 15 / 12 | 12 → 15 | 27 |
| Stationary | 39 → 36 | 0 / 31 / 5 | 14 → 9 | 29 |

The moving world's mint family rebounded from 2 bodies at tick 3,000 to 21 at 6,000. Its late occupied group expanded into a broad branching cluster. That is an interesting population rebound, not proof of adaptation or improved fitness. The moving band completes a lap at tick 4,800 and then revisits earlier territory by design.

Stationary food also kept generating changes. Its dense upper cluster cleared, a broad middle-left group developed and lower groups separated. The last 500 ticks contained 98 births and 98 deaths: a stable total population can conceal substantial turnover. Its surviving families ended at 31 and 5 bodies, and distinct programs declined from 14 to 9. It remains active but less diverse by that endpoint description.

No gated window failed. Moving finishes at the ceiling, but spends only 7 of the final 500 ticks there, below the frozen 99-tick threshold. Neither arm went extinct. All moving families remain present at every saved sample. Stationary's mint family was first absent at the tick-1,360 sample, already within the known prefix; that is a 20-tick-resolution observation, not a newly discovered late extinction.

Total accepted food was 577,576 moving and 557,491 stationary, from equal offered budgets. Coverage and timing differ by design. Those totals and program counts are descriptive, not fitness measures. The minority-family gate is permissive: five stationary minority bodies are not a guarantee of durable coexistence.

Exactly one paired attempt ran with independent repeats, no retries, new seeds or rule changes. Both first-3,000-tick prefixes matched the accepted records exactly. Only ticks 3,001–6,000 are new evidence. Parent visual judgments were recorded before overlap diagnostics and were not blind to prior prefix media.

The frozen stop rule is reached. Keep both prototypes. The smallest next proposal is unchanged paired replication at seeds 1202 and 1203 to the same 6,000 horizon under a separate reviewed plan, with both modes' own historical 3,000-tick records pinned. Do not lengthen the horizon or tune the rules as part of this result. Those replication runs have not been performed here.

# A fixed food band also stays lively in the first seed

Both moving and stationary bands pass the new per-world screen at 3,000 ticks: survival, at least two founder families, continued births and deaths, limited time at the population ceiling, and visible changes in occupied body arrangements from 2,500 to 3,000. Stop after this seed, as planned.

| Distinct programs | 10 | 11 |

Both were offered 460,800 food units. The stationary world is smaller and strongly dominated by one family; its other surviving family has only two bodies. It remains an interesting variant at this horizon, with fragile diversity. No improved survival, superiority or open-ended evolution is established.

The body-only images show the stationary world's upper-middle cluster clearing, new middle-left branches developing, and the bottom-left group widening. The moving world clears its upper-right grouping while a broad middle-right cluster forms. Each passes its own within-world visual gate. Same-age differences are descriptive: this experiment does not require one arm to look different from the other to pass.

This screen did not distinguish the arms on this seed. That is not a claim of equal behavior or no benefit from movement. The fixed band never directly replenishes the other half of the torus; movement changes which territory receives food over time. Deposited food remains in place, and matching offered food does not match usable energy. Parent visual judgments are unblinded and qualitative.

The moving arm exactly reproduces the earlier accepted seed-1202 trajectory across all 151 sampled states and frames, all 3,000 tick rows and all five full snapshots. This is a regression check, not another independent success. Both newly executed arms also match independently initialized repeats.

Keep both variants for exploration. The smallest next proposal is unchanged paired replication at seeds 1201 and 1203 under a separate reviewed plan, still stopping at 3,000. That would test whether stationary food's fragile diversity persists across starting randomness. No replication, tuning, rescue or longer horizon was run in this slice.

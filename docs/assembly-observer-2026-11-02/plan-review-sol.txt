1. **P1 — §3.5: the required `random-dup` motif cannot exist.** [PLAN.md:57](/Users/nicholas/develop/browser-life-i07-assembly/docs/assembly-observer-2026-11-02/PLAN.md:57) rejects 10-cell motifs unless `a=n−1=9`. Under the primary 4-connected grammar, every connected 10-cell object contains two disjoint, translation-equivalent domino fragments. Building that domino once and reusing it gives a construction with at most **8 joins**. Rejection sampling therefore cannot terminate honestly; accepting an exhausted solver’s bound of 9 would mistake search failure for incompressibility. **Fix:** use a demonstrably attainable, frozen criterion, require exact results for acceptance, and cap generation attempts deterministically.

2. **P1 — §3.3: the plus-pentomino value is wrong.** [PLAN.md:41](/Users/nicholas/develop/browser-life-i07-assembly/docs/assembly-observer-2026-11-02/PLAN.md:41) specifies 3 joins. Exhaustive in-memory enumeration under the stated target-fragment grammar gives **4**, under both T and D4 with 4-connectivity. A three-join construction would require either a connected 3+2 partition or a two-join tetromino plus monomer; the plus permits neither. I checked every other listed toy value and obtained the stated numbers. **Fix:** change this expectation to 4 and include its lower-bound proof.

3. **P1 — §§3.5–3.6: the main comparison confounds nesting with object size and count.** [PLAN.md:56](/Users/nicholas/develop/browser-life-i07-assembly/docs/assembly-observer-2026-11-02/PLAN.md:56) compares 40×6-cell objects, 24×10-cell objects, 20×12-cell objects, and 10×24-cell hierarchy objects. Matching total occupancy does not match these distributions. Mean construction cost can identify larger objects; `maxCopies` mechanically gives hierarchy 10 versus `random-dup` 6; shared cost also changes with the number of distinct targets. Object count is displayed but omitted from the “beyond baselines” gate at line 92. **Fix:** add a primary hierarchy/duplicate pair with identical object sizes, object counts, class multiplicities and placement distribution; include those trivial descriptors in baseline comparisons.

4. **P1 — §3.8: the ordering direction contradicts the intended reuse readouts.** [PLAN.md:89](/Users/nicholas/develop/browser-life-i07-assembly/docs/assembly-observer-2026-11-02/PLAN.md:89) requires hierarchy scores to be greater for every readout. But `a/(n−1)` decreases with construction reuse, and `sharedBound` is a cost: cheaper nested construction should lower it. Consequently, meaningful reuse can force failure while expensive construction can pass. **Fix:** freeze a direction separately for every quantity, distinguish cost from reuse benefit, and apply the same declared-direction discipline to compression baselines.

5. **P1 — §3.7: the sensitivity verdict is largely vacuous or undefined.** [PLAN.md:49](/Users/nicholas/develop/browser-life-i07-assembly/docs/assembly-observer-2026-11-02/PLAN.md:49) deliberately makes synthetic segmentation agree; thresholds vary only on clips, which line 65 excludes from separation decisions. Nevertheless, line 83 defines sensitivity through changes in the *separation verdict*. Temporal variants likewise operate on movies while the hierarchy comparison is static. Dilation/erosion only measures membership changes, without defining their connection to the verdict. **Fix:** define explicit per-clip sensitivity outcomes and aggregation rules, and include a frozen segmentation-sensitive calibration control. Do not label an unchanged, inapplicable verdict “stable.”

6. **P1 — §§5,7: the freeze check does not establish the claimed freeze.** [PLAN.md:148](/Users/nicholas/develop/browser-life-i07-assembly/docs/assembly-observer-2026-11-02/PLAN.md:148) checks only ancestry and absence of `evidence.json`. Moreover, hierarchy coordinates remain “e.g.” and generation details are implemented after freezing. The written ordering does precede calibration outcomes, but its enforcement does not. **Fix:** require both documents at the freeze SHA, verify their frozen contents against the effective protocol, and freeze complete fixture recipes, seeds, rejection rules and search policies. Identify subsequent amendments and their outcome exposure explicitly.

7. **P2 — §3.4: an unfinished beam search does not supply an upper-bound certificate.** [PLAN.md:45](/Users/nicholas/develop/browser-life-i07-assembly/docs/assembly-observer-2026-11-02/PLAN.md:45) does not specify that returned costs must come from completed target constructions. 8. **P2 — §§3.8,5: the “copy-weighted” naive metric contains no copy weight.** [PLAN.md:87](/Users/nicholas/develop/browser-life-i07-assembly/docs/assembly-observer-2026-11-02/PLAN.md:87) defines `sum a_i` over repeated classes. Two copies and two hundred copies receive the same score; four distinct repeated motifs contribute four terms versus one hierarchy term. This contradicts the bound-times-copies description at line 9. The outcome assertion at line 134 can therefore pass because of class count rather than duplication. **Fix:** state the intended multiplicity formula explicitly, test its algebra on constructed inputs, and report the frozen calibration outcome without modifying fixtures to satisfy the assertion.

9. **P2 — §3.6: required fixtures produce undefined readouts.** [PLAN.md:72](/Users/nicholas/develop/browser-life-i07-assembly/docs/assembly-observer-2026-11-02/PLAN.md:72) uses `a/(n−1)` without handling monomers: `random-scatter` and padding produce `0/0`. 10. **P2 — §3.1: toroidal connectivity is specified, but toroidal shape identity is not.** [PLAN.md:28](/Users/nicholas/develop/browser-life-i07-assembly/docs/assembly-observer-2026-11-02/PLAN.md:28) normalizes offsets from the minimum corner. The proposed seam test checks joining, not canonical identity. **Fix:** define consistent lattice lifting and winding exclusion; test canonical forms and scores before and after translations across both seams.

11. **P2 — §5: the persistence expectations do not characterize the reused fixtures correctly.** [PLAN.md:138](/Users/nicholas/develop/browser-life-i07-assembly/docs/assembly-observer-2026-11-02/PLAN.md:138) says duplicated gliders persist at lag 1 and random bits have near-zero persistence. Under 4-connectivity the recurring class is the monomer, not the whole glider. Test completed persistence pairs, not merely prefix tallies, for future independence.

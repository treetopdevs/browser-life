# Reset entity contract, version 1

Date: 2026-09-29. Status: G0 operational definitions; no new biological outcome inspected. Scope: diagnostic re-audit of the fixed 200 Test 1 links, not a new emergence ensemble. The updated `foundational-reset-plan.md` controls execution.

## What is being tested

An entity is a candidate physically supported organization, identified observationally, whose continuity and subsequent function must be tested rather than assumed from its tracker identity. Existing tracker components provide the candidate support; they do not define successful reproduction. No observer label affects physical evolution.

A candidate birth is the already sampled tracker link (or a newly observed separation in a separately fixed missed-event window). A supported reproduction event additionally needs independently resolved copy/material continuity, physical separation, and subsequent descendant viability. Organizational reconstruction and repeated reproduction are stronger separate outcomes. No prior requirement for a membrane, clonal purity, symmetric fission, or survival of the parent is imposed.

The historical 200 links are frozen by their input-file hash. Their original parent/child identities, kind, census step and dominant lineages remain unchanged even if the audit contradicts the attribution. Every row receives an outcome; no replacement or favorable filtering. There are ten source histories, not 200 independent evolutionary replicates.

## Separate observables

1. **Copy descent:** track which initial physical support supplied the copied genome at each transport event; mutations inherit that copy path while recording a genotype change separately. Equal lineage or equal genome alone supplies no parent link. Record effective mutations separately from proposals that leave genotype words unchanged.
2. **Material continuity:** record conservative bounds for bound B/P carried by the candidate parent at the start of tracing and transported thereafter. New bound material and nonparent bound material are separate where identifiable. Material's owner before trace start is unknown. Mixed reactions must not invent molecular ownership: an allocation convention must be named, or interval bounds used. Gross synthesis is not automatically retained descendant structure.
3. **Separation:** physically distinct supports at the observation's stated thresholds, with continuity from the preceding support and persistence over a frozen horizon. Transient threshold crossing, toroidal movement, contact and fusion may make this unavailable. A tracker split is insufficient by itself.
4. **Function:** survival, maintained metabolism, informative nonmass organization rebuilt in new structure, and subsequent supported reproduction. Each is measured independently at a frozen time or reported unavailable. Carried morphology and garden-wide mass growth do not establish reconstruction.

No forced propagule/invasion dichotomy is used. A link may show copy descent plus extensive foreign-material recruitment; subsequent takeover can occur after a true separation. Environmental dissolved-resource uptake is not invasion. A mechanistic invasion claim needs evidence of genome replacement over pre-existing bound material and relevant transmission consequences.

## Time and identity

Checkpoint time is not biological birth time. A trace begins with known carried material and local genome-copy origins; older provenance stays unknown. A fixed-age trait is unavailable without a resolved biological age; placement age can be reported descriptively with that name.

Birth detection is fixed before looking at offspring survival. Death, a missing birth within the horizon, identity ambiguity, observed extinction, and missing technical data are distinct. Budding retains parent continuity where resolved; symmetric fission can terminate the original support. Fusion does not automatically create or continue a biological identity. A mixed-genotype support is retained rather than silently reduced to its dominant lineage.

For Probe A, the parent membership at the last pre-event census is a local reference region, not an assertion about the entire parental lifetime. Tracing farther back requires an authenticated earlier state and a prospectively budgeted decision. A short local trace may refute a particular nearest-neighbor attribution while remaining unable to prove long-term reproduction.

## Physical and observer invariants

Historical replays retain the original configuration, mutation and random keys. Sidecar tracing never changes genome IDs, physical channels, event order or random draws. Existing mutation-off lineage-recoloring helpers cannot establish historical mutation-on ancestry. Compare exact states against an uninstrumented twin and available checkpoint hashes, and compare actual census/observer outputs at available boundaries. Summary equality is weaker than full-state equality and must be labelled accordingly.

The historical runner's extra `lineageObs` output is not checkpoint-resumable; its seen-lineage and prior-individual bookkeeping is not persisted. The common observer/tracker state is persisted. A reset adapter must restore that state and reconstruct the required pre-event membership, rather than bypassing the historical runner's refusal and claiming byte-identical auxiliary output.

## Inference and decision limits

Use all 200 sampled links for the attribution audit. Report source-history rates and stratified counts for budding/fission and clonal/mutant links. Any resampling uncertainty operates on histories; retain row-level ambiguity and worst-case classification bounds. These selected historical links cannot establish all-event prevalence, detector recall or a mutation-population success rate.

Missed-event windows are selected by a deterministic rule independent of detected births and frozen before viewing their outcomes. Their limited coverage is explicit. Constructed scripts validate logic; executed physical controls validate instrumentation; neither is independent evidence of natural organism reproduction.

Positive link continuity alone does not establish a life cycle or inherited fitness. G2(a) requires those separate probes. An unresolved detector yields a measurement report; a bounded failure is not substrate impossibility. The historical Variation gate is preserved as recorded, but does not predetermine the next mechanism. Main-checkout decision updates are the main session's responsibility.

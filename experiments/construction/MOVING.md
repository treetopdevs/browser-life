# Physical-flow follow-up: what is established so far

The stationary witness in README.md established a useful construction reward but failed when simple spreading diluted the catalyst. The next probe uses existing Flow-Lenia movement and polymer adhesion to ask whether retention can coexist with material redistribution. No new dynamics were added.

The first 16 candidate pairs used low and high affinity-density targets, different patch sizes and existing adhesion gains. None beat the nonbuilder while also leaving at least 5% of initial B outside the seed footprint at step 3000. A compact patch did retain much more active B while staying within its initial footprint; it became a candidate for persistence under physical flow, with no propagation claim.

The designed patch starts with nine sites, each 512 B and 1024 free energy, and no P/A/C. Total matter is 4608 and total initial free energy 9216. Genome mu640/sigma96 aligns the affinity scale with enough local catalytic biomass; movement dt51/spread1 and kernel radius4 remain active. Polymer provides both existing adhesion and diffusion gating.

A gain sweep distinguished the two effects. At zero or low adhesion both patches collapsed. At high adhesion, disabling polymer gating still left a substantial construction benefit, so the full benefit could not be attributed to retention. Gain256 had a promising seed1 pilot at 10000 steps: builder B2571, matched nonbuilder extinct by3400, and gate-off builder extinct by4100. The builder paid cumulative construction2276 B and4552 free-energy units.

The 24×24 fresh-seed confirmation deliberately kept that horizon and setup. Its strict transport-necessity criterion **failed**:

Gating increases final B in all five seeds, by a mean 892.4. But adhesion alone can support some patches, so these data establish a combined construction benefit and an incremental retention contribution, not universal transport necessity. No final B lies outside the initial footprint; transient redistribution outside it observed at 100-step intervals reaches 596–911 B. These are sampled maxima, not exact per-step peaks. This is persistence under flow, not reproduction or successful colonisation.

The exact checkpoints, arrays, hashes and ledgers were verified. Two nonbuilding controls were used: the matched zero-BUILD controller and the strongest controller from the earlier stationary search, adapted to the same mu/sigma. The latter is a challenge control, not a new exhaustive optimum search in this different habitat.

## Exploratory mixing

In the 24×24 seed1 pilot, eight producers plus one nonbuilder finish at B2576 (center nonbuilder) or B2211 (corner nonbuilder), with no surviving nonbuilder B. One producer among eight nonbuilders fails in both positions, and total B reaches zero by3000. Every layout starts with equal total resources and active movement. These are one-seed exploratory observations; they suggest a producer-density threshold, not demonstrated invasion from rarity.

The independent all-producer replay through the capture harness exactly reproduced hash36f622d1a0419a20. Capture records assigned B, catalytic assimilation, construction expenditure, directed A/C flux and exact ledgers. Assignment follows the inherited genome; it does not trace individual molecules originally invested by a producer.

## Next fixed question

The small dissolved reservoir may let escaped nutrients return cheaply, as seen in the failed first stationary pilot. A separate domain-size probe tests 32 and48, retaining the same initial resources and genotype. It preserves all earlier failures. Any selected larger-domain candidate needs its own fresh-seed confirmation, mixed-neighbour capture and local mutational-accessibility tests before evolution. Nothing here is evolved or a second ecological opportunity.

## Larger-domain confirmation: strict criterion failed

The 32×32 pilot passed seeds1 and102; 48×48 also passed both. The smaller successful pilot size,32, was frozen before running fresh seeds301–305 for10000 steps, with exact directed A/C transport accounting. The confirmation was not used to retune the candidate.

All controls began with the same total resources. Both nonbuilding controls became extinct in every seed. One builder died at9300 while its cost-retaining transport ablation survived; this is a sign reversal. Four other builders retained substantial biomass, but the predeclared criterion requiring all builders to persist and all controls to be extinct failed. Do not substitute an average-positive criterion after seeing this result.

The parameter search stops here. The stationary example is a demonstrated function. Extending it to this moving substrate is fragile and has not earned an evolution run. No further48-domain confirmation, larger geometry sweep or32-domain capture expansion was launched. The recorded one-seed capture pilot remains informative about frequency dependence but cannot establish robustness in the failed32-domain candidate.

The next physical question is how to preserve a producer's local resource-return time without relying on a sensitive balance between polymer adhesion, affinity, crowding and spreading. A separately specified mechanism for stable spatial retention would need its own positive example and unchanged cost-retaining controls. It must not be presented as already implemented or as guaranteed to solve the problem.

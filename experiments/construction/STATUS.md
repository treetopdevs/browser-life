## Negative extensions and the resulting perspective

The moving extension failed fresh-seed causal controls, including a sign reversal. A subsequent fixed rule-2 polymer-drag pilot also failed: all six arms lose active B by3000, with only a small delay in loss. That pilot is closed without retuning. Its optional implementation remains isolated research; existing presets and registered experiments retain rule1.

The demonstrated reward is real, and mutations can sometimes improve it. Population renewal remains unresolved in the tested witness family: the successful stationary regime cannot seed empty sites, while the tested spreading extensions lose local metabolic density. The2026-10-04 Fable review discussion prioritizes a finite initial nutrient reservoir under existing rule1 before new transport physics; this can distinguish access to substrate from transport limitations. Connected expansion may support selection without detached individuals. See the [review and remediation](FABLE-REVIEW.md) and [unexecuted next-experiment proposal](NEXT-EXPERIMENT.md). Long unrestricted searches are not supported by these results.

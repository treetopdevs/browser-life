# Next test: mutation-dependent competitive improvement

Status: draft, not an execution release. No evolution histories or new competition assays have run. The completed capability roster and its original results are unchanged; all 252 receipts passed strict validation and both scheduled replays passed.

## Question and scope

Do independently confirmed capable starting genomes produce descendants with better competitive performance against their own founding genome when mutation is enabled, compared with matched mutation-disabled histories? This retains competitive propagation as the scientific outcome. Passing a capability screen, higher monoculture biomass, and role changes cannot replace that outcome.

The result would concern mutation-dependent production of heritable genotypes with improved performance in this particular assay. It would not establish organism reproduction, open-ended evolution, improvement in every environment, or unbiased evolutionary capacity across the full genome space.

## Assay controls and interpretation

Identical genomes may finish with highly unequal abundances. Such outcomes are informative if at least one competitor remains. Both competitors absent is undefined competitive share, not a tie. Empty-slot controls check accounting and continuation in the competition geometry; they are not a universal biologically disadvantaged genotype. No zero-weight-controller superiority requirement will be imposed.

An important structural fact is already covered by a CPU test: some identical-genome position/label combinations produce exactly the same physical state with reversed scoring labels. Those are linked observations, not independent evidence of neutrality. Report availability on unique initial states as well as the complete requested technical roster. Replay and attribution checks must not be inflated into demonstrated biological discrimination or statistical power.

The primary competitive observable is the raw endpoint signed score against the original founding genome; candidate share and total mass are also reported. The mutation-enabled minus disabled contrast receives raw scores in [-1,1]. Time-zero clone competition is calibration. If changes from baseline are displayed, both conditions must reference the identical baseline observations with identical weights, so baseline cancels algebraically in their contrast; the summary must never receive individual baseline changes as raw scores. Report total remaining associated B/P, ancestry retention and both-extinct frequency jointly. Before outcomes, require at least 90% of unique clone-control initial states to retain positive total competitor-associated B/P at 20,000 steps, and at least 75% for each selected founder. In at least 75% of unique clone-control states per founder, require positive late photo+grow flux from 10,000 to 20,000, alongside positive endpoint associated B/P; this supplies independent genotype-activity confirmation. Empty-slot attribution and replay checks must pass. These are engineering usability thresholds, not biological laws. Use a meaningful improvement margin of 0.10 signed competition-score units, where score=(descendant B/P-ancestor B/P)/(their total B/P). If the pilot is predominantly both-extinct, stop that assay before long evolution histories; any geometry/duration change requires a separately frozen proposal. Do not silently substitute a monoculture-efficiency endpoint and retain the same competitive-adaptation claim.

Missing checkpoints and unresolved mutation ancestry are technical incompleteness. Biological loss of an ancestry and both-extinct assay outcomes remain distinct unavailable observations. Provide conditional available-case effects plus full-range unavailable-score bounds, with fixed founder and seed denominators. Shared cached assays and repeated sampled genomes retain their observation references; they do not add replication. Uncertainty must respect matched evolution seeds and the small fixed selected founder set.

The primary effect is the equally weighted founder mean within each of eight matched evolution-seed blocks of normal-minus-disabled ancestral competitive performance at 1m. Inputs are raw endpoint scores. The founding genomes and four assay seeds are fixed; there is no inference to all archive candidates.

For a prespecified test of repeatability, certify a seed block only when its conservative full-range unavailable-score lower effect bound exceeds 0.10. Require at least seven of all eight fixed seed blocks. Under independent identically distributed seed blocks with probability of an effect above 0.10 at most one half, the exact one-sided binomial probability is 9/256 = 0.03515625. Ties and uncertified blocks count as nonsuccesses; no failed block is replaced. The sole confirmatory time is 1m. This tests majority/typical seed-block improvement conditional on these founders and assay seeds. It does not establish positive mean improvement or improvement for every founder. Always report mean effects, full completion bounds, each seed-block result and each founder separately; seven small gains can coexist with one large loss and a negative mean.

Report seed-block bootstrap intervals as approximate descriptive uncertainty, not as a reliable five-point retention noninferiority test. An observed normal-minus-disabled retention difference of at least -0.05 is a descriptive loss safeguard only. Even no observed losses in eight blocks does not establish statistical noninferiority. Technical incompleteness prevents a definitive interpretation, while biological unavailability remains represented by full score bounds. A repeatability result accompanied by negative mean performance or meaningful observed retention loss is a tradeoff, not an unqualified success. Failure to reach the sign criterion is not proof of no adaptation or practical equivalence. These distinctions were independently reviewed before any evolution outcome.

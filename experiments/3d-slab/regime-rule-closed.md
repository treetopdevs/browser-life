## R0 How to read this file

- **Normative.** `packages/metrics/src/regime3.ts` implements R6 to R12 as pure functions of job JSON; `experiments/3d-slab/regime-readout.ts` applies them; code comments and tests cite clause numbers. A disagreement between code and this file is a bug in the code (or an amendment before any run), never a reason to read the data differently.
- **Mechanical.** Every quantity is computed from the job JSONs. Nothing is chosen by eye between the data and the outcome string (R13). The by-eye look inside a band is a later step and is not part of any outcome.
- **Arithmetic.** IEEE doubles; `ln` is the natural log; fits are ordinary least squares; `median` of an even count is the mean of the two middle values; comparisons use unrounded values; every band below is **inclusive** at both ends unless a clause says "below" or "above". A window mean (R6.3, R6.6) is the mean of the stated values taken in step order: if they are all equal it is that value exactly, otherwise a Neumaier-compensated sum divided by the count; `satFrac` is the mean of the per-step ratios, and its comparison with SAT is a comparison of doubles (a window whose exact mean coverage is 0.05 can come out one ulp below 0.05 and then is not saturated). Wherever R9 writes `n_dec(tau)` (the controls of G7, the N twin at step 100, go/no-go b) it means the window mean over the steps tau - 5 to tau + 5, clipped as R6.3 says, and its quotient by `max(liveblk_R, 1)` is the window mean of the per-step quotients (as `satFrac`). The additive margins of R9.0 are the closed intervals between the explicit cuts centre - margin and centre + margin.
- **Words.** R is the reference sim (the burn-in sim, which keeps running). X is a twin: a device-side fork of R at step T (the N and F control twins differ from R in a stated configuration key, R4.10). A **kick** is the perturbation at one **site** (a voxel). Step `t` means `t` steps after T. `f` is the living fraction `living / cells` at T. "Same-noise damage": every PRNG draw is keyed on (seed, step, cell), so a twin and R share all noise; all results are readings under that coupling and say nothing about other couplings.
- **Basis codes** in R1: **M** fixed by M4 or by the code; **L** the centre is a literature anchor and the width is a judgement; **J** a judgement call with no source (it changes only before the freeze, and after Stage 0 has been seen only within R9.0's limit of three Stage 0 runs and only after a STAGE0-FAIL; never after a STAGE0-PASS or after the freeze).
- **Names.** ORDERED, CRITICAL and CHAOTIC name **finite-amplitude, same-noise, block-scale response classes**: the response of this world to a kick of the stated size (R4), counted in blocks of 8 x 8 x 8 voxels, over at most 1,000 steps. They are not the infinitesimal-slope phases of the literature: no class is read at an amplitude that tends to zero (the smallest kick is 12 units, a large share of the biomass of the voxels it lands on), and the rule has no independently labelled CRITICAL control, only a frozen control (an ORDERED end, R4.10) and a noise-swapped control (a CHAOTIC end, R4.10); a CRITICAL reading therefore means "between the two controls by the window of R6", never "validated as critical". Every outcome string carries the reading scale (R13).
- **Not computable.** A quantity whose denominator is zero, or whose input is missing, is NA; NA propagates (a class that needs an NA quantity is NA; a job with no class casts no vote). Nothing is replaced by zero except where a clause says "floored at". Every job ends in exactly one terminal status (R3) and the readout in exactly one outcome (R10), INVALID-RUN included.

## R3 Start-state statuses

**INVALID** (R5) is determined as the checks run and overrides every status below: an INVALID job is excluded from every count and the readout's outcome is INVALID-RUN (R10). The other statuses are determined at T, in this order, and 6 and 7 again at the end of the twin phase (step T + 1,000).

1. **EXTINCT** if `living` is 0 at any 1,000-step check up to T. No twins, no class.
2. **NO_TARGET** if `N_E < NE_MIN` (R4.2), including the case that no voxel has `B >= 1` (B* is then NA, N_E = 0). No twins, no class.
3. Otherwise **METERED**, with flags that do not stop the meters: **CARPET** if `f >= 0.85`; **RELIC** if `f < 0.01`. A METERED job with neither flag is **IN_WINDOW**.
4. **FEW_SITES** if fewer than M_S_MIN sites could be placed (R4.4): the S twin is not run, the trajectory meter is unavailable, the Derrida meter still runs; the U twin shares S's kicked set and is not run either, so the dust state is DUST_UNAVAILABLE and the job casts no vote. **FEW_COLONIES** if at least M_S_MIN sites were placed but they lie in fewer than M_COL_MIN colonies (R4.4): the S twin runs and its series are printed, but no stability can be established, so the T-class is NA. Both make the job `D_ONLY` (R7).
5. **FEW_DOSES** if the origin set O has fewer than 2 doses (R4.5): the Derrida meter is unavailable and the job has no class (it is excluded from R8). It is reachable under both rungs, in worlds with fewer than about 80 living blocks (the dose cap, R1 DOSE_CAP).
6. **DIED_IN_PHASE** if `live_R` (the diff log) is 0 at any step of the twin phase: no class, no vote (a world that dies reads "ordered" because both copies die). Counted with EXTINCT in R8.1.
7. **DRIFT** if the job was IN_WINDOW at T and `f` read at T + 1,000 is outside the livable window (a world that fills or empties during the phase): the job keeps its T flag but is not IN_WINDOW; no vote. A job already CARPET or RELIC at T retains that flag and its metered class and is not DRIFT (else every carpet would be DRIFT and G3 could never hold); a DIED_IN_PHASE job is not additionally DRIFT.

Every denominator below that can be zero (`liveblk_R(t)`, `live_R`, `SB_R`, `m_S`, `n0`, the lineage union) is floored at 1 where a clause says so and is NA otherwise (R0); a status is never left undetermined.

## R4 Scale, sites, the kick

- **R4.1 Scale.** `B*` is the lower median of B over the voxels with `B >= 1` at T (ascending order, element `floor((N - 1) / 2)`). `k = max(12, floor(B* / 2))`. Block threshold `thetaB = ceil(k / 2)` (never re-derived from data: a fired trigger fails the rung's combinations, R9.0); voxel threshold `thetaV = max(2, ceil(k / 4))`.
  - [Why a block count: voxel-level damage forgets the size of the kick, so the rule counts blocks; a block sum may not cancel dust once the cascade has spread, so the dust control (R6.12) and Stage 0's measured dust floor (R9.0) test it.]
  - [A persistent block deficit is not evidence of amplification: the integer transport keeps a kick on a low-B voxel where it is (code rows in R14). The rule therefore reads retention (FROZEN, R6.4), rounding propagation (DUST_LIMITED, R6.12) and amplification (the CHAOTIC branches, and CRITICAL with the extent test) separately; `thetaB = ceil(k / 2)` is a judgement constant.]
  - [`k >= 12` keeps `thetaB >= 6`, above the few units by which dust crossing a block boundary can shift a block sum before the cascade has spread; `thetaV >= 2` because exact differences of 1 are rounding dust. Stage 0 reports the measured dust floor (R9.0).]
- **R4.2 Eligible voxels.** `E` = voxels with `B >= k + 1` (they stay living after the kick). `N_E = |E|`. `E` is listed in ascending index.
- **R4.3 Selection stream.** `h0 = lowbias32((imul(seed, 7919) ^ T) >>> 0)`.
- **R4.4 S sites and colonies.** For `j = 0, 1, ..., 4095`: `c = draw(h0, j) % N_E`; the voxel `E[c]` is accepted if its horizontal Chebyshev torus distance (x, y; z ignored) to every accepted site is at least D_SEP (40 voxels, 5 blocks, so every site has its own block); stop at M_S accepted. `m_S` is the number accepted. A **colony** is a connected component (4-neighbourhood, x and y wrap) of the columns that contain a voxel with `B + P > 0` in R at T; a site belongs to the colony of its column; `n_col` is the number of colonies that contain at least one site. Sites are not independent samples, so stability is judged over colonies (R7). FEW_SITES if `m_S < M_S_MIN`; FEW_COLONIES if `m_S >= M_S_MIN` and `n_col < M_COL_MIN` (R3). A compact livable patch cannot host six sites 40 voxels apart (a 79 x 79 patch, 9.5 percent of the 256 x 256 world, hosts at most four): that is a lack of spatial support and is reported as such (R10 UNDER-SUPPORTED), never as evidence that no band exists.
- **R4.5 Dose sites.** Sort `E` by key `lowbias32((v ^ h0) >>> 0)` ascending (ties by index). The kicked set of the twin of dose `m` under P1 is the first `m` voxels of that list, so doses are nested. `n0(m)` is the number of blocks that contain a voxel of the kicked set, computed on the CPU before any twin is built. A dose is **run** if `m <= N_E / 2` and `n0(m) <= DOSE_CAP * liveblk_R(T)`; the doses run form the origin set `O` (at least 2 are needed: R3 FEW_DOSES). Dose sites are not separated (D_SEP applies to S sites only), so a block may hold several kicks; `n0(m) < m` records that. Under P2 the dose is `m` balls centred at those voxels with the overlap rule of R4.9.
- **R4.6 The kick.** For every kicked voxel `v` (a site under P1; the union of R4.9 under P2): `B_v <- B_v - k_v`, `C_v <- C_v + k_v`, where `k_v = k` under P1; and `heatOut <- heatOut + (sum of k_v) * (eB - eC)`. Nothing else changes (no genome, lineage, A, P, E, S or MOT word). The kick conserves matter exactly and keeps the energy ledger exact (the decay law `B -> C` exports `eB - eC` per unit as heat).
- **R4.7 Twin.** R's ledger is drained first (so the copied event counter is 0). A twin copies R's `cells[0]`, `currentGenome` into both genome buffers, and the ledger; its `step` is set to R's; the kick is queued before the next submission. Config, seed and step are equal by construction for the Z, S, U and dose twins (forks). The N and F twins are not forks in this sense (R4.10).
- **R4.8 Z and U twins.** Z is a fork with no kick, run 100 steps. U (the unit-kick control) is a fork with the same kicked set as S at a reduced kick, run 300 steps: under P1 `kU = max(1, floor(k / 8))` at every S site (so kU <= k / 8, "an eighth or less", and kU = 1, which is 1/12 to 1/15 of k, for k from 12 to 15); under P2 `kU_v = max(1, floor(k_v / 8))` at **every** voxel that S kicks, so U is never an empty perturbation. The heat entry uses the kicks applied. The **U dose** is the sum of the U kicks and the **S dose** the sum of the S kicks; both are job columns. If the U dose exceeds `U_MAX` times the S dose (a ball of low-B voxels can make `kU_v` a large share of `k_v`), U is **unavailable**: the job's dust state is DUST_UNAVAILABLE (R6.12) and, in the gate, the cause is `DUST-CONTROL-UNAVAILABLE` (R9.2).
- **R4.9 Rung P2 (a ball wound; used only if the finalizer fixes RUNG = P2 from Stage 0).** The S sites are 16 ball centres chosen as in R4.4 (E, D_SEP 40). A **ball** is the set of voxels within Euclidean distance 3 of its centre (x, y, z; x and y wrap, z is clipped at the walls, so at most 123 voxels). The **kicked set** of a twin is the **union** of its balls' voxels with `B >= 3` in the frozen reference R at T; each kicked voxel `v` is kicked **once**, by `k_v = floor(B_v / 2)` where `B_v` is its value in R at T (never recomputed from a partly kicked state), so no voxel is kicked twice and the unsigned B channel cannot underflow. `heatOut` rises by `sum(k_v) * (eB - eC)`. Dose twins use `m` balls centred at the first `m` voxels of the keyed list (R4.5), nested and unseparated, with the same union rule; `n0(m)` is computed from the union. **Thresholds**: `thetaB = ceil(M / 2)` where `M` is the median of `|dB_blk(0)|` over the blocks that contain a kick of the **S twin**, and `thetaV = max(2, ceil(K / 2))` where `K` is the median of `k_v` over the voxels the S twin kicks (a ball puts hundreds of units in a block and tens of damaged voxels, and thresholds of a few units would count dust). **Every twin of the job (S, U, N, F, doses) uses these S-derived thresholds**; no twin has its own. `n0` is as in R6.3. Everything else is as for P1.
- **R4.10 N and F twins (controls; not forks of R's configuration).** The **N twin** (noise-swapped ceiling) holds R's state at T under R's configuration except `seed`, which is `seedN = lowbias32((seed ^ 0x4e4e4e4e) >>> 0)`, so every stochastic rounding and lottery draws differently; it carries no kick, runs 300 steps and is compared with R. Its `n_dec(t)` is the ceiling that complete decorrelation of the noise gives at the same scale. The **F pair** (frozen control; gate and Stage 0 jobs only) is two copies of R's state at T under R's configuration except `dtQ = 0` and `motility = false`: R_F is the reference of the pair and X_F carries the S kick (same kicked set and thresholds as S); both run 1,000 steps and X_F is compared with R_F, not with R. With zero displacement a voxel with B <= 16 sends nothing (R4.1), so a kick on such a voxel stays where it is: F is the instrument's own picture of a persistent, non-spreading scar. Both are built like the forks (R4.7) except that the sim is created with the overridden key, as `twins_any.ts` built its N twin (lines 71 and 72: `create({...state, cfg})`, no `upload`), so `upload`'s `configDiff` never sees them. C1 does not apply to them (it is the Z fork's check); C2 uses R's initial totals; C8 checks that their configurations differ from R's in exactly the stated keys.

## R5 Validity checks

A job is **INVALID** (excluded from every count, reported by name, and the readout exits non-zero) if any of these fails. A check that the job file does not record at all counts as failed: a METERED job's file carries C1 to C8 (C3 and C4 may be recorded as not applicable), an EXTINCT or NO_TARGET job's file C2, C4 and C6. **Stage 0 (R9.0) is the one exception to INVALID-RUN:** an INVALID Stage 0 job is the cause `INVALID-JOB` of every combination (G5; a failed or unrecorded C1 is also go/no-go (a), `Z-NOT-ZERO`), the Stage 0 readout lists its failed checks, the run counts as one of the three, and that readout exits 0 whatever its outcome.

- **C1 Zero control.** The Z twin's global counters (`n_blk`, `n_blk2`, `n_occ`, `L1c`, `n_thetaV`, `n_2thetaV`, `n_1`, `n_live`, `n_vox`, `L1_A`, `L1_B`, `L1_C`, `L1_P`) are 0 at every step 1 to 100 (a column that stops short of step 100 fails), **and** at step 100 the full-state hash of Z (`stateHash` of `readState`: cells, current genome, ledger and step; the configurations are equal) equals R's `stateHash` at the same step. [Counters alone would miss a genome difference with no expressed cell effect.]
- **C2 Exact ledgers.** For R and for every twin (the N and F sims included), at the end of its run: total matter equals the initial total (BigInt equality) and `ledgerResidual(start0, state)` is 0, with `start0 = totalsOf(cfgR, initWorld(cfgR, preset.init).cells)`, **R's own initial totals for every sim of the job** (so the check never rebuilds a world under the N or F configuration). (A kick without its heat entry gives a residual of exactly `-(sum of the kicks) * (eB - eC)`; the unit test uses that as the negative control.)
- **C3 Legal state.** `validateState` returns no errors for the S twin's state read back right after the kick, before step 1.
- **C4 Living-trace agreement with M4** (settings of groups A and B at seeds 101 to 103 only). At every multiple of 5,000 steps up to T, R's living count equals the M4 `livingTrace5k` entry for the same (setting, seed). Modes: **`json`**, exact equality with the per-run JSON in `runs/m4-calibration/` (copy the 180 files from `.claude/worktrees/3d-rung1b-slab/runs/m4-calibration/` before the gate and record their SHA-256 manifest in `gate.json`; the directory does not exist in this worktree); **`tsv-rounded`**, if only `experiments/3d-slab/m4-results.tsv` is available, `|living / 524288 - tsv| <= 0.00005` (the TSV has four decimals, so +-26 voxels). For a run that goes extinct, `extinctAt` equals M4's. **Not applicable, and recorded as `m4Source: none` (skipped, never counted as passed):** groups C and D and the control (M4 never ran those axes), the extension seeds 104 to 106 (M4 ran seeds 101 to 103 only), development seeds and the 2D worlds. Each job records `m4Source = {path, sha256, mode}`; the gate requires mode `json` for every G1 and G3 job; the sweep accepts both modes and the repro line counts them separately (R13). C4 is a **living-trace agreement** check: equal living counts at four times are consistent with an identical burn-in and do not prove it, and the TSV mode proves even less. Every job therefore also records `stateHashT` (full `stateHash` of R at T) and `stateDigestT` (R12.3) so that a later run on another machine, or the M3 Pro, can compare whole states.
- **C5 Initial damage.** The diff pass on each twin before step 1 gives `n_live` = 0, `L1_A` = `L1_P` = 0, `L1_B` = `L1_C` = the sum of the kicks, and `n_blk` = the number of kicked blocks (the blocks whose kicks sum to at least `thetaB`): under P1 this is `m_S`, since the S sites are at least 5 blocks apart, and `n_occ` is 0 (one voxel cannot reach OCC_V, so under the occupancy decider the initial damage count is 0 while `n0` is not); under P2 `n_blk(0) <= n0` (R6.3), as blocks whose kicks sum below `thetaB` do not count.
- **C6 Stamp.** `ruleSha` equals `runs/regime/RULE.sha256`; `codeCommit` and `srcHash` are present and `treeClean` is true. `srcHash` is the SHA-256 of the concatenated contents of `packages/sim-gpu/src/regime.ts`, `packages/metrics/src/regime3.ts`, `experiments/3d-slab/regime-sweep.ts` and `experiments/3d-slab/regime-readout.ts`; `treeClean` is true if those four files have no uncommitted change (the stages `gate`, `main` and `late` refuse to start otherwise; the runner's `--dev` rehearsals carry `rehearsal: true` and every readout refuses them; Stage 0 jobs (`--stage=dev`, ids prefixed `dev-`) record C6 as not applicable, because the rule is not yet frozen). A gate or sweep readout reads jobs with one `ruleSha`, one `codeCommit` and one `srcHash`. The Stage 0 readout does not compare stamps across its jobs (the jobs of a Stage 0 run may differ among themselves); the rung files of one gate or Stage 0 job carry one of each, and a difference within a job is a C6 failure of that job.
- **C7 Living identity.** At the end of the job (at the last step run, for a DIED_IN_PHASE job), `live_R(1000)` from the diff log equals R's `readStats().living` (the test `B + P > 0` and the lineage test agree).
- **C8 Control configurations.** N's `cfg` equals R's except `seed` (= seedN); F's pair (R_F and X_F) equals R's except `dtQ` (0) and `motility` (false); the Z, S, U and dose twins' equal R's. The sim's configuration difference against R's is recorded per twin and compared with these lists.

## R6 Meters

All counters come from one GPU diff pass per twin per step (twin X against R, one workgroup per block; the F pair's X_F against R_F); a pass before step 1 gives t = 0. A **block** is 8 x 8 columns by all layers (8 x 8 x 8 voxels in the slab, 8 x 8 in 2D), aligned to the grid. Per step, global counters:

- **Block damage: the two candidate deciders.**
  - *Net sum* (`n_blk`). For each block `dB_blk` is the signed sum over its voxels of `B_X - B_R`. `n_blk` = number of blocks with `|dB_blk| >= thetaB`; `n_blk2` (`>= 2 thetaB`); `L1c` = sum over blocks of `|dB_blk|` (the coarse L1); `liveblk_R` = number of blocks with at least one living voxel in R (every quotient by it uses `max(liveblk_R, 1)`).
  - *Occupancy* (`n_occ`). Number of blocks in which at least OCC_V voxels have `|B_X - B_R| >= thetaV`.
  - `n_dec` is `n_blk` when the selected DECIDER (R1) is `net` and `n_occ` when it is `occ`.
- **Voxel (fine) damage: columns only.** `n_thetaV` = voxels with `|B_X - B_R| >= thetaV`; `n_2thetaV` (`>= 2 thetaV`); `n_1` (`>= 1`, the dust level).
- `n_live` = voxels whose living status differs, where living means `B + P > 0` (equal to the lineage test, C7).
- `n_vox` = voxels where any of A, B, C, P, E, S, MOT differs (exact Hamming; reported as the contrast, not used).
- `L1_s` = sum over voxels of `min(|dS|, 1023)` for s in A, B, C, P (the voxel L1 matter distance).
- `SB_R` = sum of `B_R`; `live_R` = number of voxels with `B_R + P_R > 0`; and the twin's own `SB_X`, `live_X` (for the census divergence, R6.10).
- S only, per site j: `n_blk_j(t)`, `n_occ_j(t)` and the reaches `reach_blk_j(t)`, `reach_occ_j(t)` (R6.2); `n_dec_j` and `reach_j` are the chosen decider's.

**Which column decides:** `n_dec` decides every class below; R1 fixes which of `n_blk` and `n_occ` it is. The other columns are printed beside it and decide nothing. [Reasons for a block count: exact Hamming grows as a light cone in every regime of a local rule and discriminates nothing; the voxel-level response forgets the kick, so voxel counts and the voxel L1 measure the rounding-level mess a state generates, not what became of the kick; living status flickers at colony edges.] The two block counts see different things. The *net sum* sees how much biomass is where at the scale of a block, but it is **blind to rearrangement at constant block density**: two twins with the same biomass per block, organisms displaced by a voxel or two, or different lineages in the same places, read `n_blk = 0`, which is ORDERED; and it does not cancel dust once the cascade has spread (R4.1). The *occupancy count* sees rearrangement, but it also sees the dust cloud. Neither is known to be right for this system; Stage 0 picks (R9.0).
- **R6.2 Attribution and reach (S).** A block that is damaged under the decider (`n_dec`) is attributed to the S site nearest to its centre by horizontal Chebyshev torus distance in voxels (ties to the lower index; no cutoff). `n_dec_j(t)` counts attributed blocks; `reach_j(t)` is the largest such distance in voxels (0 if none). `N(t) = (1 / m_S) * sum_j n_dec_j(t)` equals the global `n_dec(t) / m_S`.
- **R6.3 Trajectory series.** `n0` is the number of blocks that contain a kicked voxel (decider-independent): `n0_S = n0 / m_S` for the S twin (1 per site under P1; one to four per ball under P2, one when the ball lies inside a single block), `n0(m)` for a dose twin. For each grid time `t_c` in TRAJ: `Nbar(t_c)` = mean of `N(t)` over steps `max(1, t_c - 5)` to `min(1000, t_c + 5)`; `r(t_c) = Nbar(t_c) / n0_S`. `satFrac(t_c)` = the same window mean of `n_dec(t) / max(liveblk_R(t), 1)`. The series is **saturated at `t_c`** if `satFrac(t_c) >= SAT` **and** `r(t_c) >= R_AMP`: the damage covers a twentieth of the living blocks *and* has at least doubled per site. If `satFrac(t_c) >= SAT` but `r(t_c) < R_AMP` it is **not** saturated and carries the flag FOOTPRINT (the kick footprint alone covers that share of the living blocks). [Coverage alone is a function of the living-block count (under P1 `satFrac = m_S * r / liveblk_R`), so a flat response would read as saturated in a small world; with R_AMP a flat response reads flat at any size of world (R14).] The series is **saturated** if it is saturated at any grid time. Fit: `y_j = ln(max(r(t_j), 1 / (2 m_S)))` on `x_j = ln t_j`, OLS slope `b`, over the grid points before the first saturated one (all eight if none); `b` is NA if fewer than 3 points. Exponent for the map: `e = +2` if saturated; else `e = -2` if `r(1000) < ORD_R`; else `clamp(b, -2, +2)`.
- **R6.4 Trajectory class (T-class).** In this order:
  1. NA if the job is FEW_SITES (no S twin ran) or FEW_COLONIES (no stability can be established, R7).
  2. CHAOTIC if saturated (this reading does not depend on the site attribution, so it stands under OVERLAP).
  3. NA if the job is OVERLAP (R6.5).
  4. ORDERED if `r(1000) < ORD_R` or `b < B_LO`.
  5. CHAOTIC if `b > B_HI`.
  6. ORDERED, form `frozen`, if `speed < SPEED_MIN` (R6.11): the damage persists (`r(1000) >= ORD_R`) but its extent does not grow, a scar and not a cascade. This is what the frozen control F reads by construction (R4.10, R9.2 G7); a world in which nothing happens must not read as the target class.
  7. CRITICAL otherwise: persistent, flat within t^+-0.5, and spreading by at least one block over steps 100 to 1,000.
  Growth-form label (printed, decides nothing): `decays` if ORDERED and not frozen; `frozen`; `saturating` if saturated; for `b > B_HI`: `exponential` if the residual sum of squares of `y` against `t_j` (exponential model) is below that against `ln t_j` (power model), else `cone` if `b <= 2.5`, else `super-cone`; `flat` otherwise.
- **R6.5 Overlap.** A site is **spread** if `reach_j(t) >= 20` at any stored step (all steps in the eight windows and every 10th step). The job is OVERLAP if more than 25 percent of the sites are spread. Then the trajectory meter is NA and the job keeps its Derrida point (R7).
- **R6.6 Derrida meter.** For each dose twin of dose `m` in O and each tau in TAU: `nbar(tau; m)` = mean of that twin's `n_dec(t)` over steps `max(1, tau - 5)` to `min(300, tau + 5)`. `n0(m)` = the number of blocks that contain a kicked voxel of that twin (R4.5). **Response ratio** `s_m(tau) = nbar(tau; m) / n0(m)`: damaged blocks per kicked block after tau steps. (Under the net decider and P1 every kicked block is damaged at step 0, so `s_m(0) = 1` and `s_m` multiplies the initial damage; under the occupancy decider `n_occ(0)` is 0 and `s_m` counts blocks that reach OCC_V differing voxels per kicked block, which is not a multiplier of initial damage; the readout prints which.) These are finite-amplitude ratios at the scale of a block, not an infinitesimal slope (R0). **Pooled ratio:** `s(tau) = sum over m in O of nbar(tau; m) / sum over m in O of n0(m)`; O is the set of doses whose kicked blocks are at most a tenth of the living blocks (R4.5), so the pooled ratio is dominated by no dose that already marks half the world. The S twin gives `s_S(tau) = r(tau)` as a cross-check column. **Curve points:** x = `n0(m) / liveblk_R(T)` (the kicked-block fraction), y = `s_m(tau)`; the columns `d0_m = m / live_R(T)` and `D_m(tau) = nbar(tau; m) / liveblk_R(T)` are kept, with `D_m / d0_m = s_m * (n0(m) / m) * (live_R / liveblk_R)`, which is **not** `s_m`; the reference line of the plot is `s = 1`, not `D = d0`. Linearity ratio `lin_m(tau) = s_m(tau) / s(tau)` (printed when `s(tau) > 0`). Denominators are floored at 1.
- **R6.7 Derrida class (D-class), decided at tau = TAU_DEC (30).** ORDERED if `s(TAU_DEC) < D_LO`; CRITICAL if `D_LO <= s(TAU_DEC) <= D_HI`; CHAOTIC if `s(TAU_DEC) > D_HI`. **Stable** (`stD`) if the classes of `s_m(TAU_DEC)` for the two largest doses in O and the class of `s(TAU_DEC)` are all equal. The tau = 100 and tau = 300 values are columns only. [The size of the kick must still matter at the deciding step; Stage 0 checks that it does.]
- **R6.8 Second columns** at the eight grid windows (S twin): the block count that did not decide (`n_blk` or `n_occ`), `n_blk2`, `L1c`, `n_thetaV`, `n_2thetaV`, `n_1`, `n_live`, `n_vox`, `L1_A`, `L1_B`, `L1_C`, `L1_P`, `L1_B / SB_R`, and the fine-to-coarse ratio `n_thetaV / n_dec`; the **ceiling ratio** `q(t) = n_dec,S(t) / max(n_dec,N(t), 1)` at the TAU columns (the damage as a fraction of the noise-swapped twin's, R4.10); per-site **median and inter-quartile range** of `n_dec_j` at the TAU columns (site responses are heavy-tailed, so a mean over sites can be carried by one site).
- **R6.9 Spatial entropy rate of B (descriptive only; no entropy value enters any class, the gate or any outcome).** On R's B channel at T and at T + 100. Symbol `q(v)` is the bin of `B(v)`: `{0}`, `{1..3}`, `{4..15}`, `{>=16}` (q = 4; edges 1, 4, 16 stated as the resolution; the entropy of a continuous field depends on it).
  - `H0`: marginal entropy of `q` at T.
  - `h_sp`: conditional entropy of `q(x, y, z)` given `q` at (x-1, y, z), (x-1, y-1, z), (x, y-1, z), (x+1, y-1, z), pooled over the layers (x, y wrap). 256 contexts against 524,288 samples; plug-in estimator; bias at most `contexts * 3 / (2 N ln 2)` = 0.0011 bits, printed.
  - `h_z` (slab only): conditional entropy of `q(z)` given `q(z-1)` at the same (x, y), pooled over z = 1 to 7.
  - `h_st`: conditional entropy of `q_{T+100}(v)` given `(q_T(v), m_T(v))`, where `m_T(v)` is the number of the six face neighbours with bin >= 2 (x, y wrap, missing z neighbours count as not >= 2): 28 contexts. This is the space-time (dynamical) variant.
  - `c_ratio`: `compressionRatio` (fflate deflate level 6, `packages/metrics/src/complexity.ts`) of the `q` bytes in Hilbert order (order-8 curve per layer, layers concatenated) divided by the same for a seeded Fisher-Yates shuffle of those bytes (stream `lowbias32` from `h0`). 1 means no structure; far below 1 means structure.
- **R6.10 Genome, lineage and census divergence (descriptive only; none of it decides anything).** At t in {100, 300, 1000}. `J_id(t) = |ids_S symmetric-difference ids_R| / |ids_S union ids_R|` over the lineage ids of living voxels (genome words 0 and 1). **`J_g(t)`** is the same quotient over the triples (id_hi, id_lo, fingerprint) of living voxels, where the fingerprint is a 32-bit chained `lowbias32` hash of all genome words of the voxel, read from `currentGenome` of S and of R (6 reads of about 100 MB, 75 to 110 ms each): equal ids with different genomes are possible, because a mutant's id is (step + 1, cell) and the transport lottery can pick different parents in the two twins (`step3d.ts:426`, `:642`), so ids alone could read 0 over a heritable difference. `n_gdiff(t)` is the number of voxels living in both whose fingerprints differ. **Census divergence:** `dLiving(t) = |live_S - live_R| / max(live_R, 1)` and `dSumB(t) = |SB_S - SB_R| / max(SB_R, 1)`. **Scale curve:** the relative L1 of the column-summed B field, `sum |B_X - B_R| / max(sum B_R, 1)` after summing B over z and coarse-graining to s x s columns, for s in {1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32}, from `readChannel(B)` of S and R (2 MB each). Together they say at which scale the twins differ, so a CHAOTIC class at the block scale is a statement about that scale and not about ecology.
- **R6.11 Speed (decides FROZEN, R6.4).** `speed` = median over sites with `n_dec_j(1000) > 0` of `(reach_j(1000) - reach_j(100)) / 900`, using the window means; NA if no site has damage at 1000 (then `r(1000) < ORD_R` and the T-class is already ORDERED). It is printed.
- **R6.12 Dust control.** `Nbar_S(tau)` is the S twin's window mean (steps `max(1, tau - 5)` to `tau + 5`) of `n_dec(t) / m_S` and `Nbar_U(tau)` the U twin's, both at `tau = TAU_DEC`. `ratio_U = Nbar_U / max(Nbar_S, 1 / (2 m_S))`. The job's **dust state** is **DUST_CLEAR** if `ratio_U < DUST_CLEAR`; **DUST_LIMITED** if `ratio_U >= DUST_LIM`: a much smaller kick (R4.8) leaves at least half the damage of the full kick, so the response is not attributable to the kick (rounding dust, or sensitive dependence; this comparison cannot tell which, and the readout says only "observed dose insensitivity"); **DUST_AMBIG** otherwise; **DUST_UNAVAILABLE** if the U twin is unavailable (R4.8) or was not run (FEW_SITES, R3). **In the sweep only DUST_CLEAR jobs vote** (R8.2): the other jobs are valid, are listed with their ratios, and are counted by the DUSTY setting class and by SENSITIVE-EVERYWHERE (R8, R10); a response that is declared "not attributable to the kick" can never support a band. In the gate a job that is not DUST_CLEAR counts against G6. [The state is evaluated at TAU_DEC, where the size of the kick still matters; a response read at a saturated time is not attributable, whatever it is. The margin between 0.25 and 0.5 is on purpose: a single line would make a pass on new seeds close to a coin flip.]

## R7 Job class

Inputs: T-class (R6.4), D-class (R6.7), `stD`, the bootstrap stability `stT` and the dust state (R6.12). Bootstrap over **colonies** (R4.4): `BOOT` resamples; each resample draws `n_col` colonies with replacement, the colony at position `p` of resample `u` having index `draw(bootSeed, u * n_col + p) % n_col` (`i = u * n_col + p` runs over all resamples, so no two resamples reuse an index) with `bootSeed = lowbias32(h0 ^ 0xb007)`, and takes all S sites of each drawn colony (a colony drawn twice counts twice); each resample recomputes `N(t)` at the stored steps as the mean of the per-site series over the sites drawn, then `r`, `b` and the T-class with the saturation flag held at its point value; `stT` is the fraction of resamples whose T-class equals the point T-class. The colonies are ordered by the lowest S-site index they contain, and the sites of a colony by S-site index. In each resample `m_S` is the number of sites drawn (repeats counted), `n0_S` is the mean of the drawn sites' original kicked-block counts, the fit floor is `1 / (2 m_S)`, and OVERLAP and the speed are recomputed on the drawn sites; only the saturation boolean is held at its point value, and FEW_SITES and FEW_COLONIES are ignored within a resample. [The sites of one colony share its fate, so sites are not resampled as independent.]

1. No class if the job is FEW_DOSES, DIED_IN_PHASE or DRIFT (R3).
2. If the T-class is available: a T-class ORDERED with form `frozen` together with a D-class CRITICAL **agree on ORDERED** (flag FROZEN): a persistent scar has a response ratio near 1, which the D window calls CRITICAL, and the two meters are reading the same fact. Then MIXED if T-class and D-class differ; UNSTABLE if they agree but `stT < STABLE` or `stD` is false; otherwise the common class.
3. If the T-class is NA (FEW_SITES, FEW_COLONIES or OVERLAP): UNSTABLE if `stD` is false; else the D-class if it is ORDERED or CHAOTIC (flag `D_ONLY`); MIXED if it is CRITICAL (flag `D_ONLY`), because one meter cannot certify a band.
4. **Vote.** A job whose class is ORDERED, CRITICAL or CHAOTIC **votes** (R8.2) only if its dust state is DUST_CLEAR; otherwise it is a valid job without a vote.

MIXED jobs are listed in the readout as the near-band candidates (the two meters disagree: a long-lived transient or a slowly spreading front). They never count as in-band.

## R8 Setting class

- **R8.1 Status.** From the three seeds' job statuses: UNMETERABLE if at least 2 are EXTINCT, NO_TARGET or DIED_IN_PHASE; else CARPET if at least 2 are flagged CARPET at T; else RELIC if at least 2 are flagged RELIC at T; else the setting is **windowed**.
- **R8.2 Windowed setting.** **Valid** jobs are METERED, IN_WINDOW (at T and at T + 1,000), not INVALID, not FEW_DOSES, DIED_IN_PHASE or DRIFT, with a job class (R7). **Voting** jobs are the valid jobs whose class is ORDERED, CRITICAL or CHAOTIC and whose dust state is DUST_CLEAR (R7.4). Fewer than 2 valid jobs: INSUFFICIENT. Otherwise, if at least VOTE jobs are voting, the class is the one of ORDERED, CRITICAL, CHAOTIC with at least VOTE votes among them, else SPLIT. If fewer than VOTE jobs are voting: **DUSTY** if at least VOTE valid jobs have a class ORDERED, CRITICAL or CHAOTIC that the dust rule kept from voting (dust state LIMITED, AMBIG or UNAVAILABLE: the response is not attributable to the kick, or cannot be shown to be; the class they would have formed by majority is printed as the `would-be` class), else SPLIT (the meters disagree or are unstable).
- **R8.3 Pool.** The windowed settings with at least 2 valid jobs (INSUFFICIENT excluded; SPLIT and DUSTY settings are in the pool). The **class-bearing** pool settings are those whose class is ORDERED, CRITICAL or CHAOTIC.
- **R8.4 Coordinate.** `ell` = median over the voting jobs of `ln(max(s(TAU_DEC), S_FLOOR))`; NA with no voting job.
- **R8.5 Extension.** When R10 gives UNRESOLVED, seeds 104 to 106 are run once for the SPLIT settings (same rung and decider, T0; C4 does not apply to these seeds, R5). The extension is licensed only by that outcome on the table of seeds 101 to 103 alone, and only for the settings that are SPLIT there; every such setting needs all of seeds 104 to 106 before R10 is read again (a started, incomplete extension is not read), and jobs at seeds 104 to 106 for any other setting, or when R10 did not give UNRESOLVED, are not read (the reader refuses them). A setting's class is then the class with at least 4 votes among its voting jobs when it has at least 5 voting ones, else SPLIT; its status (UNMETERABLE, CARPET, RELIC) stays that of its first three seeds. A setting with six seeds is late-checked with the same six seeds and the same rule (R11). Seeds 1 to 6 are never used.

## R9 Ground-truth gate: Stage 0 (development) and the held-out gate

R9 has two parts. **Stage 0** is a development pilot on seeds 9001 to 9003 that selects one combination of rung and decider and checks that the instrument can be read at all; it runs before the freeze and its result goes into R1. The **held-out gate** runs on seeds 101 to 103 after the freeze, tests that single combination once, and closes the candidate if it fails. The gate holds two worlds with a built-in answer, the frozen and the noise-swapped control (R4.10). [The combination is selected on seeds whose results the rule may use, so the held-out test is one test and not a search.]

- **R9.0 Stage 0 (development; before the freeze).**
  - *Worlds and seeds.* The worlds G2 and G3 of R9.1 on seeds 9001, 9002, 9003 (G1 and G4 are skipped: G1 is a repro check against M4 and development seeds have no M4 data), plus slab `mu30-s8-k256` (B0) on the same seeds, informational (it shows the sweep's own base reading and its dust state). Same job structure, controls and twin banks as the gate; slab time T0, 2D times T1, T2 (the 2D worlds are not read at T0, R1). Outputs under `runs/regime/dev/`, job ids prefixed `dev-`.
  - *Selection.* For each combination in the order **(P1, net), (P1, occ), (P2, net), (P2, occ)** evaluate G2, G3, G5, G6 and G7 of R9.2 on the development seeds (job classes, dust states and votes under that combination) and the margin condition below. The first combination that satisfies all of them, and for which the go/no-go reports and the threshold trigger below do not fail (go/no-go a for every combination, go/no-go b and the trigger in its own rung's bank), is **selected**: the Stage 0 outcome is STAGE0-PASS and the finalizer writes the combination into R1 (RUNG, DECIDER). If none does, the outcome is **STAGE0-FAIL**, printed with the causes of all four combinations.
  - *Margin condition.* A combination is `MARGINAL` (a failure cause) if, in any metered Stage 0 gate job, a decision rests on a quantity within its margin of a threshold: a dust ratio within MARGIN_RATIO of DUST_CLEAR or DUST_LIM; a pooled ratio `s(TAU_DEC)` within a factor MARGIN_S of D_LO or D_HI; a bootstrap stability within MARGIN_ST of STABLE; or a grid time at which both `satFrac` and `r` are within a factor MARGIN_S of SAT and R_AMP. [The margins are there so that a pass on held-out seeds is not a coin flip.]
  - *Go/no-go reports* ((a) is a cause of every combination; (b) and the threshold trigger below are causes of the combinations of their own rung only; (c) feeds the trigger): (a) the Z twin is exactly zero (C1) in every Stage 0 job; (b) the dust floor: in every metered job that has a U twin (*Missing evidence* below) the U twin's block-net count `n_blk(TAU_DEC) / max(liveblk_R, 1)` is at most SAT, so the unit-kick cascade does not blanket the world at the deciding time (cause `DUST-FLOOR`); (c) for the U twin at TAU_DEC, the median `|dB_blk|` over blocks with a nonzero difference, next to thetaB (it feeds the threshold trigger below). *Scope:* (a), (b), (c) and the threshold trigger read every metered Stage 0 job, B0 included, and each rung's bank separately; (b) always reads the U twin's block-net count with that bank's `thetaB`, whatever the DECIDER, not the combination's `n_dec`. *Missing evidence:* a metered job with no U log in a rung's bank (a FEW_SITES job runs no S twin, and the U twin shares S's kicked set, so it runs none either, R3.4) contributes nothing to (b), (c) or the trigger in that bank: it is neither a pass nor a failure, and the Stage 0 readout counts the jobs the U twin was read in and names the others. A U log that was run and shows no block of nonzero difference has coverage 0 and an empty set of nonzero blocks, so its median in (c) is NA (R0) and does not fire (c). A U log that exists is read by (b), (c) and the trigger even when its dose makes the job's dust state DUST_UNAVAILABLE (R4.8); what counts a job without a U twin against a gate world is G6 (`DUST-CONTROL-UNAVAILABLE`), not (b). **A failure of (b) in a rung's bank fails that rung's two combinations only** ((P1, net) and (P1, occ) for the P1 bank, (P2, net) and (P2, occ) for the P2 bank); a failure of (a) fails all four. The margin condition is read in the metered G2 and G3 jobs only (B0 is informational), on both sides of each threshold, edges inclusive.
  - *Repetition.* **The first STAGE0-PASS is final**: its combination is written into R1 (RUNG, DECIDER) and no further Stage 0 run is permitted, also when the threshold trigger below fired in the other rung's bank. After a STAGE0-FAIL on run 1 or 2 the finalizer may change J constants (R1) and rerun Stage 0, **at most twice** (three Stage 0 runs in all); every run and every change goes into the development log (R0.2). A decision not to rerun after a STAGE0-FAIL, and a STAGE0-FAIL on the third run, are **STAGE0-CLOSED**: candidate 8 ends (a decision not to rerun is entered in the development log; the readout prints STAGE0-CLOSED itself on the third run). Only J constants may change after seeing Stage 0, only after a STAGE0-FAIL (before the rerun), and only before the freeze; after a STAGE0-PASS every constant Stage 0 read stands unchanged to the freeze, and R15 governs any change after it. Nothing from seeds 101 to 106 or 1 to 6 may be used.
  - *Threshold trigger.* The trigger **fires in a rung's bank** if, in some metered Stage 0 job, the U twin's block-net dust at TAU_DEC in that bank exceeds `thetaB` (go/no-go c: the median `|dB_blk|` over its nonzero blocks is above `thetaB`) or covers more than SAT of the living blocks (go/no-go b: a failure of (b) fires the trigger, so such a rung shows both causes). **When it fires, that rung's two combinations fail with the cause `DUST-THRESHOLD`**; the other rung's combinations are not touched, so a trigger confined to one rung's bank does not by itself end Stage 0. `thetaB` (`ceil(k / 2)` under P1, R4.1; the S-derived value under P2, R4.9) is **never re-derived** from the U or N twin's dust or from anything else Stage 0 shows: a threshold that the dust overruns fails its rung, and J constants change only through the Repetition clause above. Nothing but RUNG and DECIDER is left to fill before the freeze.
  - *Constant screen* (a descriptive line in the Stage 0 readout; it decides nothing). On the seed-9001 B0 state at T0, for each of `dtQ`, `spread`, `thetaMass`, `massUnit`, `diffA`, `diffC`, `diffS`, `gateK`, `mutRate` at half and at twice its base value (or its range bound): fork R's state under the changed configuration, apply the S kick of the selected rung to one fork and none to its twin, run 300 steps, and report `n_dec,S(TAU_DEC)` and `n_1(100)` relative to the base configuration (about 70 s in all).

## R10 Outcomes

The target class is **refClass** from the gate (R9.2). "Target settings" are pool settings (the control excluded) whose class equals refClass. The readout evaluates the first clause that applies and prints that outcome (wording in R13).

1. **INVALID-RUN.** Any job is INVALID (R5), or the jobs carry more than one `ruleSha`, `codeCommit` or `srcHash`: print the job ids and the checks that failed. No outcome; the readout exits non-zero. Stop.
2. **GATE-FAIL.** The gate failed (R9.4). Print the failure causes of the selected combination, the other three combinations' readings as information only, and CLOSED. Stop.
3. **NO-CONTRAST.** The pool has at least POOL_MIN class-bearing settings (R8.3) and at least NO_CONTRAST of them have class refClass: reading refClass then says nothing about a band, because the instrument does not separate these settings from one another. No inference; the settings are listed. Stop.
4. **Target settings at T0.** Let TARGET0 be the class-bearing pool settings (the control excluded) whose class is refClass. If TARGET0 is not empty the late check (R11) decides: **PENDING-LATE-CHECK** while late jobs are missing, then **BAND-FOUND**, **LATE-INCONCLUSIVE** or **TRANSIENT-BAND**. Stop.
5. **If TARGET0 is empty,** the first of these that holds:
   1. **EMPTY-WINDOW:** the pool is empty (no setting has 2 valid jobs).
   2. **TOO-MANY-UNMETERABLE:** the number of UNMETERABLE settings (control excluded, 31 sweep settings) is above `UNMET_FRAC` of them.
   3. **THIN-POOL:** fewer than POOL_MIN pool settings are class-bearing, or the class-bearing settings differ from B0 along fewer than AXES_MIN swept axes between them.
   4. **SENSITIVE-EVERYWHERE:** more than `SENS_FRAC` of the valid jobs of the pool settings that have a U twin (dust state CLEAR, AMBIG or LIMITED) are AMBIG or LIMITED. Absence of votes is then not absence of a band. (Jobs without a U twin, FEW_SITES, count under UNDER-SUPPORTED instead.)
   5. **UNDER-SUPPORTED:** more than `SUPPORT_FRAC` of the valid jobs of the pool settings are D_ONLY (FEW_SITES, FEW_COLONIES or OVERLAP): the trajectory meter could not certify a band in most of them, so a lack of spatial support is not read as a lack of a band.
   6. **UNRESOLVED:** the number of SPLIT settings is above `UNRES_FRAC` of the pool. Run the extension (R8.5) once and evaluate R10 again from clause 3; if this clause still holds the outcome is **UNRESOLVED-FINAL**.
   7. **CROSSING-BRACKETED** (only when refClass is CRITICAL): a bracket exists.
   8. **NO-BAND.** Label: `all-ordered` if every class-bearing pool setting is ORDERED; `all-chaotic` if every one is CHAOTIC; otherwise `scattered`.
6. Outcomes 5.7 and 5.8 are followed by the axis ranking and the inference clause (R12). BAND-FOUND carries the sentence of R12.6 and no ranking. No other outcome carries an inference.

**Bracket.** Two class-bearing pool settings that differ in exactly one swept axis, adjacent in that axis's tested list, one ORDERED and one CHAOTIC. Tested lists: mu 20, 30, 40; sigma 8, 12, 16, 20; lightK 256, 1024, 4096 (only at equal mu and sigma); diffA 100, 200, 240; diffC 100, 200, 240; diffS 75, 150, 240; gateK 16, 64, 256, 1024; dtQ 26, 51, 102; spread 4, 8, 16. The C and D settings sit on the lists with B0 as the base value. Settings marked INERT (R12.3) form no bracket.

**Ties.** Several target settings: all are listed in the late-check order (R11). With 3 seeds no two classes can both reach VOTE, and equal counts below VOTE (1-1-1) give SPLIT; with 6 seeds (R8.5) a 3-3 split is SPLIT. Axis ties: R12.4.

## R11 Late check

- **Which settings.** LATE0 is the first LATE_MAX settings of TARGET0 in the order: B0 first, then settings that differ from B0 along fewer swept axes, then setting id. Settings of TARGET0 beyond the cap are `UNCHECKED-CAP` (listed, never run, never agreeing). For each setting in LATE0 run its seeds (the three, or the six of an extended setting, R8.5) at T = T1 with the selected rung and decider (job id suffix `t50000`). Late-check jobs exist only for the settings of LATE0 at the seeds of their T0 jobs; the reader refuses any other job at T1. Statuses, classes and dust states are as at T0, and C1 to C3 and C5 to C8 apply unchanged; C4 against M4's 50,000-step count applies only where R5 says (groups A and B, seeds 101 to 103). Each job re-runs the burn-in from scratch: the runner keeps no checkpoints (a slab run refuses them). [Continuing every windowed job to T1 instead would cost far more than re-burning at most LATE_MAX target settings.]
- **Per setting at T1** (its class is computed as in R8 from the T1 jobs): **AGREES** if it is windowed and its class equals refClass; **TRANSIENT** if its T1 status is UNMETERABLE, CARPET or RELIC, or its T1 class is SPLIT, DUSTY or a class other than refClass; **INCONCLUSIVE** if it is windowed with fewer than 2 valid jobs (INSUFFICIENT) after all its late jobs have completed (a rerun cannot change this: every draw is a function of seed, step and cell, so a job is deterministic); **PENDING** while a late job is missing or INVALID.
- **Outcome.** While any setting of LATE0 is PENDING the outcome is **PENDING-LATE-CHECK**, listing the missing job ids. Otherwise: **BAND-FOUND** if at least one setting AGREES (the settings that are TRANSIENT, INCONCLUSIVE or UNCHECKED-CAP are listed beside it); else **LATE-INCONCLUSIVE** if at least one setting is INCONCLUSIVE (no inference: the evidence at T1 is insufficient, not negative); else **TRANSIENT-BAND** (every setting of LATE0 is TRANSIENT; the UNCHECKED-CAP settings, if any, are listed). A critical reading at T0 that does not recur at T1 is a stage of the transient, reported as such; it is still a place to look by eye, and the readout lists its T0 job ids.

## R12 Axis ranking and inference

- **R12.1 Distance to the target.** refClass CRITICAL: `d(ell) = max(0, ln D_LO - ell, ell - ln D_HI)`. refClass CHAOTIC: `d(ell) = max(0, ln D_HI - ell)`. (`ell` is the log response ratio at TAU_DEC of the voting jobs, R8.4.)
- **R12.2 Ranking.** B0 must be in the pool with a class ORDERED, CRITICAL or CHAOTIC and `ell` defined (at least two voting jobs), else the readout prints **NO-RANKING** [a B0 with no class would give `d(B0)` = 0 and every Delta would mechanically fall below NO_AXIS]. For each swept axis a, the candidates are the class-bearing pool settings that differ from B0 along a alone and whose value on a is **adjacent to B0's in a's tested list**, one on each side where one exists (a minimum over more candidates is biased toward the axis that has more of them; adjacency gives at most two per axis): mu `mu20-s8-k256`, `mu40-s8-k256`; sigma `mu30-s12-k256`; lightK `mu30-s8-k1024`; diffA `-dA100`, `-dA240`; diffC `-dC100`, `-dC240`; diffS `-dS75`, `-dS240`; gateK `-gK16`, `-gK256`; dtQ `-dtQ26`, `-dtQ102`; spread `-sp4`, `-sp16`. `Delta_a = d(B0) - min over the candidates of d`; NA with no candidates. **If every `Delta_a` is NA (B0 qualifies but no swept axis has a class-bearing candidate next to its value, an INERT one excluded), the readout prints NO-RANKING as well, with its own sentence: no swept axis has a class-bearing setting next to B0 on its tested list (every Delta is NA), so no constant was compared and no axis can be ranked or excluded. It is not NO-AXIS (R12.4), which says that constants were compared and none moved the meters.** The best candidate is the one that attains the minimum; equal minima are broken by the smaller `|log2(x / x_B0)|`, then by the setting id ascending.
- **R12.3 Inert gateK (state-digest test).** A gateK setting is INERT if, for all three seeds, its `stateDigestT` equals B0's for the same seed. `stateDigestT` is the `stateHash` (R2.5) of R's state at T with its configuration replaced by B0's, so it covers cells, genomes, ledgers and step: with P identically 0 the gate factor is exactly 1 and the whole run is byte-identical apart from the configuration field. [Comparing sums would not prove equal states.] INERT settings are excluded from the candidates and from brackets and are listed.
- **R12.4 Order.** Axes sorted by `Delta_a` descending; axes within `TIE` of the top `Delta_a` are tied and are ordered by the smaller `|log2(x_best / x_B0)|`, then alphabetically. `NOISE` is the range (maximum minus minimum) of `ln(max(s(TAU_DEC), S_FLOOR))` over B0's voting jobs, the seed-to-seed spread of the very quantity the Deltas are made of. If the top `Delta_a` is below `max(NO_AXIS, NOISE)` (there is a top only when some `Delta_a` is defined; with none, R12.2 applies), print **NO-AXIS**: no swept constant moves the meters toward the target by more than a factor 1.4 or by more than B0's own seed-to-seed spread, so the levers are among the constants not swept (R2.3) or in the structure (kernel radius, topology). The readout then lists the constants not swept and, if it exists, the Stage 0 constant screen.
- **R12.5 Direction.** For the top axis: `raise` if `x_best > x_B0`, else `lower`.
- **R12.6 Inference.** Only outcomes BAND-FOUND, CROSSING-BRACKETED and NO-BAND carry one. Every sentence is followed by the reading scale of R13. Where a sentence speaks of criticality it is printed only when refClass is CRITICAL and AMP-ROBUST (R9.8); otherwise "critical" is replaced by "in the CRITICAL class at this kick scale".
  - BAND-FOUND: "No physics change is indicated by these meters: at least one swept setting reads like the project's alive 2D worlds at T0 and T1." No ranking is printed (the target is reached).
  - refClass CRITICAL, NO-BAND: "A physics change is indicated by these meters: the project's alive 2D worlds read critical and no swept slab setting does." The ranking names the constant to try first.
  - refClass CRITICAL, CROSSING-BRACKETED: "The critical manifold lies between the two bracketed settings; refine along that axis before any physics change." The ranking still prints.
  - refClass CHAOTIC, NO-BAND: "No swept slab setting reads like the project's alive 2D worlds (chaotic-like). This does not test the criticality hypothesis." The ranking names the constant that moves the meters most toward chaotic-like.
  - Never printed: "proof". The outcome is a pre-stated trigger for a physics investigation, and the ranking is an ordering by effect on the meters, not a causal claim.

Date: 2026-09-29. Status: **calibration complete; candidate fails the declared readiness gate and is not registered for confirmation**. Original M4 remains not met. ## Claim and executable definition

For each complete one-million-step history, compute

`g = [C_new(1,000,000) - C_new(500,000)] / 5`.

The units are threshold-crossing episodes per 100,000 steps. `C_new` counts crossing episodes, including possible recurrence after extinction, rather than necessarily unique innovations. The proposed endpoint requires treatment's mean g to exceed neutral's by more than 1 unit in each of gradient-m3 and spots-m3. This is a finite-window claim; bounded adaptation or repeated turnover may satisfy it without open-ended innovation.

The executable candidate is `m4-growth-v1.ts`, with real analysis in `tools/lib/m4-growth-runs.ts`. The detailed pre-outcome design and assumptions are in `docs/m4-growth-amendment-design.md`. Matched seeds share actual initial worlds and counter-based inputs; independent seed pairs, not observations within histories, are the statistical units. Use one-sided paired Student inference with alpha 0.005 per preset, preserving a 0.01 family bound across the two growth claims under the test assumptions. Zero estimated variance makes inference unavailable. Every completed extinction remains; incomplete, unexpected, duplicate or nonconserving histories make the requested matrix unavailable.

Reuse the compatible frozen activity thresholds 10,008 and 14,613 only with unchanged rule/schema/metrics/preset identities and the one-million-step, census-100, deep-10 schedule. Confirmation must rerun original endpoint 1 on the same fresh required histories and pass it as well as amended endpoint 2. Old endpoint-1 positives do not transfer. No broader held-out or M5 success is asserted.

## Required calibration and readiness decision

Independent validation uses frozen manifest `runs/foundations-next/m4-calibration-validation-v4-manifest.json`: 200 trials in each of ten scenario/preset strata, with 64 pairs per trial and master seed 650009002. Development uses a separate master. The declared strata include paired endpoint-1 null stress, rare-negative mean-boundary stress, extinction at the mean boundary, a mean-3 alternative, and periodic turnover. Every trial uses the declared activity construction and estimator. Synthetic provenance is explicit; these are not physically evolved worlds.

For each null stratum, the prespecified one-sided 95% binomial upper bound on false support must be at most 0.025. This is only a coarse inflation screen for a nominal 0.005 test. A pass would not establish its exact rare-event size. The alternative requires a one-sided 95% power lower bound above 0.8 and mean confidence margin at most 2. Original endpoint-1 size is also challenged in the equal-marginal and periodic null strata. A failed stratum is a blocker, not a reason to remove that stratum or silently redesign against these results.

Before confirmation, the final decision must address both empirical calibration and the adequacy of that coarse envelope. The alternative acceptance rule assesses endpoint-2 power, not power for the joint endpoint-1 AND endpoint-2 milestone gate. Joint support is recorded descriptively; n=64 is not a validated joint-gate allocation. Launch readiness also requires a separately frozen joint-power rationale or an explicit unresolved-power limitation. It must also reconcile the development pipeline, compatible thresholds, measured costs, exact future seeds, independent review and immutable code/manifest freeze. A failed calibration or insufficient precision is a valid negative outcome of this investigation; it leaves this candidate unregistered.

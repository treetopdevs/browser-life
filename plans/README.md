## Findings considered and rejected

- **No-op mutations minting lineage IDs** (`packages/sim-ref/src/step.ts:515-521`): real, but about 0.3% of mutation events, so too small to matter. Lineage-ID turnover in general is the real problem, handled in 005.
- **Compartment detector blind spots** (thin or hollow membranes fall outside the B+P ≥ 48 component): LOW confidence that it explains M4's zero compartments. A cheap first check (polymer pools in `series.jsonl`) should precede any plan.
- **Evaluator-level or analytic evolvability score for founder selection:** founder 7's mutants change role in the evaluator in 27–32 of 32 seeds, yet it originates no role in open worlds (founder-policy workspace), and the weights-only probe already failed validation. Not now.
- **Red Queen time-shift rerun, island-biogeography sweep, full anticipation run, POET-style environment generation, building duplication or silent slots, scheduling exploratory runs through the coordinator:** low information for the current question, or large builds before the cheap readouts (004, the upper-bound world) say which lever binds.
- **Rewriting the recorded recurrence verdict:** `docs/plan.md` already states "mixed, leaning one-shot" by hand. 003 puts the rule in code and writes `fr-v2.json` beside `fr.json`, rather than overwriting the record.
- **Changing `pickSubjects`'s tie-break in `tools/lib/obligates-x.ts`:** it differs from the plan's lowest-index convention, but changing it during a run would alter the subjects. Recorded in 001's maintenance notes instead.

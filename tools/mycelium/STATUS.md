- **R2b** (does heredity matter, GPU, 100,000 steps): **MEASUREMENT-LIMITED at calibration; confirmation never run** (13 Nov 2026). Protocol `docs/mycelium-r2b-2026-11-13/PROTOCOL.md`, Freeze 1 and Freeze 2 in `FROZEN`, result in `docs/mycelium-r2b-2026-11-13/RESULT.md`. In plain words: 100 dev runs (5 arms x 20 seeds, 100,000 steps, ledger residual 0 throughout) showed that the inherit worlds mostly lose their trail before the window (steps 50,000-100,000), so the trail-following null could not be matched (median deposit rate 2e-6 against a 1/16 floor that sits above every arm's rate), the structure readout (Moran's I) was measurable in 1 of 20 seeds (8 needed), and a PASS became unattainable. Two life-history readouts (median age and turnover) responded to a positive control and were kept. An independent skeptic recomputed every number and could not change the label. Not evidence about heredity either way. Heredity-blocked worlds kept far more trail than inherit ones, as a description only. No amendment was made (two are allowed before a first confirmation run). Code: `r2b.ts` (runner), `r2b-analyze.ts`, `r2b-protocol.ts`, `r2b-seeds.ts`, `r2b-decide.ts`, `r2b-stats.ts`, `r2b-report.ts`, `r2b-freeze.ts`, `observe-r2b.ts`. The R2 files and their `FROZEN` are untouched, and R2's frozen code hash no longer matches (its provenance is commit c0966c4c). The `/mycelium/` page and its viewing tools (brushes, zoom, GPU renderer, `brush.ts`) are not part of any frozen hash.
- **R3** (is the missing trail purged by selection? directional, 14 Nov 2026): **PASS** (purge supported) on 16 fresh slots (6001-6016), 7 arms, 100,000 steps. Protocol `docs/mycelium-r3-2026-11-13/PROTOCOL.md`, freezes in `FROZEN`, result in `docs/mycelium-r3-2026-11-13/RESULT.md`. Readouts are defined at zero trail (window means of the deposit rate d and trail mass; early window (0, 5,000], late (50,000, 100,000]). D1 inherit's late deposit below blocked's (Cliff's delta -0.875, Holm p 6e-6), D2 a larger relative fall from early to late (delta -0.883, Holm p 8e-6), D3 a dose response across deposit cost 1, 2, 4 against blocked at each cost (tau +0.50, p 2e-4); the frozen arm (no mutation) also ends with zero late deposit probes in 13 of 16 runs, so the low deposit does not need mutation (consistent with selection on the standing founders; drift among founder lineages not separated). A direction test, not a measurement of selection; says nothing about R2b's heredity-vs-null question, which stays measurement-limited. Two analyses (TypeScript and independent Python) agree; kernel byte-identical to R2b's; uncommitted. R3 here is a purge test, not the plan's genotype-pool rung (that rung is still not started).
- **R4** (is the kept trail USED or only paid for? toll rule, `moveCost` 12, strength 64; 14 Nov 2026): **TOLL-ONLY** on 16 fresh slots (7001-7016), 5 arms (inherit, blind-gradient lesion, matched physarum anchor, blocked, keyless), 100,000 steps; branch `claude/mycelium-trail-use` on the unchanged kernel e5b43f69. Protocol `docs/mycelium-r4-2026-11-14/PROTOCOL.md`, freezes in `FROZEN`, result in `RESULT.md`. Four endpoints (ALIGN of intended movement with the trail gradient, OCC concentration on trail, ACUTE change when the gradient inputs are zeroed, SENS dependence of all five outputs on them) all lie inside +-15% of what the physarum follower shows on dev (margins fixed from 20 dev runs): ALIGN +0.014 [-0.004, 0.038] against 0.162, OCC -0.022 [-0.055, 0.005] against 0.919, ACUTE -0.003 [-0.007, -0.002] against 0.149, SENS 0.0031 [0.0027, 0.0040] against 0.0139; 99.6% of particles request exactly one unit, the discount is complete at essentially every particle position (median `sat` 1.000), retention 15/16 inherit, 13/16 blind, 16/16 physarum and blocked, 0/16 keyless (the toll condition keeps the trail). Readouts can see use: physarum ALIGN +0.993, OCC 7.2. Says nothing about use of the local trail level (input 2), selection or other settings; in-state contrasts are survivor-conditioned. Two analyses agree (TypeScript, independent Python: 0 of 1,520 readout mismatches; one declared bootstrap-endpoint allowance exceeded by 4% on SENS, exact interval shows it is Monte Carlo, status unaffected). Uncommitted; kernel byte-identical to e5b43f69; no amendment.

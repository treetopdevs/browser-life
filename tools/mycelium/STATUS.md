# Mycelium world: status (13 Nov 2026)

Kept apart from `RULES.md` on purpose: `RULES.md` is covered by the R2 `code_hash` (`docs/mycelium-r2-2026-11-13/PROTOCOL.md` section 10), so editing it would invalidate the frozen R2 calibration. This file is not hashed.

- **R0** (CPU reference, `world.ts`): done. Matter and energy residuals are exactly 0.
- **R1** (WebGPU port, `gpu.ts`): CPU and GPU match bit for bit on all 30 cases of `tests/deno/mycelium_gpu.ts` (R2b added the physarum and blind cases and two resume variants). Three unfixed edge-case review notes (counters and step past 2^53, read/upload overlap) are listed in `docs/mycelium-r2-2026-11-13/RESULT.md`. Since R2b the physarum arm (with `physarumDeposit`) and `blindTrailGradient` are on the GPU too, with parity cases.
- **R2** (does heredity matter?): **MEASUREMENT-LIMITED** on the five dev seeds (no readout kept, confirmation not run). Not a FAIL and not evidence about heredity either way. See `docs/mycelium-r2-2026-11-13/RESULT.md`. Restarting needs a dated amendment or a new protocol R2b.
- **R3 and later** (genotype pool, bonds, learning, lesions, lineage files): not started, and the gate they wait on (R2, then R2b) was not passed: both were measurement-limited.
- **R2b** (does heredity matter, GPU, 100,000 steps): **MEASUREMENT-LIMITED at calibration; confirmation never run** (13 Nov 2026). Protocol `docs/mycelium-r2b-2026-11-13/PROTOCOL.md`, Freeze 1 and Freeze 2 in `FROZEN`, result in `docs/mycelium-r2b-2026-11-13/RESULT.md`. In plain words: 100 dev runs (5 arms x 20 seeds, 100,000 steps, ledger residual 0 throughout) showed that the inherit worlds mostly lose their trail before the window (steps 50,000-100,000), so the trail-following null could not be matched (median deposit rate 2e-6 against a 1/16 floor that sits above every arm's rate), the structure readout (Moran's I) was measurable in 1 of 20 seeds (8 needed), and a PASS became unattainable. Two life-history readouts (median age and turnover) responded to a positive control and were kept. An independent skeptic recomputed every number and could not change the label. Not evidence about heredity either way. Heredity-blocked worlds kept far more trail than inherit ones, as a description only. No amendment was made (two are allowed before a first confirmation run). Code: `r2b.ts` (runner), `r2b-analyze.ts`, `r2b-protocol.ts`, `r2b-seeds.ts`, `r2b-decide.ts`, `r2b-stats.ts`, `r2b-report.ts`, `r2b-freeze.ts`, `observe-r2b.ts`.

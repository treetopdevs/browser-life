Breeder

At each pond cycle every pond is cleared and reseeded from a few donor ponds. Left alone, the world's own rule chooses the donors.

Select a pond on the field to add or remove it. Each pond shows its number and, after #, its rank by the measure above. Donors seed the ponds in turn, in the order picked.

    Field tools

    Feed and Drain change the world's total matter. Each use is logged as an intervention, and the ledger counts it.

    Checkpoints

    Save keeps a checkpoint in this browser. Export downloads one as a file. Both settle the world at its next census first.

    Save and Export are off while a pond cycle waits: the world stands before its cycle, and a checkpoint cannot hold that. They return once you breed.

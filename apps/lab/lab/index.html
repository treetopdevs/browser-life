Plant a world

Same starting world and seed number: the same history, every time. Anything you do by hand is extra, and logged.

Breed by hand

At each pond cycle every pond is cleared and reseeded from a few donor ponds. Left alone, the world's own rule chooses them.

Click a pond on the field to add or remove it. Each pond shows its number and, after #, its rank by the measure above. Donors seed the ponds in turn, in the order picked.

    In your hand

    Feeding and draining open the jar. Each use is logged, and the jar check counts what you added.

    Keep a copy

    Save keeps a copy in this browser. Download gives you a file to keep or share. Both wait for the next census, so the copy is complete.

    Save and Download come back once you breed: a copy holds a world between pond cycles, not one paused inside a cycle.

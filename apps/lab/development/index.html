Reading the instructions

Each cell follows a small growth program.

A cell tries the direction named by its program. At set depths, the program turns or adds side branches. New cells keep the place where they grew, so each body remains connected.

Parents pass a copied program to each child. The mutation world may alter one gene in that copy. Color tracks the original founder family; lighter or darker shades distinguish program variants. Selection and ancestry follow body IDs, never color.

Both worlds share the starting seed, locations, food and founders. The worlds are deterministic within the same browser runtime. Reproduction, lifespan, food renewal and the population limit are designed rules with known scheduling effects; births or survival do not show adaptation.

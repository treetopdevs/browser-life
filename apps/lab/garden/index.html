The shared garden · the commons

One world, grown in turns.

The commons is one big storm world that islands grow a day at a time, at most one day each dawn. It grows only while someone tends it, and this page says so: how far it has grown, how long it has slept, and which days a second island has replayed. Anyone can drop a stone in it.

Your guess

Your receipts

Your guess stays on this device. The garden has no route for guesses and this page sends none, so nothing is won, ranked or kept anywhere else; a receipt (the day, your choice, when you made it, and what the page showed then, with a fingerprint of it) convinces only you, since you could edit it. Once the next day is verified, this page checks your receipt against the garden’s public numbers, or says why it cannot. The answer is not hidden either: each day’s end state becomes a public checkpoint once it and every day before it have been replayed, and the physics is exact, so anyone can replay the next day before it is released. Guess for fun; a held receipt is not proof of foresight.

Today’s letter

Drop a stone

A stone is a wound at a place: where it lands, bodies turn to waste and their stored energy to heat. It is a small nudge to one patch of the garden, and we do not know yet how long its mark lasts. It lands at the end of a day and is public from the moment you send it. It carries a place and a size, and nothing about you.

Or send a sun

A sun dims the whole sky for one day (on some worlds it can brighten it instead). From the end of the day it lands on, the world’s light is the share of its own you choose, then its own light returns. Light is energy, so no matter enters or leaves the jar. One sun a day, and it takes one of the day’s letter places.

Reading the numbers

Honest about time

The garden has no server of its own growing it. Islands, computers lending it their GPU, run one day of steps each; when nobody comes, it sleeps, and this page says for how long. Missed dawns do not pile up: an island that comes after a quiet week gets one day, not seven.

A day counts once a second island has replayed it and got the same state, bit for bit. When both ran on the same browser and GPU, it was replayed here, not yet verified elsewhere; a different browser or GPU may still be the same computer. Replayed means the same rules produced the same state; it does not make anything in the garden a result.

The shared garden · the commons

One world, grown in turns.

The commons is one big storm world that volunteer islands grow a day at a time, at most one day each dawn. It grows only while someone tends it, and this page says so: how far it has grown, how long it has slept, and which days a second island has replayed. Anyone can drop a stone in it.

Drop a stone

A stone is a wound at a place: where it lands, bodies turn to waste and their stored energy to heat. It lands at the end of a day and is public from the moment you send it. It carries a place and a size, and nothing about you.

Reading the numbers

Honest about time

The garden has no server of its own growing it. Islands, browser tabs lending their GPU, run one day of steps each; when nobody comes, it sleeps, and this page says for how long. Missed dawns do not pile up: an island that comes after a quiet week gets one day, not seven.

A day counts once a second island has replayed it and got the same state, bit for bit. When both ran on the same browser and GPU, it was replayed here, not yet verified elsewhere; a different browser or GPU may still be the same computer. Replayed means the same rules produced the same state; it does not make anything in the garden a result.

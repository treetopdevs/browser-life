Soft Roam · going to the food

Bodies that glide to their food

Each soft shape is a body: cells joined together, grown from a plan the cells inherit. Food drops onto the ground in patches and spreads out as a blue haze until it is eaten. A cell that has eaten enough divides; a daughter that stays joined grows the body, and one that is let go starts a new body. A cell that starves or wears out dies, and its matter goes back onto the ground as food. This is Roam taken off its grid of tiles: cells push each other apart and joined cells pull on each other like springs, so a body glides, bends and turns instead of hopping from square to square. Every body begins as a single cell and grows by its plan, so give it a minute.

Designed ingredients: the growth plans, the kinds of cell (shells, mouths and leaves), the sun, the senses and the rules for healing and turning are all written in. Bodies are not declared: a body is whatever cells happen to be joined. Every rule is in the header of apps/lab/src/softroam/kernel.ts, and the notes are in docs/softroam-2026-11-25/ in the repository.

What we found

Soft Roam: off the grid Exploratory · 25 Nov Off the grid, bodies slide and bend smoothly, but they stayed small, mouths took over and no hunter was seen to turn toward prey. Seeds 1 and 2 only; nothing confirmed on unseen seeds.

Soft Roam: a year, bigger bodies, speeds Exploratory · 26 Nov A yearly cycle showed in the cell count on four seen seeds, and healing made bodies of 10 or more common, though 20 or more stayed rare. The speed round failed half its own bar. Nothing confirmed on unseen seeds.

Soft Roam: levels and an inner pulse Exploratory · 27 Nov With three levels all stayed lived in all year, but the floor held 80 to 88% of cells. A quarter to a third of cells beat with no clock, yet beating was as common when it did nothing. Seeds 1 and 2 only; nothing confirmed on unseen seeds.

What you are seeing

  • A body: one soft shape, coloured by the founder it comes from, with a dark seam where it meets another.
  • Food: a patch drops now and then and spreads out as haze until it is eaten.
  • A strand: matter flowing from a cell to the joined one that has less; brighter means a bigger difference.
  • A mouth: the only cell that feeds on other bodies, dusky rose at rest and hot red while it bites.
  • A shell: pale armour that does not eat and costs little to keep; it cracks after enough bites.
  • A leaf: lives on light, so it earns most in the daylight band that sweeps round the arena.

How the big bodies live

A bright band half the arena wide sweeps round once a day, and the whole world brightens and dims once a year. Green leaf cells live on that light. Hunters see prey from a distance and prey run from hunters, and a body only lets go of a new body once it has grown big enough.

A body heals: where a cell dies, cross-braces hold the rest together and the cell that made it grows it again. A body with a mouth that sees prey turns as a whole after it, and a body that sees a hunter turns away. Several mouths together can open a body too big for one, two bodies that could each eat the other leave each other alone, and hunters prefer prey no other hunter is at. A share of the upkeep is kept for the leaves, and winter costs: every cell pays a little more in the dark of the year, so winter thins bodies and the numbers swing with the year.

Under Depth and Inner pulse in the dials are the two newest pieces. Levels give the world a floor, a surface and water between. The inner pulse gives every cell a small inherited network that passes two signals along its bonds; it can hold back a cell’s push and lengthen or shorten its springs. There is no clock: a beat is whatever the network and the bonds make, and in the picture it shows as light moving along a body.

Limits of what you see. Every setting was tuned by watching seeds 1 and 2, with seeds 3 and 4 looked at once in some rounds, so a world on any other seed is one nobody has looked at, and nothing on this page has been confirmed on unseen seeds. Bodies of 20 cells or more stay rare, no hunter has been seen to curve after prey by eye, and nothing here says beating helps a body. The world steps in your browser at the speed shown above, and the same seed and settings always give the same history. Watching never changes the run.

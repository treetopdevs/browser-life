The slab worlds, live.

Two thin three-dimensional worlds run on your graphics card: three types that each beat the next in a ring, and the main ecology on a slab. You see one tile per layer (layer 0 is the surface, where light enters) and a top-down view of all layers. Neither world passed its gate; this page is for looking, not for evidence.

Exploration, not a result. The default choices and the seeds marked recorded come from committed records, named on the page. Other choices (the kill on from step 0, the smaller ecology size, any other seed) were never recorded. There is no registered setting: cyclic competition stopped at Gate 0, and the ecology calibration found no setting that qualified.

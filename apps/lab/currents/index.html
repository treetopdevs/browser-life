Can a nutrient conveyor keep the growth zone supplied?

Four tanks share one starting state and one clock. Rain and a selective nutrient-only conveyor are varied independently; every tank continues through tick 6,000, including after biomass reaches zero.

Designed pump: every four ticks, nutrient follows 32 paired-column loops. The arrows mark supplied path direction, not measured fluid velocity or biomass movement. No biomass or detritus is carried; burial is off. Closed tanks retain a finite reservoir that is counted but unused.

How to read this

Translation is counted; supply is measured.

The pump translates all nutrient along four-tick pulses. “Nutrient unit-moves” is a cumulative translation ledger, even if a uniform field looks unchanged; it is not net delivery or an energy budget.

The nutrient-difference channel compares each current tank with its matched still sibling at the same tick. Both use a fixed −8 to +8 scale with zero neutral; clipped values are saturated. Brightness in the stock channels uses a common square-root scale.

These are designed material-flow rules. Biomass patches are not organisms, and a surviving biomass stock is not evidence of ongoing evolution.

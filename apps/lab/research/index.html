RESEARCH LOG / REVIEWED 27 SEP 2026

What the evidence says

The goal is to test for sustained organization and evolution. The results below are bounded by their documented experiments.

OBSERVED

Regenerating founders

The M3 screen found genetically distinct candidates that recovered after a 30% lesion and died in a no-light control under the specified test. Stronger retesting narrowed the founder set to 12 genomes.

Passing that gate does not establish indefinite evolution.

CALIBRATED

Activity thresholds

A neutral-only pilot fixed the activity threshold for each registered preset before the long-run comparison: 10,008 for gradient-m3 and 14,613 for spots-m3.

These are measurement cutoffs, not positive evolutionary findings.

PENDING

Long-run claims

The main ensemble asks whether adaptive activity, continued growth, and ecological roles exceed predeclared controls over the observation window. The preregistration is still a draft; no positive M4 or M5 result is claimed here.

METHOD

Checks before claims

Runs are compared across conditions and seeds. Conservation checks, CPU/GPU golden tests, and replay verification help detect implementation drift. Observation files still have their own provenance limits, so the research keeps verification and interpretation separate.

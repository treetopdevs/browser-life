Ingredients

A designed material loop with a moving light.

The light band moves across the upper 36 rows. Lit biomass can turn nutrient into biomass, then extend into nearby empty tiles. Biomass loss becomes detritus; detritus can be recycled to nutrients or settle downward.

Threshold relaxation redistributes nutrient only when neighboring amounts differ by at least four. Rain moves units from a finite shared-size source into the top row. Burial permanently moves detritus from the lower marked rows into sediment. The bars use the same initial-total scale, with exact counts beside them.

Color brightness uses the same fixed square-root scale in all tanks and clips above N 32, B 64, and D 16. Stocks show current locations; transfer counts and unit-moves are cumulative. These designed rules describe this toy only.

How to read it

Labels say how far to trust a row

Confirmed is kept for tests whose rules were frozen before their data. Negative means a test with pre-stated rules came back no. Measured and Exploratory are counts and looks without that freeze: real numbers, few seeds, no claim beyond them. Open and Unclear mean the test could not tell, usually because the instrument failed its own check first. That is different from a no.

Nothing on this page is evidence of open-ended evolution.

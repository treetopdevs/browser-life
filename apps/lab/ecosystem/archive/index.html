How to read these recordings

These are the original three recorded 20,000-tick observations, not newly generated runs. Raw censuses were sampled every 100 ticks. The source’s captureEvery: 25 field describes movie frames: 801 frames at 20 fps represent 25 ticks each (40.05 seconds). The browser copies were re-encoded at 192 × 192 pixels; resizing and re-encoding are lossy and do not preserve a physical snapshot.

Movie tick is estimated from the displayed frame time using the archived 20 fps rate, rounded down to the prior 25-tick sample and capped at 20,000. The videos are not frame-locked to one another. Family and trait charts summarize the entire recorded trajectory and do not follow seeking.

Refuge and drought rules were enabled in these runs, but their outlines are not drawn in the movies. Seed 1103 is retained with its observed Jade and Hunter losses. The model is engineered: food supply, family selection, traits, introductions, refuges and weather are specified by the experiment.

An assay check, before any tournament

Can a head-to-head contest tell a few known organisms apart?

Five organisms, each planted in small discs on a small tile, two at a time, with mutation switched off. The score is who holds more biomass after a fixed time. Before trusting that score, we check it against three things it must get right: a deliberately weakened copy must lose, the same organism under two names must not win by name or by position, and a contest where both sides die out must be called unreadable, not a tie.

CPU only: small reference-simulator tiles, no GPU evidence. Local experiment, kept off the public site. This is a sensitivity check, not a tournament and not the M3 environment.

What this does not show

  • A sensitivity check on five genetically separated organisms in small CPU tiles. It is not the M3 environment: one fixed chemistry, mutation off, a few hundred steps.
  • A result is the share of biomass held at a step. It is not reproduction or fitness, and a win can simply be vigour: see the growing-alone view.
  • The cycle test was proved on a synthetic A-beats-B-beats-C-beats-A fixture. "None detected" on the real organisms is not "none exist".
  • The final-time, even-start numbers are the primary analysis, but every result on this page is exploratory (see the freeze note below). Other times, the rare start, and the growing-alone residual decide nothing.
  • An interval that contains an even split is not evidence of equality. Ten seed blocks give wide intervals.
  • The same-organism position check is low power: ten blocks, a threshold fitted on three other seeds, and single-seed place effects reached +0.20. Passing it means no bias was seen at this resolution, not that none exists.
  • The rare start is raw and not readable alone: one disc against three loses share to arrangement even for the same organism under two names. The evidence also stores a version corrected by that control per seed; it is exploratory, and the control is itself only six seeds.
  • Exploratory throughout: nothing was committed before the evidence was first written, so the freezing of thresholds is not independently timestamped, and fixes after review were made knowing the earlier outcomes.
  • The weakened copy is one manipulation, a sensitivity floor and not a spectrum of effect sizes.
  • No archive of past generations exists yet, so there is no time-versus-opponent history and no tournament. Contest time here means steps inside one contest.

    How to read the pictures

    • Green tint: where this world may offer food right now.
    • Gold squares: the tiles the most recent food pulse actually picked. A pulse offers 768 units; a tile picked twice shows once. Before the first pulse there are none.
    • Outline: the band's edges. A dashed outline is a band that is not receiving the main share now.
    • Faint green wash: food on the tile (brighter is fuller). Coloured squares: body cells, one colour per founding family.

What is supplied?

Links form between nearby same-family particles, apply equal-and-opposite steering, and break when stretched or when a particle dies. Their steering affects motion and movement-energy cost; no spring potential energy is added to the ledger.

Births belong to individual particles. Newborns may form links on a later tick, but connected groups do not reproduce as units. A connected component describes graph connectivity, not a separate organism.

The lines show current links along the shortest route across the torus. Edge summaries count current components, isolated particles, degree and bond turnover. This is a separate 2D toy, not the conserved-matter research simulation.

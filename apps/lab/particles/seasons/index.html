What is supplied?

Each seasonal phase lasts the listed number of ticks; a full cycle has three phases. The row multipliers 1.75×, 1× and 0.25× add to the same nine-patch offered renewal budget as still food. Actual renewal can be lower when patches are full, and feeding still competes for available stock.

The row strokes and labels show the renewal multiplier applied at each world's last completed tick. Before the first step, phase 1 is ready for tick 1. The next-change label gives an absolute tick number. Fill strength shows current stock. Particles are supplied a weak pull toward the nearest patch, without checking which patch is richest. Extinct worlds keep their phase and food frozen at the extinction tick while the comparison clock continues.

Population history shows the latest 3,000 ticks. A population rebound is a demographic observation, not evidence of adaptation or independent recovery fitness. This is a separate 2D toy, not the conserved-matter research simulation.

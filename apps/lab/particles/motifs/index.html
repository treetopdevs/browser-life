What happens when familiar rules meet?

How to read the gallery

Compare the recipes, then inspect the worlds.

Food renewal is supplied by the habitat. The chase interaction steers families toward other family colours; it does not make particles eat one another. Family colours show the three inherited families.

Population and motion are observations of this open-energy toy. They do not establish adaptation, evolution, or a survival advantage. Each card reports local measurements without ranking or filtering the compositions.

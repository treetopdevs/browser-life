What we put into this world

Built ingredients. Changing patterns.

These are deliberately designed attraction and repulsion rules, with renewable food, finite lifespans and energy shared at birth. A weak built-in food-seeking pull draws every particle toward its nearest patch. Pursuit means chase-and-flee motion; particles do not eat each other.

The world holds at most 240 particles. Births blocked by that limit are counted above. Extinct worlds stay empty until you reset. Mutation changes inherited movement traits, but motion alone is not evidence of adaptation or open-ended evolution.

A separate 2D toy, not the conserved-matter research simulation. Repeatability is checked within the same runtime. The page pauses when hidden and stays paused when you return.

What is supplied?

Inherited mutation can change movement traits in offspring. The food-seeking pull toward each world’s nearest patch is supplied steering. Travelling food is also supplied: the nine patches move in alternating directions while carrying their current stock.

The “inside food patches” measure is a geometric snapshot of particle positions. It does not show that a particle fed. Motion or population change alone does not establish adaptation. Extinct worlds stay empty until restart. This is a separate 2D toy, not the conserved-matter research simulation.

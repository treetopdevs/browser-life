You choose recipes. Particles inherit traits.

Your choices generate nearby attraction, alignment and food-renewal settings. Separately, particles reproduce and may inherit small changes inside each world. Human preference is not organism evolution, and motion alone does not show adaptation.

Mint, amber and lilac mark founding families. Short lines show current velocity. Brighter circles hold more food. A weak engineered pull guides particles toward the nearest patch; pursuit is chase-and-flee motion, not predation. Food renews, lifespans are finite and the population is capped at 240 per world.

This is a separate 2D toy, not the conserved-matter research kernel. Worlds advance together by fixed ticks. Extinct worlds stay empty; their extinction time is shown. Repeatability is within the same runtime. Hiding the page pauses it until you press Play.

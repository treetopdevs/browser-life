THE IDEA

Life-like behavior is a question, not a setting.

Cells obey local rules for movement, resources and inheritance. The project measures whether organized, evolving behavior appears across many runs and controls. A vivid world is a starting observation; evidence comes from repeated tests.

The current work has found regenerating founder candidates. Long-run claims about open-ended evolution remain under test.

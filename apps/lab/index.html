THE IDEA

Life-like behavior is a question, not a setting.

Cells obey local rules for movement, resources and inheritance. The project measures whether organized, evolving behavior appears across many runs and controls. A vivid world is a starting observation; evidence comes from repeated tests.

The first long-run test for open-ended evolution did not pass. The work now asks what was missing; the latest results are below.

RESEARCH LOG / 1 OCT 2026

Latest results

Read the full log →
  1. 1 OctA life cycle for groups replicates. With ponds cleared and reseeded from a successful pond each cycle, heritable differences between ponds and the regrowth advantage of cycled ponds both held in 6 of 6 fresh histories. Exploratory, and the cycle is imposed rather than discovered.
  2. 29 SepVariation stalls. Genomes are copied faithfully, but mutation rarely gives a genome a new ecological role, and tracked births turned out to be weak evidence of reproduction.
  3. 28 SepM4 not met. In 170 long runs, mutation beat both controls in one of two worlds, but neutral runs counted as growing too.

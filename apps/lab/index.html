A garden of tiny sealed worlds

Grow something surprising.

Cadence Garden keeps little worlds in sealed jars: light comes in, heat goes out, and nothing else crosses the glass unless you open it. Plant one, feed it, wound it, breed it, and see what grows back. Nothing in there was told to be alive.

What's in a jar

Five ingredients. No recipe for life.

Every world is closed in matter and open to light. Inside, four kinds of matter turn into each other, and whatever lives has to make its living from that. The lab shows them in these same colours.

The sun

Light in, heat out.The only thing that crosses the glass. It can be steady, a gradient, a season, or a sun that wanders across the jar.

Nutrient · blue

Dissolved food.What light turns into biomass. When it runs out, something has to recycle.

Biomass · green

The living stuff.It grows, divides, moves, and carries a genome that mutates when it is copied.

Membrane · white

Walls that bodies build.They decay, so staying in one piece takes constant work.

Waste · brown

What's left after work.Someone has to turn it back into food, or the whole jar stalls.

The seal

Nothing lost, nothing made.The rules conserve matter exactly. The lab checks the accounting as the world runs, logs every time you open the jar, and can replay a run bit for bit on another supported GPU.

The working hunch

Give life room to change.

Many steady worlds fill up and settle down. Some of the liveliest results came when space and resources opened up again: a wound, a pond reset, a moving patch of light.

So we're turning up the possibilities. Combine changing environments, useful signals, varied founders and selection among groups. Look for the combinations that produce something worth watching.

The ambition: amplify a compelling trait tenfold. That's a search target, not a result. Too much disturbance can erase the very bodies we want to grow.

Where this is going

From a strange little jar
to a garden we share.

Storm worlds and the breeder are in the lab as sandbox worlds, exploratory and not registered. The naturalist and the shared garden are what we want to grow toward.

  1. 1
    In the lab

    Storm worlds

    Moving light, seasons, wounds and inherited shapes in one world. Find a rhythm that keeps opening possibilities without wiping everything out.

  2. 2
    In the lab

    The breeder

    Choose which ponds seed the next generation by a trait we want to amplify. In the lab you can already pick the donor ponds by hand; the aim is a loop where people pick what fascinates them.

  3. 3
    An idea

    An AI naturalist

    A model that watches worlds, notices unfamiliar behavior and helps steer the next experiment. A companion for exploring artificial life in the age of AI.

  4. 4
    Someday

    The shared garden

    A persistent archipelago. Creatures moving between islands. Visitors feeding a world, shifting its sun or dropping a meteor. Games and collaborative spaces that keep evolving while we're away.

  5. 5
    Someday

    More ways to be alive

    Predation, richer body plans and new ways to interact. Change the rules where today's worlds hit a ceiling, and see what becomes possible.

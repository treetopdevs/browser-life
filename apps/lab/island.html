This browser as an island

Press Join and this tab runs simulation segments on your GPU and sends the results back. It can use a lot of power. Some of the work is replayed elsewhere and compared bit for bit. Press Stop or close the tab to stop taking new work. What gets sent?

Joining makes your GPU available. Work starts when a researcher queues an experiment, so the island may wait between segments. See what's queued.

How to read this view

Depth is part of the world.

All three coordinates affect movement and wrap at the cube boundaries. The wire cube is a perspective view of those positions; markers farther from the camera appear smaller. Food centers are shown only when enabled.

The optional slice filters displayed particles by torus distance from its z center. The full population count stays visible beside the displayed count. This is an engineered toy world, separate from the conserved-matter research simulation; movement and turnover alone do not show adaptation.

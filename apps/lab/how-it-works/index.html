FIELD GUIDE / 01

How the world works

A browser simulation with local rules, a fixed energy source, and no predeclared organisms.

THE SUBSTRATE

Cells and resources

Each cell occupies a position in a grid and carries material, energy, and a set of inherited numeric weights. Light supplies energy. Matter moves and changes form within the world. A ledger checks that the accounting balances as the simulation advances.

The rules use integer arithmetic so identical starting states can be checked against a CPU reference and replayed bit for bit on supported GPUs.

WHAT WE OBSERVE

Individuals are measured

The program does not place a ready-made species into the world. It tracks connected structures, lineages, splitting, merging, mutations, resource roles, and recovery after a lesion. Those labels are measurements of patterns produced by the rules.

Those measurements have limits. A recent check found that the parent the tracker credits with a birth often contributed no copied material, so tracked births are not treated as proof of reproduction on their own.

One attractive image is not evidence of evolution. The research compares repeated runs with controls that remove heredity, mutation, or environmental features.

TRY IT

A useful first experiment

  1. Open the lab and choose a preset and seed.
  2. Press Play and watch the population and matter ledger.
  3. Switch to Inspect and click a cell; then try a lesion.
  4. Create the same world again to compare its trajectory.

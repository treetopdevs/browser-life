Wall gate

Reading the measurements

Connected regions use four-neighbor wrapping and a density threshold of 0.35. A region is counted only at four or more cells; smaller regions are listed separately. These are image regions, not organisms. A rising region count does not mean that anything divided.

Sampled change is the mean absolute density change and the fraction of threshold-mask cells that flipped during the last completed sample interval. It updates every 100 ticks, or at extinction. The wall metric counts edges gated by the current wall field. History in a downloaded observation keeps at most 300 samples.

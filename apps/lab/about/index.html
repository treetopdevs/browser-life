ABOUT / THE PROJECT

For the joy of strange life

Browser Life is an open playground for evolving life forms, built from a forty-year fascination with cellular automata and the worlds they might become.

PURPOSE

Why build it?

The spark is personal: wanting more from Spore, and still wondering what a computer world could grow if its inhabitants had more room to surprise us. We want compelling creatures and behaviors, and eventually games and shared spaces that keep changing long after their creators step away.

That means taking bigger bets: mixing environmental pressures, breeding for visible traits, and following the occasional “almost” into a new experiment. Careful accounting and replay help us understand what happened. Curiosity decides what is worth trying next.

AI opens another line of inquiry: what do adaptation, memory and collective behavior look like across different artificial systems? We’re interested in those parallels, and in AI as a future naturalist that helps us notice what we might otherwise miss.

The browser lab and volunteer compute runner exist today. A persistent shared garden, visitor-directed breeding and an AI naturalist remain directions to explore.

ABOUT / THE PROJECT

A laboratory in a browser

Browser Life is open research software for exploring which life-like patterns can arise from local rules.

PURPOSE

Why build it?

Many simulations look alive because an author tells them what an organism is. This project asks a narrower, testable question: can structures that maintain themselves, reproduce, and diversify be detected from a shared physical substrate without declaring them in advance?

The experiment combines an interactive WebGPU lab with headless runs, a CPU reference, measurements, and a coordinator for replicated histories.

FOLLOW ALONG

Source and questions

The source repository holds the rules, plans, tests, and experiment definitions. You can follow progress or raise a question through its issue tracker.

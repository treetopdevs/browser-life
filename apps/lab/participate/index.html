Optional · compute

Lend your GPU as an island

An island is a browser tab that runs assigned simulation segments and sends the results back for comparison. This is today's compute network; the persistent garden with creatures migrating between visitors is a future direction.

1

Check your browser

You need WebGPU and a secure site. A self-test checks this browser against the CPU reference before its work counts.

2

Press Join

Opening the island page starts nothing. Pressing Join asks the coordinator for work. The tab may use a lot of GPU time and power while it runs.

3

Stop whenever you like

Press Stop or close the tab. Finished work and uploaded results stay with the coordinator. The privacy page lists exactly what is sent.

Ready when you are

Open the island page

It shows its connection and what it is doing. You make the final Join choice there.

Open the island page →

Availability depends on a configured coordinator. If it is offline, joining fails without starting a simulation. Read the privacy details before joining.
